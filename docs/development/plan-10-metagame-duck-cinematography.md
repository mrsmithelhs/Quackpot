## Goal, non-goals and rationale

Make the same physical rubber duck feel like a movie character through viewpoint, lighting, apartment setting and the player's dialogue. The object itself remains inanimate. The character may reposition it between compositions; that does not authorize autonomous blinking, beak movement, breathing, wing flaps or squash/stretch. The owner favors a 2D-first approach and requires good SVG appearance at different zoom levels.
