# Plan 12: Guided opening and progressive shop investigation

**Date:** 2026-10-24. **Type:** investigation. **Mutation:** report and isolated local wireframe only. **Owner/model:** one investigator; owner selects recommendations. **Expected artifacts:** `reports/development/plan-12-guided-opening-and-shop-progression/proposal.md`, local wireframe if useful, and `progress.md`.

## Goal and authority

Plan 03 proved a short mechanism, not an effective first-time tutorial. The owner wants understanding to emerge through actions and presentation, without requiring learners to find unexplained buttons or read all rules at the start. Read `AGENTS.md`, canonical design in full, owner triage, Plan 03's accepted limits, `reports/orchestration/plan-03-owner-play-feedback.md`, and Plans 06/11. Run `check plan-12`; stop if blocked. Own only the report folder. Do not change production behavior or consume image-generation quota.

## Bounded work

**Owner direction, 2026-10-24:** Make the first transition from the apartment into the gacha app visually enticing, not merely a paragraph followed by a button. Propose a short visual/audio invitation and guided action that demonstrate the app's appeal, coordinated with Plan 14's context transition and Plan 11's compositions. Retain an accessible information path, silent/reduced-motion alternatives and immediate exit. Enticing presentation is an artistic/teaching objective, not a claim that the game has measured or caused addiction. Plan 15 separately investigates pull anticipation, acceleration and bounded responses to repeated fast interactions.

Propose one recommended opening beat map with one alternative: physical-duck motivation, entering the fictional app, the free pull, first win and following starter offer, while preserving canonical chronology. Specify what the player sees, does and learns at each beat, with the minimum necessary language and an accessible equivalent for visual/audio cues. Introduce Gems when they become relevant; introduce duplicates and Down when a duplicate actually occurs. Define an honest no-duplicate fallback rather than silently forcing the PRNG to deliver a teaching example. A pending resource may remain unobtrusive until relevant; do not hide a currency balance or price needed for a meaningful decision.

Show the shop at first offer and at a later, more complex stage with the same comparison tasks. Propose how bundles, leftovers, prices and optional explanations appear progressively. Welcoming presentation must not withhold purchase terms or turn required information into an undisclosed odds-navigation mechanism. Separate fictional persuasive shop copy from neutral help. Plan 06 still owns the optional odds-detail decision; do not decide it here. Preserve O1's display/action/impact distinction and log only cues actually shown.

## Validation and gate

Use matched storyboards/wireframes, not polished art, to explain where a first-time player could misunderstand Gems, duplicates, Down or a purchase. Include a short owner walkthrough, no-duplicate case, keyboard/reduced-motion information path and a list of later integration changes. Record questions and untested learner comprehension honestly. Return recommendations for owner approval before code. Commit owned reports, state the consultation branch, never push or set status. Production tutorial/shop work needs an approved follow-on scope.
