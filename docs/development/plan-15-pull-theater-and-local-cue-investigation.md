# Plan 15: Pull anticipation and bounded local cue investigation

**Date:** 2026-10-24. **Type:** investigation. **Mutation:** report and isolated local prototype only after prebuild approval. **Owner/model:** one investigator; owner selects recommendations. **Expected artifacts:** `reports/development/plan-15-pull-theater-and-local-cues/proposal.md`, optional comparison preview and `progress.md`.

## Goal, authority and limits

The owner wants pull presentation to build anticipation for a few seconds, shorten gracefully for players choosing efficiency, and investigate fictional persuasive interventions after repeated fast interactions. This packet authorizes proposals, not an adaptive manipulation policy or new gacha odds. Read `AGENTS.md`, canonical design in full (especially near-miss candidates, Time, event deck, Receipt and hard constraints), owner triage, Plan 03 acceptance limits, Plans 06/09/12 and `reports/orchestration/seeded-drama-and-local-interaction-direction.md`. Run `check plan-15`; stop if blocked. Own the report folder only. No production source edits, generation quota, remote libraries, telemetry or teacher-export schema.

## Phase A — anticipation and efficient control

Phase A compares free presentation controls with unchanged draw costs. The separately gated paid-convenience candidate in Phase B is an explicit alternative to investigate, not an exception silently applied to this baseline.

**Owner follow-up, 2026-10-24 — research:** Add a small, source-bounded comparison of reveal patterns in representative established gacha/loot-box/reward products. Prefer official gameplay captures, publisher demonstrations and first-hand observations of publicly accessible material; no purchases, account creation or downloading branded assets are authorized. Identify anticipation timing, sensory cues, rarity signalling, staged escalation, batch/single reveal and acceleration controls. Separate what was directly observed from interpretations about excitement or habit. Do not infer causation, addiction or commercial success from a polished sequence; if calling a sample commercially successful, cite an independent primary success metric with its date, or simply call it an observed sample. Research references may name real products in reports; Quackpot's game remains entirely fictional. Recommend transferable principles, not copied characters/assets or a claim of psychological efficacy.

Propose a small comparison: a short first-use sequence, a brisk repeat sequence, and explicit fast/reveal-all controls. Identify timing, player action, local synthesized sound, layer motion and reduced-motion/static equivalent. Consider light/shape/sound cues without assuming a particular rarity teaser or near-miss is already approved. Prefer one initial comparison rather than a full effects catalog. Propose how explicit repeated acceleration can shorten later presentation without hiding results or adding extra clicks. Return timing/control recommendations for owner approval before prototype code or Phase B adoption.

Commit the complete draw outcome, odds, inventory/Down and RNG continuation before presentation; persist the same boundary as the accepted slice. Fast/reveal-all/reduced-motion paths must yield identical outcomes and costs. Keep game time paused during reveal theater under the canonical clock. Record displayed results only after actual presentation; do not log all frames/cues as shown merely because a sequence was scheduled. Reload between commit, interrupted theater and individual reveals must be honest. Presentation randomness must not change the draw stream. Do not imply a real rarity or chance from arbitrary visual effects; any deliberately misleading near-miss candidate needs its own owner-approved eligibility/firing/copy and Receipt treatment.

## Phase B — local response candidates, with a separate owner gate

**Owner proposal, not adopted — gem-priced Fast Pull:** Compare free normal pulls/free acceleration with an explicitly disclosed gem premium for a separate convenience option. First define whether Fast Pull means shortened theater, batch processing or an automatic repeat action; these are different behaviors. Keep reduced-motion/static presentation and necessary accessibility controls free and equivalent. Compare a simple per-use premium against any alternative only if needed; no selected price, automatic charge or reactive price increase is authorized. A convenience fee is a new gem sink: show fee separately from draw cost, affordability/insufficient-funds behavior, confirmation, repeat-use totals and Receipt accounting. Check whether it changes pull counts, pity reachability, leftover balances, starvation and the short/floor pacing assumptions; route affected economy checks through a bounded simulation amendment before implementation. Do not manufacture excessive mandatory delay solely to sell relief. Any adopted premium must preserve genuine draw odds/outcomes and be owner-approved with its teaching rationale. Prefer clearly differentiated labels for cosmetic acceleration versus an actual paid draw action.

Investigate whether a fictional intervention after repeated explicit fast interactions teaches a recognizable technique. Compare no intervention with at most one or two bounded cues, such as a dismissible publisher message or collection-progress nudge that pauses between completed sequences. These are hypotheses to review, not selected techniques or mechanics. Do not silently grant value, impose a price change, fabricate scarcity/social proof, or modify odds as a consequence of using fast controls. New economic effects require a separately approved rule.

Define trigger inputs in terms of observed actions (for example consecutive uses of fast/reveal-all), not inferred motives such as speedrunning, boredom or susceptibility. Prefer explicit control usage over raw click cadence; input devices, accessibility preferences and interruptions make timing ambiguous. If timing is proposed, compare false positives under keyboard/reduced-motion use, slow rendering, reload, interruption and intentional repeated clicks. Specify a bounded local window/reset rule, event eligibility and frequency cap, dismissal and neutral fallback. No continuous behavioral profile, cross-run fingerprint, network calls or hidden data transmission. Export/analyzer fields remain Plan 07's gate.

The fictional app need not announce its decision rule in the moment. Its eventual Receipt must explain the intervention and the actual triggering action pattern in the three registers: **We displayed / You did / Did this affect you?** Example proposal: a cue was shown after three recorded fast-reveal uses; do not write that the player was rushing or that the cue slowed them down. Log eligibility, rule version, measured inputs, cue actually shown, dismissal/action and any approved result. A skipped/cancelled cue is not shown. This is local educational simulation, not a claim that the classifier knows intent.

Keep fast controls stable and effective. Do not sabotage keyboard input, move a control under a click, force longer theater in response to acceleration, repeat interventions endlessly or make exit harder. Keep Uninstall reachable and show no retention after the Receipt. Reduced motion preserves information and agency; do not use it as evidence of a motive or target for extra intervention.

## Validation, delivery and stop

Return matched traces for first-use, repeated ordinary pulls, explicit fast use, reduced motion and a false-positive case. Show the exact log-to-Receipt wording and distinguish neutral delight from named manipulation. Cite primary sources for technique names, while labeling fictional candidate designs as proposals rather than empirically effective interventions. A local prototype, if approved, compares one cue and a no-cue control; no production integration or efficacy claim follows from it. Commit scoped artifacts/report with consultation declaration, never push or set status. Owner selection and a bounded implementation work order follow investigation.
