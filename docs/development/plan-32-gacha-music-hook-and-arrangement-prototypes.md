This packet proposes/composes source motifs; Plan33 independently researches transformation methods. Neither adopts a production audio library or narrative trigger schedule.

Stop for root mechanism and owner listening-brief approval BEFORE generating tracks/installing libraries/launching rendering jobs. Preserve melody/rhythm anchors and contrasting sections; allow peaceful-to-enticing identity in base gacha mood without requiring constant high intensity. Candidate alternate arrangements may keep motif while varying harmony, register, texture, pulse, bass/drone, articulation or unresolved phrases. No claim minor means distress for every listener. Compare matched volume/tempo where possible so louder/faster isn't mistaken for composition quality.

Return source/provenance and proposed listening matrix, practical looping/crossfade/headroom/CPU/memory considerations, a few named hooks the owner can recognize, and rights/credits plan.
