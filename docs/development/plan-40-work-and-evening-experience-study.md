## Goal and why this exists

Production grants wages and jumps from the tutorial pull/race to another morning without letting the player understand work or evening life. The immediate night-shift button also combines accepting, working, health cost, income and paying rent. Make those experiences understandable and meaningful without turning work into filler or a reflex minigame. Pair a modest ordinary-work representation and optional extra-shift treatment with a first-evening activity candidate such as calling Mom.
