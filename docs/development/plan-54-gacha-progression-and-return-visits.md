## Goal and why this packet exists

Establish what a player hopes to accomplish inside Quackpot across successive visits, beyond repeating pulls and races. Apartment and life systems have advanced substantially; the gacha needs a coherent progression direction to make its leisure opportunities meaningful. Compare three distinct organizing models before adding currencies, activities or more production UI. An attractive preview is not evidence of a viable economy, replayable implementation or compelling student experience.

The owner approves this investigation's direction and Sonnet consultancy. This packet's draft status does not dispatch an implementer or authorize production changes. Plan11 remains the visual-composition study; Plan15 remains the pull-anticipation/local-cue study. Plan54 informs their priorities without absorbing their effects work or reopening their separate gates.

## Work plan and authority to proceed

### 1. Map the actual baseline and propose the comparison

Write a compact current-state map with source pins: implemented, owner-selected but pending, historical/superseded, and unresolved. Include current number of identities, existing session opportunities and available shop/collection/race actions; verify rather than assume. Identify contradictions in historical design prose for root reconciliation, not unilateral rewriting.

Propose three coherent models: **collection-led**, **competition-led**, and **event-led**. Explain each player's goal, next-visit hook, ordinary nonspending path, spending temptation, content/asset burden, and reuse of existing systems. Models may later combine, but must differ enough to compare. Include an option that relies primarily on existing mechanics rather than more chores or permanent rewards.

Publish a compact hypothesis table with a falsifying observation for each claim. For example, collection-led progression loses its roster-depth justification if a single best duck remains the obvious choice across its proposed situations; event-led progression loses its low-burden claim if understanding a visit requires tracking several independent clocks. Compare matched scenarios where the models predict different choices. Owner preference is evidence about preference, not proof that rival models are universally false.

Return the mechanism, scenario matrix, exact report-local files and baseline/preserved/context/placeholder/deferred table to the owner in the implementer chat. Obtain approval before prototype construction. This is one coherent prebuild gate, not permission per screen or command. Bring root in early only for a material conflict with selected rules, unavailable consultancy, changed production scope or an important dependency.

### 3. Build the approved report-local comparison and interview

Show a small set of matched early, return and later visits for each model, including limited cash and competing life commitments. Show what remains interesting without buying Gems. Distinguish cosmetic objectives, mechanical advantages and reward-bearing goals.

Use actual accepted mechanics where demonstrated; clearly mark proposed banners/identities/objectives/rewards and illustrative balances. Price/count illustrations are examples pending calibration, not newly ratified values.

Keep reviewer scenario switches, annotations and state outside student-facing compositions. Supply plain-language summaries and accessible keyboard/static versions. Review only the changed experience; no forced whole-game replay. Preserve source-bound context and explain what a future integration must not copy, especially placeholder HUD, shop, race, transition, Receipt and scheduling surfaces.

After prebuild approval, proceed autonomously with owner interviews, contextual choices, copy revisions, small report-local experiments and appropriate finite checks through delivery.
