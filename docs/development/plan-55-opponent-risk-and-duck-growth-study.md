## Goal and why this exists

Determine whether reading a recurring fictional field can change a reasonable pre-race choice, and whether a favorite duck can reach one understandable development decision within an ordinary run. Plan54 established an appealing experience direction, not a tested mechanism. Avoid building a full progression economy around forecasts or abilities that make no useful difference.

## Work plan

1. Map current mechanisms and recurring-opponent gaps. Distinguish authored fictional profiles, forecasts, actual choices and recorded race outcomes. Explain what would make two fields favor different choices; don't claim strategy from decorative labels.
2. Propose the smallest comparison: accepted current-race control; recurring-opponent/forecast arm retaining two current pace choices; richer risk arm with one legible growth/ability choice and explicit no-injury alternative. Specify only enough candidate mechanics to test the hypotheses. Keep field selection, in-race assistance and result manipulation distinct; don't bundle adaptive leaderboards now.
3. Bring the owner a compact concrete decision trace and relevant ramifications. Preserve permanent banked-point/no-slot preference, progress for every completed race and raceable-injury direction as investigation inputs. Assess growth budgets, injury/rest stacking and recoverability; don't assume a geometric curve solves convergence. Stop for candidate/protocol clearance before execution.
4. After clearance, run a predeclared finite paired-seed protocol using identical initial rosters/conditions across arms, no seed filtering. Include small/common rosters, tired/injured and no-injury, losses, ordinary nonspending and limited-cash routes. Define a short represented horizon plus an ordinary-run opportunity budget, reachable first choice, meaningful stat advantage and power-feedback measures. Use simple decision policies and regret/best-response comparisons where useful; simulation is not evidence of human engagement.
5. Show only a small representative contrast in a report-local owner preview, with context/placeholder boundaries explicit. Separate visual usefulness, mechanical difference, uncertainty and chosen owner experience. Record failed hypotheses and cost to correct.
6. Deliver recommendation and exact later integration boundary. Leaderboards/event calibration follows concrete core candidates; optional cosmetics/achievements and profile exports remain separate.
