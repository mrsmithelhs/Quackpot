## Implementation requirements

### 1. Growth and first assignment

Each completed race awards the entered owned duck +2 progress, plus +1 for a top-three finish. The first point requires 4 progress. Preserve earned overflow; do not invent later redemption thresholds or silently discard progress. Bank the point until the player permanently chooses Bounce or independent crowd acceleration. Assignment consumes no life slot and needs clear irreversible confirmation. Losing, one-duck and non-spending routes must work. Report later thresholds/caps as deferred, not a repeated cost of four by assumption.

### 2. Injury and recovery transactions

An actual injury reduces race rating by 10% during the same race and remains relevant to later races. Compose injury, existing rest and the Down upgrade before the accepted affine rating conversion; a rating reduction is not a direct world-speed multiplier. Injured ducks remain selectable. Recovery requires two actual in-game day changes. A completed race entered already injured has an independently seeded 25% chance to add one recovery day, disclosed before commitment. Newly injured entrants that began healthy do not qualify; unresolved attempts award neither growth nor extension.

Commit validated retained injuries from an unresolved attempt independently of presentation, equally for watched, skipped and reduced-motion paths. Discarded/rolled-back contacts must not injure ducks. Show an accessible no-result explanation without inventing a finish or reward. Serialize injury, reward, growth and assignment so reload/replay cannot duplicate them. Advance recovery once per actual day change, including early-bed/bedrest routes, never by real elapsed time or repeated rendering.

### 3. Bounce, crowd acceleration and contact semantics

Port the reconciled multi-contact candidate deliberately. Bounce may activate during weaving or collision recovery without erasing existing impulse. Its duck receives full injury protection during the active window, including landing; it does not heal an existing injury. Landing onto an occupied duck is a genuine collision with independent risk for the other participant. Distinguish closing impulse from genuine non-closing contact; reconcile the same physical boundary once, and preserve separation/rearm and rollback rules. Never force a crossing to repair an unsafe physical state.

Crowd acceleration is independent of Draft and uses actual eligible nearby participants. Pin versioned opportunity, activation, effect and termination evidence separately from graphics. Root must explicitly adopt initial injury odds, risk differences, prediction cadence, durations, cooldowns and crowd assistance constants in the proposal. Plan55 diagnostic values are candidates, not implicit owner-approved balance. The small paired screen did not establish equal ability strength or final occurrence rates.

### 4. Reusable selected visuals and truthful binding

Extract Plan57's selected angular pop/scattered fragments/earlier burst A treatments, one-second participant white return and actual-injury bandage. Define deterministic variant selection and log/replay mapping; no presentation random draw may change mechanics. Injury markers attach only to genuinely injured ducks.

Extract all eleven Plan58 selected treatments into focused reusable production presentation modules: Bounce, crowd acceleration, Guard, Quick Recovery, Dive, Wide Flap, Flap Ahead, Draft, Straight Shooter, Steady Nerves and Wake Break. Bind only the first two to live ability events. The other nine remain unmounted exports, with contract fixtures; do not expose a misleading unlocked catalog or claim their graphics implement effects. Burst remains deferred.

Use real duck rig coordinates, heading and layer ownership, unique instance IDs, named source/target participants and explicit start/end/cancel. Preserve upright labels, facing, camera zoom, readable drama glow/dimming, individual finish/coast and accepted podium layout/loops/player emphasis. Test finish cancellation and teardown explicitly: the study galleries did not establish these. Add truthful enlarged explanations to selection/Duck-Dex for implemented abilities, with static/text equivalents. Cosmetic state never resolves results or writes rewards.
