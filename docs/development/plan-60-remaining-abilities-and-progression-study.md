## Goal and why this packet exists

Give the remaining visually explored abilities useful, distinct mechanical identities and explain how ducks could develop beyond their first point. A compelling animation does not settle triggers, benefits, trade-offs or upgrades. The study should recommend a small next integration group, plus explicit deferrals or mergers where appropriate, rather than require every concept to survive.

## Work plan and gates

### 1. Compact study mechanism and efficient creative consultation

Propose exact sources, read-only production imports if needed, report-local experiment seams, experiment bounds and consultant transport before executable work/provider calls. Root clears this once. After clearance, proceed through routine research, owner dialogue, implementation and repairs to delivery without one approval per edit or test. Stop at genuine owner choices, shared-contract changes or resources outside the envelope.

Use Sonnet as a context-rich creative/experience consultant: batch the whole ability family and progression problem, including current race/rig references, selected visuals, player motivations, free/one-duck paths and known failure cases. Ask for differentiated purposes, counterexamples, concise player explanations and combinations. Reuse the conversation for useful focused revisions rather than nine cold calls. Haiku is preferable for any small bounded drawings or reference tasks; do not commission a new art catalog by default. Codex owns integration and verification. Preserve teacher subscription efficiency without imposing a fixed subagent-count quota.

Root reviews actual transport, requested model, permissions, input delivery and bounded time/memory/output before calls. Read-only instructions are not enforcement; disclose the distinction. Record observable identity, useful outputs, failed calls and privacy-safe evidence. Provider opinions are hypotheses, not empirical findings. If unavailable, report the limitation rather than silently substitute.

### 2. Define coherent options with the owner

For each ability specify player purpose, trigger/opportunity, automatic seeded activation, eligible source/target, magnitude/duration, cooldown/rearm, failure/termination, drawback and interaction with speed caps, steering, contact, injury and finishing. Address air/ground/submerged/landing semantics where relevant. Distinguish the concept from Bounce/crowd and neighboring abilities; Guard should be evaluated for a healthy favorite duck as well as an already injured one, not dismissed through a narrow tired-duck scenario.

Group contextual owner choices by meaningful families: contact/protection/recovery; movement/positioning; leader/follower/wake interactions. Show concrete contrasting race situations and ramifications before asking a preference. Do not ask the owner to choose unexplained numeric constants or nine isolated names. Owner selections may occur directly in the implementer chat. Summarize major settled boundaries for root review, with provisional recommendations distinguished from owner decisions; avoid a second competing ledger.

### 3. Progression that changes useful choices

Compare later point thresholds and uses, ability investment versus learning a second ability, caps/slots, and possible rarity starting advantages. Show favorite/common-duck, rotating-roster, one-duck, loss and non-spending traces. Explain what an additional point changes in a race, whether choices remain distinct and whether power growth overwhelms base stats or makes older ducks worthless. Preserve earned progress and irreversible-choice clarity. Prices, repeated threshold curves and rarity grants remain unselected until explicitly reviewed.

Future Gem acceleration and limited-time events are contextual follow-ups, not prerequisites: Plan55 proposals do not establish later redemption thresholds or a settled monetization schedule. Do not use paid recovery or a free-path lockout to make progression attractive.

### 4. Falsifiable comparisons before broad tuning

Write a hypothesis table before running experiments: rival claims, observations that would falsify each, and an experiment where live rivals predict different results. Decouple trigger frequency, effect strength, current injury and chosen risk so they do not accidentally stand in for one another. Predeclare controls, finite seeds/cases, metrics and rejection criteria. A failed or redundant ability is a useful result; do not retune until every candidate wins.

Use authored fixtures for exact semantics and separate paired natural runs for opportunity/exposure and outcomes. Include no-ability controls, healthy and injured entrants, steady/risky stances, relevant rarity/rating gaps, individual and strategically chosen combined abilities. Distinguish artificial opportunities from naturally occurring ones; report samples, exposure counts and uncertainty. Do not claim a winner while rivals remain consistent with observations, or infer equal strength from a tiny sample. Exhaustive all-ability combinations and whole-game economic balance are not required to recommend a bounded next slice.

Build a report-local explanatory comparison only where it helps the owner understand a real decision. Pin real imports; no copied full production shell or gallery mock presented as integrated gameplay. Follow existing bounded launcher/resource policy with exact finite commands, fresh preflight and default 90-second/1-GiB heap/2-GiB process-tree envelope. Coordinate captures and shared hardware; never stop owner servers, weaken process cleanup or run unbounded simulations.

### 5. Deliver prioritized contracts, not a new monolith

Recommend which abilities to implement next, which to defer/merge, and what progression stage is ready. For each selected candidate provide a compact versionable input/event/state contract, concise player description, selected art mapping, unresolved trade-offs and targeted acceptance examples. State whether it fits Plan59's interfaces or needs a reviewed amendment after its stable handoff. Do not change Plan59 rules underneath an active port.

Declare advisor posture under repository guidance. Root may authorize one Terra-medium read-only review for substantive experiment code/contracts before delivery; no automatic repeated review or advisor call for prose alone. Preserve unsuccessful investigations and original evidence.
