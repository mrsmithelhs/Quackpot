## Goal and why this packet exists

Make noticing, choosing, reading and responding to metagame messages feel like part of an existing life. Existing portrait conversations provide a starting grammar, but work/Ren/loan communications currently compete with the desk phone's expected Quackpot action. Explore the surrounding Messages experience before introducing production message identities, read state or save/replay changes. Preserve immersive fictional language and distinguish being notified, reading a message and making a consequential choice.

## Selected experience direction

The owner accepts these directions, with exact visual treatments still to compare:

- Global header Messages entry; available desk phone reserved for Quackpot. Before launch, preserve the selected invitation/reply/ack flow without invented install/reward behavior.
- Badge counts unread **threads**, not individual bubbles. Latest delivered message orders threads most-recent-first; unread does not reorder them. Reading marks the delivered revision read only after content actually appears, not upon inbox click or failed loading.
- Portrait phone overlay with list, avatars, sender, preview and readable time reference; back arrow from a thread to the list. Reading and unresolved action/due status remain separate.
- Owner's early gallery selection is overlapping bubbles and a stronger but bounded arrival cue. Consequential actions such as repayment use a separate game decision outside the phone, not an inline Decide panel or simulated text-suggestion system (DECISION-089). Phone content remains sender-authored communication. The exact transition affordance is still a gallery choice; opening/reading never pays, replies or accepts. Informational/resolved content does not retain redundant decision controls.
- Ordinary unread, actual time-sensitive obligations and sender-created promotional urgency have distinct meanings. Use a badge plus restrained arrival cue; stronger attention is bounded, then settles to a static marker. A loan offer's pressure is not falsely labelled an objective repayment deadline. Static/text/reduced-motion equivalents preserve information.
- Backdrop click, Escape and a visible close control dismiss without answering. If a payment confirmation is open, dismissal cancels it without payment. Restore focus/context. Uninstall remains immediately reachable.
- Authored older threads establish a life before the run: small fixed read-only exchanges rather than clickable dead ends saying they are uninteresting. They start read, contain no hidden reward/decision, and never pulse as new delivery. They are fictional bundled background, not generated during play.

### 3. Thread choosing, conversation and decision grammar

Build one coherent end-to-end prototype: header -> recent-first thread list -> portrait conversation -> optional Decide panel -> back/dismiss. Use delivered revision identities in mock data; a new bubble/revision may make an existing thread unread, but simply changing an unpaid balance is not automatically a delivered message. Preserve read threads for review. Expired/canceled messages must not imply an available action or pulse indefinitely.

Cover work/Marisol, Ren, property office, payday lender and Quackpot invitation/ack references without inventing binding new story beats. Demonstrate a small curated set of historical fictional threads (roughly six to ten is an initial content target, not a quota) including ordinary family, work and mundane-life exchanges. Old timestamps/previews must not expose future story spoilers, contradict relationship history or masquerade as real run events. Do not assign new genders/biographies/relationships or Ren traits beyond accepted material without owner review.

Back navigation and backdrop dismissal must work with a clearly separate outside-phone game decision on desktop and an appropriate narrow presentation. Do not embed decision controls or game-state commentary inside conversation content. Define the transition to that decision and cancellation back to the read thread without payment, new unread delivery or lost due status. No nested redundant decision layer. Content failing to mount leaves unread unchanged. Keyboard focus enters/restores predictably, Escape behavior is explicit, unread status is announced politely, and phone controls remain usable under text enlargement. Respect the distinction between a screen-reader-friendly implementation and an actually observed AT test.
