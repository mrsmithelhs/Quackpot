Remove “or” alternatives from the executed route. Avoid making one cheap purchase an automatic punishment while the other branch is also insolvent. Recommend a bounded trade-off with visible benefits/costs and viable recovery, and show the no-purchase, purchase, recovery and Uninstall traces. State a designed final event/day for continued play. Distinguish a test seed yielding that result from a guarantee imposed on every learner run, and record actual conditional odds/direct grants honestly. The guaranteed Rare in the starter pack is also a new proposed benefit requiring owner approval. Finite identities and duplicate-to-Down conversion must not turn nine duplicate draws into nine independently owned racing identities. Treat “50 + 80 leaves 130” as one trace only; log actual balances and repeat retention-offer behavior without asserting that all players see that leftover. Retention acceptance alone is not a bundle-shop display.

The report also alternates a 9.8 field and an 11.2 field, which changes whether that Common can win. Specify outcome calculation, randomness (if any), visible field information and consecutive-race consequences. Compare at least two choice sequences under stated visible conditions, or keep duck fatigue out of this first slice and propose another meaningful decision for owner review.
