## Controlling conditions

1. **Motion-ready hierarchy without animation scope.** Put the crown/hat attachment under the head group, so a later head transform carries the hat. Attach the monocle to the head/eye; attach neckwear and medals deliberately to the body/neck/chest as appropriate. State local coordinates and parent-relative pivots; keep wing sublayers under a common wing pivot. Define accessory front/behind slots instead of placing every item at the root. No race/reveal animation is required now. A static transform diagnostic may prove attachment but does not select a motion.
2. **Multiple instances must be independent.** Accept a safe per-instance ID prefix or equivalent composition mechanism. Namespace all IDs, filter/clip references, href/use and accessibility label references. Verify differently colored/shadowed ducks together in one DOM, not just one SVG in isolation. Keep cosmetics independent of finite identity and stats. A pure markup composer must validate supported option values rather than interpolating arbitrary strings as markup.
3. **Keep the proof small.** Author one base, no more than three representative palettes, two hats and two accessories (include one head-attached and one body/neck-attached item). Show selected useful combinations, not every permutation or a complete catalog. Compare flat offset and filtered shadows at 32/64/128/256px against light/dark/pond backgrounds. Claims such as crisp at 32px or higher filter cost remain hypotheses until observed/measured. Produce exactly one short sound effect, recommended `QUACK_SHORT`, with one documented text-parameter contract, conservative gain, user-gesture start/resume and silent fallback. Do not build a general multi-effect audio engine, three effects or music. Text-parameter Web Audio in the proposed jsfxr/ZzFX style is acceptable for this small proof; no runtime dependencies or audio downloads.

Alternatively serve it locally and provide one tested command and URL. Choose and document the approach before building. Reuse the source composer/synth implementation; do not hand-maintain a second preview implementation. Generated inline preview assets may be derived locally from source. No CDN or external asset requests.
