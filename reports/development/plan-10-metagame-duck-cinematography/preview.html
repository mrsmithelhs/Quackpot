Cameras and scale. Every area uses ONE camera for all its objects. Rooms (living, kitchen, bedroom): one horizontal pinhole camera per room; every object is drawn as a front elevation at its true depth, so the fridge, couch, TV, toy shelf and the duck keep consistent proportions and perspective (camera height is set per room so the duck's surface lands in the lower third; tabletop and shelf tops are drawn as receding surfaces). Desk side view: eye-level across the desk. Desk top-down: camera straight above; every object is a footprint, with shadows scaled to object height and, under the warm lamp, pointing away from it. The duck uses its front/side/rear view in every non-overhead scene (High and Low need other cameras and fall back to Side); the overhead duck is an extra drawing, not one of the five views. Phone proportions follow the game's desk phone (9:17, 9:41 status row, QUACKPOT invitation text). Real-world sizes are assumed (the repo specifies none): duck about 64 mm tall, so it is small in wide room shots; use Medium framing. The living-room “Couch and TV sold” state previews the design's visible emptying.
