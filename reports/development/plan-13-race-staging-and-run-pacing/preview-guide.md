# Plan 13 combined-race preview guide

The isolated [preview](./preview.html) adds one selectable interaction arm: **Combined · rank-weighted weave + seeded 15–20° contact**. It uses the same controller, course, speed model, event/RNG identities, and guard limits as the two existing constituent arms. The arm adds no speed penalty and keeps the one-contact-per-race limit.

## Reproduce the named interaction witness

1. Open `preview.html` in the locally served report folder.
2. Set **Experiment** to **Combined · rank-weighted weave + seeded 15–20° contact**.
3. Keep the defaults: seed `Quackpot Plan 13 named-seed example`, race ID `R03`, player/field base speeds `11.7` / `11.5`, **Steady**, and no trial.
4. Choose **Start race** or **Skip to finish**, then expand **Show race narrative**. Select the contact event and check the per-duck acquired heading, held-turn and recenter statements. The finish summary reveals the committed order and payout.

The paired headless evidence uses 20 matched seed/race pairs against **Rank-weighted weave · recenter controller** and **Contact candidate · seeded 15–20° response**. It records winner/order changes, finish-time deltas, trajectory changes, shared performance and weave phase keys, participant contact-angle keys, recovery completion, and unresolved guard outcomes. Differences are observations of the combined interaction; they do not establish that either individual treatment caused an additive share of an outcome.

Changing the experiment or race inputs resolves a new committed race. Camera, lighting, reduced motion, event seeking, playback slowdown, replay and skip reuse that committed outcome. This preview is a report artifact, not production gameplay. The headless scan cannot determine whether labels visually overlap or whether the starting rows appear balanced; those remain visual review findings, with no label offsets or physics values changed for this comparison.
