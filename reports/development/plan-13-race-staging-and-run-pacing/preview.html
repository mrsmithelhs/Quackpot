Committed race inputs

Recomputes the outcome only when a race input or experiment changes. Camera, highlight, reduced motion, skip, replay, and snapshot restore reuse the committed trajectory and do not resolve a second time.

Six ducks start in two lanes and three rows. Each race ends at the earliest interpolated forward crossing of the actual finish line, or is marked unresolved at 40 seconds / 800 ticks with no winner or payout. Followers are classified at the winner's crossing time. The 0, −4, and −8 m rows travel different distances to the line. Mechanical event and weave-opportunity records are kept separately.
