    Day-pacing observation cards

    Example maps from the proposed 900-second budget. These are scripted observation aids, not a timed whole-run claim. The sample contains no mandatory idle waits.

    Ordinary day · 55 sec

    1. Balances and expense cue · 8 sec · running flow
    2. Familiar companion line · 5 sec · paused
    3. Read offer and choose spend/save · 12 sec · live labeled window, 1 decision
    4. Pull/decline and skippable reveal · 15 sec · reveal pauses
    5. Settlement and transition · 15 sec · pause as needed

    New rule time: 0–5 sec if needed. Repeated waits: 0.

    Consequential day · 95 sec

    1. Balances and upcoming bill · 8 sec · running flow
    2. Explain Drafting unlock · 12 sec · paused, player-paced
    3. Shop window · 20 sec · live labeled window, 1 decision
    4. Choose one race entry · 10 sec · 1 decision, no reflex deadline
    5. Fixed race · 20 sec · active presentation
    6. Result/payout/companion beat · 10 sec · paused
    7. Settlement/transition · 15 sec · pause for consequential choice

    Comprehension: at least the 12-sec introduction; may extend. Repeated waits: 0.

    Illustrative run split: setup 90 sec + 7 ordinary days (385 sec) + 3 consequential days (285 sec, including three 20-sec races) + Receipt/ending 120 sec + 20-sec non-idle orientation allowance = 900 sec. Longer player-paced scenes can extend the run; this has not been measured with students.
