## C. Rejected or flagged items (summary)

- Kenney duplicates (rows 4, 26; casino pack): excluded per the owner's rule. The Kenney.nl researcher covers these.
- UI Sound Effects Library (row 16): CC-BY 3.0, but its provenance is disputed in the page comments. Excluded until verified.
- Machine shutting down (row 12): CC0 on its page, but it is a derivative of a freesound recording whose license was not checked. Kept as T3-026 and flagged.
- Click UI Menu SFX (row 1): CC0 on its page, but it is a sped-up copy of SuperTuxKart sounds whose license was not checked. Kept as T3-005 and flagged needs-compat-check.
- Robin Lamb UI set (row 15): the uploader says the sounds are from VCSL and VSCO 2 CE. That is the uploader's statement and was not verified.
- Entries with no license shown (rows 17, 18): excluded.
- Completion sound (OGA-BY 3.0 · CC0) and the Wierd Whooshes pack (CC-BY-SA and GPL in the license field): excluded, with the compat notes above.
- Quiz Zukan (row 8): entry dated today with 0 downloads. Kept, but the zip contents need verifying before any audition.

## D. Restrictions summary

- CC-BY 3.0 (attribution required): T3-002, T3-003, T3-004 (KillaMaaki); T3-025 (jalastram).
- CC-BY 4.0 (attribution with a required credit line): T3-001, T3-019, T3-020, T3-021, T3-028, T3-029 (Spaceyyy).
- CC0 (no attribution required; credit optional): all other candidates.
- Login, paywall or access restrictions: none encountered on any OGA page visited. No downloads were attempted.
- Flags: needs-compat-check (T3-005); derivative-source-unverified (T3-026).
