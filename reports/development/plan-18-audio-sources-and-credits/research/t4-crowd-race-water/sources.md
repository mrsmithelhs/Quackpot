# T4 Sources: crowd reactions, race cues, water and contact impacts

Date of research: 2026-10-28. Scope: search and record only. No audio was downloaded, and nothing was heard. All judgments of fit come from titles, descriptions, listed durations and sizes.

Method notes:
- A license is recorded only from that file's or item's own page. Library-wide claims were not used as a file's license unless the item page states it.
- `name_based_only: true` in candidates.json means the fit was judged from a file name or track title alone, with no page description.
- Quotes are short and verbatim. Pages are not reproduced.
- Freesound downloads need login. Those items are listed as `unverified-lead` with `login_required` in flags.
- Every page below was reached. No site was unreachable. Search-snippet-only items are marked as such.

## 2. Restrictions and license notes

- CC0 and public domain items need no credit. Credit is still recommended where a page asks for it.
- CC-BY items (T4-001, T4-004, T4-014) need attribution. The suggested attribution is in candidates.json.
- Dual-licensed item T4-007 (OGA-BY 3.0 or CC0): the owner must elect the CC0 option and record that choice.
- Ambiguous item T4-011 (race start countdown): unresolved badges, including GPL and CC-BY-SA. Do not use until resolved.
- Freesound items T4-025 and T4-026: login required to download. License shown on page but not verified at file level.
- Wikimedia public-domain items (T4-002, T4-010): the license box links to the Wikipedia public-domain article. The file-page release sentence is the operative statement.
- Google Actions Sound Library: sound files are governed by the Actions on Google Terms of Service, "intended solely for use by you in Actions that you create", and "Distributing sound files separately... is not permitted." The CC BY 4.0 license on that site covers the documentation text, not the sounds. Not licensed for a standalone browser game catalog. See section 3.
- Pixabay: "You cannot sell or distribute Content" in standalone form. Custom license, rejected.
- Mixkit: the license page names "Sound Effects Free License" but states no terms. Rejected as unverifiable.
- SFXMint: site states "Every sound is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0", but the footer says the sounds are "AI-generated or procedurally synthesized". Publisher is not identified. Rejected for the recorded-catalog requirement.

## 4. Role coverage and gaps

Coverage in candidates.json (26 candidates; 23 `file-page`, 3 `unverified-lead`):
- CROWD (11): cheer (T4-001, T4-026 lead), applause (T4-002, T4-003, T4-004, T4-021, T4-022, T4-023, T4-025 lead), race-track crowd (T4-024), clapping win (T4-005).
- RACE (5): start whistle (T4-010 soft), start beep/countdown (T4-011, unresolved), start/finish bells (T4-008, T4-009), race-track crowd (T4-024).
- WIN (8): win stings and bells (T4-005, T4-006, T4-007, T4-008, T4-009, T4-016), applause finish (T4-002, T4-004).
- IMPACT (9): splash (T4-012, T4-013, T4-014), plop/drop (T4-015, T4-016), squeak (T4-017, T4-018), synthetic bounce/recovery (T4-019), thud/bump fallback (T4-020).

Gaps (no acceptable CC0/CC-BY recorded candidate found):
- Gasp, "aww"/disappointment crowd reactions. Searched OGA and Commons. Only non-CC0 or ungrounded results (Myinstants, AudioJungle).
- Murmur swell: partial only (T4-001 chatter tracks; T4-024). No dedicated CC0 murmur file found.
- Podium sting: no CC0 podium sting found. T4-006 is the closest generic win chime. Victory fanfares on OGA are music and rejected.
- Toy-like race horn: none found on the four listed sources. The BigSoundBank toy horn is off-list and was not opened.
- Soft rubber bump or thud: T4-020 is described as harsh. No soft rubber bump found.
- Soft rubber squeak-bounce: T4-018 (squeak toy) and T4-017 (mechanical click) are the closest; neither is verified by listening.

## 5. Coverage limits

- No audio was heard. Durations come from listed metadata where shown. Estimates from file size are labelled as estimates in the notes.
- Commons category and OGA collection pages were read only for listings. Per-file licenses for files not opened were not checked.
- Internet Archive Red Library tracks were judged from the item listing, not from per-track pages.
- Freesound files were not downloaded (login required). Their details come from the public listing page only.
