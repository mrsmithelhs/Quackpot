# T5 sources: META (phone, paper, coins, apartment) and optional AMB

Research date: 2026-10-28. Search and read only. No audio was downloaded, no login was used, and nothing was heard. Judgments on sound come from titles, descriptions, file metadata and durations.

## Method and license levels

- file-page: the entry's own page states the license for one to two sound files.
- pack-level: a multi-file collection (zip, 7z or a larger set) whose page states one license for all files. Per-file licenses were not checked individually.
- unverified-lead: a Freesound page, where the license is shown but download requires login. Recorded as a lead only.
- Accepted: CC0 1.0, explicit public-domain dedication, CC BY (exact version). Rejected: SA, NC, ND, royalty-free and other custom licenses.
- Page-level license text was read with a summarizing fetch. Where a page showed only the CC0 badge, the evidence is recorded as the license field, not as a full sentence.

## Restrictions and flags

- login-required: T5-005 and T5-030 (Freesound). Downloads were not attempted.
- preview-only: T5-005 and T5-030 (view-only in this research).
- attribution required (CC BY 3.0): T5-028 (DoKashiteru, Rain and Thunder Loop).
- weather-not-approved: all AMB candidates (T5-024 to T5-029) and rain-containing items T5-004 and T5-025. Owner has not approved weather audio for production.
- thunder-flag: T5-028 and T5-029. Thunder or rumble is flag-only. T5-028 is a thunderstorm recording. T5-029 is a generic rumble, not thunder.
- Large downloads: T5-009 (142 MB), T5-004 (162.8 MB item), T5-025 (FLAC 75.5 MB, OGG 19 MB), T5-019 and T5-020 (7z archives, extraction needed).
