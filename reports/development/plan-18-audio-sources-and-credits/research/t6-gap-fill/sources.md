## 4. Restrictions and caveats

- SoundBible's "Attribution 3.0" label does not name Creative Commons or link a licence. Its CC identity and version are inferred, so all SoundBible CC-style candidates carry the flag CC-identity-unconfirmed. The owner should confirm.
- SoundBible "Public Domain" (T6-005) has no licence URL on the page.
- Commons PD-USGov-FWS (T6-007) is a US-specific template. Its scope outside the US is not confirmed.
- BigSoundBank: per-sound label says CC0. Site licence text says "CC0-like". Owner should confirm.
- Freesound Library pages (CC BY 4.0) do not name the creator. Attribution text uses the site name.
- MixClap items (T6-018, T6-019) are unverified-lead. Reseller. The underlying Freesound licence was not checked.
- OGA "Squeak toy sounds" (T6-014) is a pack-level CC0 zip with unknown contents.
- For the down cue, the only accepted SoundBible item is Sad Trombone (T6-017). It is probably over 2 s and comedic. No sub-2 s soft descending cue was found.
- Riser candidates are whoosh accents (T6-015, T6-016, T6-018) plus one 2.0 s reverse swell (T6-019). No clean CC0 riser was confirmed.
