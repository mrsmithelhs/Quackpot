# Plan 23 — position-conditioned movement assistance, R2 proposal

**Status:** root-approved report-only research candidate (not adopted mechanics). Root authorized the isolated four-arm headless screen and the owned study-file set. Browser preview and production/canonical adoption remain separately gated.

## Why another candidate

Drama study 01's acceleration-only assist executed in only 66 of 25,962 scheduled checks in the rear-only arm and 82 of 25,992 in the combined arm. The restrictive intersection of a 3 m gap, 0.25 top-speed headroom, 15 m beyond braking distance, and no active steering/recovery blocked most checks. The resulting paired race outcomes were unchanged. Do not infer effective catch-up from the event count.

The larger 16–20°/15-tick leader weave increased sustained lead changes only from 1.50 to 1.73 per race and increased wall-impact/overlap records from 36 to 322 across 432 races. The player's mean place worsened by 0.343 and win rate fell from 62.7% to 49.3%. This is a warning against making the selected weave angle larger as the default drama lever. It remains an experimental comparison, not a production selection.

## Candidate A: bounded backmarker acceleration plus lane recenter help

Keep the affine candidate, front-row fixture, accepted weave schedule/keys, 15–20° contact, standings, finish rule, and all trial/rest/stance inputs identical to the matched baseline. This is one position-based rule for every entrant, including the player if they actually meet the backmarker condition.

- **Opportunity:** every 40 ticks, a keyed 0.5 draw is considered only for rank 4–6 entrants at least 2 m behind their immediate predecessor. Keep a frozen tick-start rank/gap snapshot and a separate keyed namespace. Use an 80-tick cooldown and at most two assist windows per entrant per race. Do not force a pass or target an identity.
- **Acceleration channel:** for up to 40 ticks (2 s) per successful opportunity, use 5.0 m/s² instead of 3.0 while the normal speed update is accelerating. Preserve the entrant's mapped `topSpeed`, accepted curve braking, and acceleration order. Do not require top-speed headroom as an eligibility gate: when the racer is capped or curve braking prevents acceleration, record zero applied speed delta and keep the attempt in the denominator. The assist must never erase the input-derived speed ceiling.
- **Turning/recentering channel:** during the same window and only while the entrant is in the existing recovery/recentering phase (not weave/contact acquire or hold), use a 6 m cross-track lookahead instead of 10 m for the existing lane-recentering correction and a temporary turn cap of `min(90°, existingTurnCap × 1.5)`. Keep the accepted ±20° correction maximum, nominal corner/braking target and recovery state machine. Do not alter contact or weave targets, teleport, cancel an accepted contact, or invent a pass.
- **Window limits:** fixed 40-tick maximum, 80-tick cooldown, and two windows per entrant/race. Do not extend a window because it has not caused a pass. Seeded assistance remains a short opportunity, not an automatic rank correction.
- **Trigger and audit:** evaluate opportunity eligibility from one frozen tick-start rank/gap snapshot before the existing weave is processed. Keep assistance draws in their own keyed namespace and sidecar; do not spend or raise the existing 84 weave-diagnostic cap. Limit the sidecar to 120 scheduled rows per race (six entrants × 20 checks through the 800-tick watchdog). Freeze each episode-local audit at its actual 40-tick or early end, recording acceleration applied/saturated, recenter correction actually changed, turn-cap changes, and window end. Do not let a later full-lap snapshot overwrite the episode endpoint. Any new mechanical-event records stay within the accepted 128 cap and its existing unresolved guard.

This candidate deliberately removes the extra 15 m curve buffer and no-active-episode eligibility gates. Normal braking can suppress acceleration, and the correction channel waits through acquire/hold; recovery and movement remain physical. Report actual topSpeed bands and input tiers for scheduled backmarkers, both channels' execution rates and paired place/win/movement outcomes, especially whether the low-speed ducks actually gain ground while higher-speed ducks retain their paired advantage. These changes are hypotheses only. If either channel is null or rarely executable, return that evidence instead of increasing force or weakening accepted caps without review.

## Candidate B: bounded leader slowdown

Use the selected 12–16° weave deviation and 10-tick hold unchanged. When a rank 1–2 entrant's existing weave episode enters its hold phase, apply a separate `targetSpeed × 0.8` penalty for at most 20 ticks (1 s). Keep normal braking and acceleration behavior; do not change contact angles, weave frequency, turn/recovery rules, or finish classification. It may execute at most twice per entrant/race and never stacks. Record a distinct seeded penalty event with actual start/end, per-tick target and speed effect, reason, rank, and overlap with wall/contact/recovery events. If it has no effect under the target/corner envelope, report that null result rather than changing its magnitude.
