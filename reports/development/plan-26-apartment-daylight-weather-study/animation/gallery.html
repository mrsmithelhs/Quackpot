Plan 26 — weather motion prototype

Review page, not game UI. Rain motion and a single-pulse lightning study. The scenes are the approved still weather SVGs, unchanged, with a host-side controller (animation/controller.mjs) that adds animation-only rain layers while motion is allowed and restores the exact approved still otherwise. Lightning is host-triggered only (button presses below, disabled until you tick the checkbox): one brief cool lift of the window and room per accepted request, never automatic. Warning: this page can flash. It is a design study, not a photosensitivity certification. The controls are free to use: pause freezes in place, Show still restores the approved still, and reduced motion keeps the still and refuses flashes.
