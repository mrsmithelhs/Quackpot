   - Verify the exact five conditions (melody-only isochronous, melody+harmony isochronous, rhythm-only, rhythm+harmony, and original full tune) and recognition percentages across professional musicians (N=14), amateurs (N=19), and non-musicians (N=13).
   - **Critical check:** Does the prior interpretation confuse *removing rhythm* (isochronous melody vs. full tune) with *replacing harmony*? Did Kuusi (2009) ever replace original harmony with altered or dissonant harmony under an intact melody, or did all harmonized conditions use the tune's standard chords? 2. **Liu (2018)**, "An Investigation of Adult Listeners' Conservation of Melody Under Harmonic Deformations," *Journal of Research in Music Education* 66(2), 210–223 (DOI `10.1177/0022429418776936`) and **Liu (2022)**, "Conservation of Melody With Varied Harmonizations: A Comparison of Adult Listeners From China and the United States," *JRME* 70(2) (DOI `10.1177/00224294211031448`).

3. **Jimenez, Kuusi, & Ojala (2022)**, "Veridical and Schematic Memory for Harmony in Melody-and-Accompaniment Textures," *Music Perception* 40(2), 89–112 (DOI `10.1525/mp.2022.40.2.89`), plus **Jimenez & Kuusi (2018)**, "Connecting chord progressions with specific pieces of music," *Psychology of Music* 46(5), 716–733 (DOI `10.1177/0305735617721638`) and **Jimenez & Kuusi (2016)** (ICMPC14).
