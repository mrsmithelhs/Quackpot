## Gaps

1. **Liu (2022) full-text access:** Accessible via abstract, article indexing, and detailed secondary citations. The exact numerical percentages and ANOVA F-values across Chinese vs US cohorts could not be viewed in full-text, but the core qualitative and statistical conclusion (both cohorts demonstrated strong, statistically significant conservation of melody across all three harmonic conditions) was verified.
2. **Lim & Goh (2012) full-text access:** Accessible via abstract and detailed quantitative citations in Schellenberg & Habashi (2015). The specific d-prime values for intermediate instrument family pairs could not be directly inspected.
3. **Pollard-Gott (1983) and Mélen & Deliège (1995) full-text access:** Accessible via abstract, psychological abstracts, and extensive subsequent citations in cognitive musicology. Detailed MDS dimensional weights and individual trial matrices were not inspected.
