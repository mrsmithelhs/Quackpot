## Gaps

1. **Direct Physiological Data on Adaptive Audio in Browser Games:** Across the empirical literature, studies directly measuring physiological metrics (EDA/GSR, ECG/HRV, EEG, or endocrine markers) in response specifically to adaptive musical structures (as opposed to linear soundtrack vs. silence or high-action vs. calm game mechanics) are extremely scarce. Available empirical evidence for adaptive game audio relies almost universally on subjective post-play or continuous slider self-reports.
2. **Full-Text Archival Access for Dutta (2020) and Worrall (2025):** Full-text doctoral dissertations are held in institutional repositories (University of Huddersfield and White Rose eTheses); verification was performed via comprehensive repository records, abstracts, and associated peer-reviewed publications (e.g., Worrall, Yin, & Collins 2024; Worrall & Collins 2023) rather than whole-volume inspection.
