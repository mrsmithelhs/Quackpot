## 1. Settled Baseline (Do Not Reopen Without Owner)

- **Action-based time selected:** Fictional life time advances on discrete action commitments, not real-world elapsed time, reading speed, accessibility mode, animation playback, or device performance (DECISION-034).
- **Session pacing:** Engaged run target of at least roughly 30 minutes, without an enforced upper duration ceiling or delayed exit/Uninstall. Around 15 minutes is a rapid low-reading reference only (Hard Constraint #4, owner revision 2026-10-30).
- **No real-time punishment / no delayed exit:** Reading apartment text, inspecting the Duck-Dex, browsing settings, or idling carries zero time or life penalty. Immediate Uninstall/Leave remains binding.
- **Physical companion vs. Digital gacha roster:** Physical rubber duck remains static in each scene; digital gacha ducks animate and race. Pawning the physical companion affects cash/story, not gacha collectibles.
- **Receipt distinction:** Receipt strictly separates **what we displayed**, **what you did**, and **how it affected you** (reflection question), with zero inferred player motives.
- **Presentation ownership:** Plan 28 owns apartment presentation (Direction A "things in their places"); Plan 38 owns production integration; Plan 29 coordinates numerical calibration.
