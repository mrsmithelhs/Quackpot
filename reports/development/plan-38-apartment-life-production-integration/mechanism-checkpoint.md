## Proposed policy and transition model

New runs receive immutable `lifePolicyVersion: 1` and an immutable configuration snapshot, separately from envelope schema; existing saves remain legacy-policy continuation, including old routes, values, RNG and historical events, with no invented conversion of their schedule. Schema 4 adds new-policy state and events; explicitly accept 1, 2, 3 and 4, and validate race-rest evidence by its introduction version (3), not by comparison with current schema. Preserve original localStorage snapshot/backup until an explicitly successful replacement; unsupported, invalid or failed hydration enters a recoverable non-writing screen rather than silently starting over. Migration wraps old state without reseeding, rewriting events, or relabelling a legacy run as new. Do not migrate an ambiguous in-flight legacy transaction into a guessed new phase.

One persisted `life` authority: day, named phase, phase anchor (`available` or committed transaction ID), breakfast/dinner window state per day, session ID and allowance/usage/active/suspended/finished, schedule and missed-shift count/employment status, rent obligation ID/status, pending work and recovery IDs, and next IDs. `state.phase` remains run lifecycle; clock/HUD/day bar and phone labels derive from `life` for new runs. Pure helpers decide eligibility, warnings and transition descriptors; engine alone applies cash/HP/RNG, records events and commits state. Views send intents; no second mutable life clock. Action IDs are persisted and rejected on replay. The engine validates intent again on commit, not only at display.

Proposal for initial working values, subject to root disposition: preserve existing $15 start, $5 each purchased meal, $15 remaining rent, 100 starting/max HP; $60 completed scheduled shift (selected); breakfast/dinner skip costs min(5,current HP); recovery restores 20 HP once after one phase; LateNight fatigue costs min(5,current HP) on next morning once; leisure restores up to 5 HP once. Values other than $60/skip cost are provisional for integrated review, not economy approval. No 70/30 thresholds or wage reduction. Proposed schedule: one scheduled work opportunity per day, Morning anchor, Days 1–4 and subsequent days; missing it at departure from Morning increments exactly once, three misses terminate scheduled work. Proposed overtime available before termination in Evening, $60 once on completion as a provisional implementation assumption; post-termination overtime disabled pending owner disposition. These calendar and overtime choices are explicitly open to root/owner; never label them selected policy.

Proposed baseline session allowance: one active commitment per phase, with a free welcome ten-pull counted as one commitment (not ten); a race or purchased pull counts one. Browsing, reveal playback, reading, phone down/re-entry and Dex are free. First commitment reserves that phase's anchor; leave unused baseline unspent until Finish Session or transition; Put Phone Down suspends only. A further commitment requires a clear warning naming next phase and any scheduled obligation displaced, and explicit confirmation before advancing/reserving it. Mandatory due-rent resolution precedes unrelated anchor commitment. One welcome entitlement survives suspension/reload and cannot be replayed. Day1 evening access also opens if the first shift was missed; this is a proposed exception, not selected rule. Explicit Finish ends session and advances according to chosen phase transition; no automatic phase advancement from navigation.

On entering Day4 Morning latch rent due before displaying it or running recovery; do not require notice inspection. If cash covers remainder, present Pay or Hold; Pay debits once including exact-zero balance; Hold keeps cash, logs a voluntary choice and immediately escalates. If short, escalate automatically with actual shortfall and no fictional refusal. Escalation retains obligation and allows visible Uninstall/Leave, inspection and payment if funds later become available; any additional available action needs root disposition, not invented lending/interest. Early voluntary payment resolves that same ID once. At 0 HP, latch recovery due; a one-phase recovery advances via the same phase-arrival resolver, latching rent at every crossed boundary. If recovery and rent coincide, persist both, resolve arrival/latch first, recovery may finish but cannot erase or bypass the rent decision; unrelated anchors blocked until the due decision/escalation is accounted for.

Work has separate agree → doing → explicit complete → once-only credit, with persisted work ID, disclosure and pending state; no rendering-triggered pay, automatic rent, or timer. Reload during doing resumes the same work; repeated completion cannot pay twice. Exit/Uninstall remains immediate; log only actual completed transactions and interruption facts before terminal and Receipt. Draw/race outcome and RNG snapshot remain committed at their existing engine resolution boundary; interrupted presentation resumes or uses static equivalent with no reroll/reward. An action and its new state/log/RNG are serialized as one envelope snapshot; this is not a guarantee of durable transaction on abrupt failure before save. On save failure surface warning and preserve old snapshot; repeated actions within current memory remain idempotent, but a crash before a successful save can restore the prior snapshot, which must be disclosed and tested rather than represented as crash-proof.

Events distinguish actual commitment, completion, wage, due, payment, hold, automatic shortfall, actual HP delta, recovery, phase arrival and terminal; attach transaction/obligation IDs and actual before/after amounts. Receipt projects log evidence only, matching payments by obligation ID even with interleaved meals. Initial configuration must be available to validation and projection without rewriting old history. All facts precede terminal because completeness currently requires terminal immediately followed by receipt_shown. No motivation inference or fabricated voluntary action.
