Owner-selected inputs used: Ren is a neighbor who is a maker/tinkerer (pencil, overalls, work jacket, tools); presence = phone avatar plus a half-body figure at the door; they/them. Owner direction (flexible) used: ambiguously gendered presentation, Black/Latino mixed heritage treated as characterization rather than proven by a face, possible tattoo and earrings. Proposed here: everything about how Ren is drawn. Heritage is expressed through a range of undertones and in the character brief (family, food, speech, craft), not through any single facial or skin signal. Each study shows two variants that differ in complexion/undertone, hair, dress and accessories; the shared sprout tattoo and gold earrings are held constant so only the drawing language is being compared.

1. The three directions, two variants each

A — Soft flat, kit-matched

Thick brown outline (3 px), rounded shapes, dot eyes. Same line language as the accepted apartment kit, so Ren would sit in a room without looking pasted in.

B — Angular graphic

No outline; colour blocking and a single darker shadow shape. Hair, jaw and clothes are faceted. Strongest silhouette and smallest file, but furthest from the kit.

C — Storybook, finer line

Thin dark-brown line, two-tone shading, almond eyes, defined lips, longer neck. Most expressive and the most naturalistic, but heavier to draw and more likely to clash with the thick-line kit.

3. Complexion / undertone range (one direction-neutral strip)

Three warm-brown fills with hue-shifted (warmer, more saturated) shadow and blush, chosen to differ in depth and undertone. They are a range to choose from, not a claim about what any heritage looks like. Colours were picked by eye as warm browns; they were not checked against the Monk scale or any measured palette, and no contrast ratios were computed.

5. Reviewer questions

  • Which direction (A/B/C) should refinement continue from, or is a hybrid wanted?
  • Which complexion reads as the intended Ren, or should the final show a different mid/deep blend? (Either variant could wear either outfit.)
  • Which hair feels right: short tapered curls, shoulder-length curls, or something else (twists, locs, a puff, a headwrap)? Hair here is not meant to signal heritage.
  • Keep both the tattoo (tiny sprout on the neck) and earrings? Keep the pencil-behind-ear and tool clues, or drop to keep Ren less "job-defined"?
