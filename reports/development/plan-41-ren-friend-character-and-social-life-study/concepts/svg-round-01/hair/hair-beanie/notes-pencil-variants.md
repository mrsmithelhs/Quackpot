# hair-beanie pencil variants

Both are lead-drawn copies of `hair-beanie.svg` with the on-ear pencil removed (owner request after the original pencil overlapped the cuff).

- `hair-beanie-pencil-behind`: pencil drawn before the ears and head, so it is behind the head and the cuff. Only the upper part sticks out past the cuff's right edge on the viewer's right, tip pointing up and right. The eraser end is hidden behind the head.
- `hair-beanie-pencil-tucked`: pencil lies on the crown, tilted outward, with its tip end slid under the cuff (the cuff is drawn over it). Eraser up, about two thirds of the pencil visible.
- Pencil is scaled 1.3x here so it still reads at this size.
- At a glance: both read as a pencil kept in or behind the beanie. The "behind" version shows less of the pencil and reads best at the larger preview.
