# hair-bedhead

- Drew: bedhead on waking. The viewer's left side is a smooth, flattened cap close to the skull. The viewer's right side is a lopsided cluster of uneven circle curls, with four stray looping curls.
- Construction: the hair mass is one group in `<defs>` (`hair-bedhead-mass`, a smooth left-side path plus circles). It is drawn once with `class="hair l3"`, then the four stray curls (dark 2 px outline with a `var(--hair)` inner stroke), then again filled with `class="hair" stroke="none"` on top. The curls sit between the two passes so their ends tuck cleanly into the mass.
- Template lines removed: the template's `base-hs-shapes` hair group inside LAYER-hair. Nothing else was removed. Tattoo, earrings, ears, face, neck and torso are unchanged, and the pencil is kept on top of the hair.
- Not drawn exactly as specified: none. The stray curls are dark-on-dark where they overlap the hair, so they read mainly against the background.
- At a glance: the silhouette is clearly lopsided and reads as bedhead, though the domed left side and tall right tufts make it look a little like a bun at small size.
