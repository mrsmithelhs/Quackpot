# hair-bonnet-night-v2

Lead-drawn (no helper) after v1 read as a beret.

- Teal satin sleep bonnet: a lumpy cloud-shaped puff that sits behind the head and ears (so the ears and both earrings stay in front of it), a front crown panel with two pale sheen patches and short gather lines running into a darker elastic band across the forehead.
- The bonnet is a different silhouette from the hat drawings on purpose: it is much wider than the head and bulges past the ears like loose cloth, with no brim or flat top.
- Template lines removed: the on-ear pencil (the bonnet covers that ear's hair; a night look).
- At a glance: reads as a puffy sleep bonnet; the band is a plain strip with squared ends, which could be softened.
