**Drawn:** Ren's base short curly hair (template cloud of circles, unchanged) dusted with pale plaster or drywall powder across the top, using flat patches in #e9e4da and #cfc8bb plus small dots, densest on top and thinning toward the sides.

- The Gemini dust is grainy speckle. I drew it as flat, outline-free patches and dots, since powder reads better as soft fills and the brief asks for flat shapes only.
- The dust is clipped by a clipPath built from the base hair circles and ellipse, inset by 2 px so it does not cover the outline stroke.

**Template lines removed or changed:** None removed. The `LAYER-hair` group keeps the template's two base hair `<use>` lines and adds the dust group after them. The pencil `<use href="#base-pencil"/>` is kept. No neck or arm tattoo is drawn.
