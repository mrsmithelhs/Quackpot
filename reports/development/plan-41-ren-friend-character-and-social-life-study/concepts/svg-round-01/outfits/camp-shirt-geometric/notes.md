# camp-shirt-geometric

Drew a short-sleeve camp-collar shirt with flat notched cream collar leaves, a cream placket with two gold buttons, a bold teal triangle-and-diamond pattern clipped to the shirt shape, small cuffs, and bare upper arms at the left and right edges.

Deviations and notes:
- The pattern is built from one 48 px tile of two triangles and two diamonds, placed with `<use>` in a 5 x 3 grid. The clip path and tile are defined inside the `LAYER-torso` group's own `<defs>`, so no other layer was touched.
- The collar leaves are solid cream rather than patterned, for legibility at small size.
- The placket sits in `LAYER-torso` (below the neck) and the collar leaves sit in `LAYER-collar`.
- No template lines were removed. The neck sprout tattoo is kept, and it stays visible over the background and the cream collar edge.

At a glance the shirt reads clearly as a patterned camp shirt, with the neck and sprout clearly visible.
