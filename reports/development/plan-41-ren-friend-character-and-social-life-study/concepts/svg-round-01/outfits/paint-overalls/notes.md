# paint-overalls

Drew Ren's baseline teal overalls and striped tee with flat mustard, sky blue and coral paint splatters, concentrated on the bib, pocket and lower tee, plus one drop on each strap.

- Edited only the `LAYER-torso` group in the copied template. The stripes, straps, bib, buttons and pocket are unchanged. The splatter group (`id="paint-overalls-splats"`) sits at the end of that group, on top of the bib and pocket.
- Head, face, ears, neck, earrings, pencil, hair and the neck tattoo are untouched and visible.
- No template lines removed.
- Blobs use class `l2` outline (thin dark brown); the small dots have no outline.
- Could not fully match the Gemini cell's splatter density; kept it to about a dozen shapes to stay a "handful" at small size.

At a glance it reads as paint-splattered overalls over the striped tee, as intended.
