## Checklist that makes curly bedhead read as bedhead (synthesis, helper-labelled partly as inference)

1. Flatten **one side at the roots only**; the pressed side hugs the skull and the other side keeps lift.
2. Break the outline with **small rounded clumps** that jut out irregularly, not a smooth ring.
3. **Vary clump direction** between neighbours; do not fan everything from one point.
4. Leave **gaps between clumps** so the volume reads layered, not solid.
5. Rounded C and S curl clumps; a few sparse stray flyaways only.
6. Build volume **outward in height and width**, not down the length.
7. Keep clump groups intact; do not draw every curl separately.

## Wrong readings to avoid

- **Ponytail/bun:** volume converging to one point or one lump/stalk.
- **Spiky triangle tufts:** sharp uniform points read as gel or a spike style.
- **Perfect halo/helmet:** a symmetrical unbroken circle reads as a deliberate afro, not bedhead.

## Why this matters here

Bedhead v1 read as a ponytail; v2 had triangle spikes. Both match the "wrong readings" above.
