## Helper's eight patterns, as the lead reads them (all PROPOSED, none selected)

1. Bounded visit with in-world availability, not a visible friendship number.
2. Three answers to an invitation: yes / not now (offer a time) / not this week.
3. Declining is free; only an accepted-then-missed commitment is tracked.
4. Slow, floored decay; no notifications.
5. Reconnection as two or three short scenes keyed to gap length.
6. Ren's own capacity as a visible cost of visits, worded as Ren's state, not the player's failure.
7. Name needs and causes, not virtues.
8. One neutral in-world line per missed moment; no repeated sadness or stacked notifications.
