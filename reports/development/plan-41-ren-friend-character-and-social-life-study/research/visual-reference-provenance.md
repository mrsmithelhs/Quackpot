## Documented pitfalls the concepts try to avoid

- Removing every gender cue can look child-like or non-human; androgynous presentation is not the same as a non-binary identity (snippet-level). Concepts therefore keep adult proportions and a few ordinary cues (build, clothing, jewelry) without resolving to male/female.
- Heritage as a single template or a single skin tone; "identity only skin deep." Concepts put heritage into the *characterization* (family, food, speech, neighborhood, craft) in `02-character-brief.md`, and offer a small *range* of undertones instead of one.
- Narrow roles. Ren is a maker, neighbor and friend, not a stock role.
- Skin shadows that merely darken; hair as a flat fill; ten motifs = zero motifs (one tattoo, one or two earrings).
- Unverified/disagreeing guidance on blue or purple shadows: concepts use warm hue-shifted shadows only; cool shadows are left as an untested option.
