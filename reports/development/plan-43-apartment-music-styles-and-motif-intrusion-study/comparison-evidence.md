## Score, level and controls

The editable frozen event lists and adapters are in `comparison.js`; exact note events, MIDI velocities, per-engine records, upstream voice tables, hashes and page-level HTTP requests are in `auditions/render-manifest.json`. Three 9 s files per engine test marimba, bass, and guitar single note plus staggered chord; the 20 s combined phrase has 14 notes over about 12 s followed by a long tail and is a sparse mapping probe, not a finished pilot or developed composition. Upstream schedules 15 table entries including its initialization note at most, below 16; all authored upstream note/channel/onset/release entries were checked against the score before rendering. This does not independently establish sample-exact timing, guitar performance fidelity, or every parameter's equivalence.

Both use GM identifiers 12, 24, 32, identical intended pitch/onset/held time/velocity and master gain .5. Plan 39 receives velocity as MIDI value / 127 and its own recipes; upstream uses its own velocity-squared response, per-program definitions, and compressor. Reverb/room, microgroove and shaker are off. Same MIDI identifiers/gain do not imply the same acoustic level or timbre. Raw PCM sample peaks A/B: mallet .06219/.15059; bass .09972/.23604; guitar .07864/.41036; ensemble .11295/.34124. Page defaults attenuate B by ratios .41296, .42245, .19163, .33100 respectively; A remains 1. Raw toggle sets each to 1 and individual sliders permit adjustment. Peak alignment is not a perceptual loudness match, LUFS or true-peak measurement; use ears and a comfortable device volume. WAV bytes are unmodified by the player.

Each 48 kHz stereo PCM16 WAV's SHA-256 was independently checked against the manifest and its header decoded; eight files total 18,048,352 bytes. Final-second float sample peaks were zero for all eight, a finite check, not exhaustive tail completion or clickless looping. No loop claim or subjective audio assessment was made. No safety normalization applied. Upstream noise/impulse buffer generation uses randomness, so bit-identical repeated output is not promised. One render at a time; 8.35 s total launcher time, 621215744 sampled private bytes, no stop/cleanup notes. Existing browser helper reported confirmed owned-tree termination, no residual or uncertainty, profile removed. Page/iframe HTTP request trace contained only localhost assets; this does not establish absence of browser-global background network traffic. The page does not read or write game storage.

The listener page was checked in isolated headless Chrome at 390×844: eight media metadata loads returned durations of 9 or 20 s; raw and restore controls and one slider returned expected values; no horizontal overflow was observed. Browser tree/profile cleanup was confirmed; this was not human listening, full accessibility or device validation. Plan 39 source, M1–M6/C0 and exports remain unchanged.
