Original and revised drums

Each pair plays the same three hits. The original uses genuine upstream TinySynth's drum bank; the revision uses the same TinySynth engine with a locally changed instrument recipe. No recorded samples were added.

The revision explores a less synthetic kick, less tonal snare and a longer noise-like hi-hat. Those are the aims; your ears decide whether the sounds actually improved. The revised hi-hat is much quieter at raw settings.

By default the louder sound in each pair is reduced to roughly align measured sample peaks. This is not perceptual loudness matching or LUFS; adjust by ear. Raw restores original playback levels. Starting another sound attempts to pause and rewind the others; paused-clip rewind has not yet been verified in the browser check.

For each drum, which version works better? Does the hi-hat now have enough of the “shhh” after its attack, and does the snare still have a flute-like tone?
