Two small guitar experiments

These are short dry sound tests, not finished music. They keep your earlier Plan 39 guitar A recording as the reference.

For sound, compare the reference and the new plucked-string voice: they use the same five pitches and onset/held-time intentions, though they interpret the note release differently. The plucked-string voice is a deliberately simple experiment, not a real recorded guitar.

For playing, compare the reference and the alternate chord: both use the Plan 39 voice, but the chord notes, strum timing and damping change together. It is a combined performance experiment, not an identical score.

The default turns down the louder new files to approximately align measured sample peaks with the reference. This is not perceptual loudness matching or LUFS; adjust by ear. Raw restores unaltered playback levels, and the original WAV files never change.

Does either new sound read more like guitar? Does the alternate chord sound more like someone playing guitar, even if the tone is still wrong? What feels artificial or distracting?
