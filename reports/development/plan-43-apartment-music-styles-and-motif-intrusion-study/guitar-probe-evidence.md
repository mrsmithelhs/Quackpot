## Two different questions

- **Same-score timbre:** `pluck-same.wav` uses the original five-note guitar score, identical pitch/onset/held-time and normalized velocity intent, through a small original deterministic plucked-string recurrence. It is neither upstream TinySynth nor a recorded guitar. It uses a finite 80 ms fade after note-off, while Plan 39 follows its own envelope/release; the held-time intent is shared, exact envelope semantics are not. Seed values, note times, delay lengths and frequency estimates are in `guitar-auditions/manifest.json`.
- **Performance treatment:** `performance-A.wav` uses unchanged Plan 39 guitar voice but changes the four chord notes from MIDI 55/60/64/67 to 48/55/60/64, changes stagger from 60 ms to 40 ms, and shortens held notes. The first single note stays the same. These combined changes test a playing treatment, not one isolated factor or identical score. The selected voicing is a plausible starting idea, not proof of human guitar performance.

The raw sample peaks are reference .07864, procedural .28665, alternate performance .08304. Default page playback keeps reference at 1 and attenuates procedural to ~.2743 and performance to ~.9470, based on sample peaks; raw and manual controls are provided. This is not perceptual loudness matching, LUFS or true peak. Both new renders have last-second sample peak zero, a finite observation rather than proof of full decay or seamless looping. The 9-second probes deliberately include tail/quiet space; they are not musical pilots.
