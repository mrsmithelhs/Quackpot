## What research supports

1. **Timbre and playing are separate.** An acoustic guitar's plucked-string modes interact with body resonances; the acoustic study's [abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26723347/) describes transient body-induced partials and pluck-position effects. [Yamaha's pickup explanation](https://www.yamaha.com/en/musical_instrument_guide/electric_guitar/mechanism/mechanism002.html) describes electric-guitar pickup conversion of string vibration to an electrical signal. Pickup, amplifier and effects are a different chain from an acoustic body's radiated sound, although acoustic/electric instruments and clean/processed sounds overlap. None of these sources says a single EQ preset produces convincing guitar.
2. **A different synthesis family is a plausible experiment.** The original [Karplus–Strong paper](https://www.moforte.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Karplus-Strong-CMJ-1983.pdf) describes a simple plucked-string algorithm. [Aalto's electric-guitar synthesis paper and sound examples](https://research.spa.aalto.fi/publications/papers/cmj-electric-guitar-synthesis/) show a more involved model incorporating excitation, pickup filtering and changing decay. These are useful starting points, not proof that a small browser implementation will sound realistic or meet our resource limits. [Tone.js PluckSynth documentation](https://tonejs.github.io/docs/r12/PluckSynth) documents a Karplus–Strong-type example and notes tuning limitations. No Tone.js dependency is proposed by that citation.
3. **Playing cues can be tested independently.** Voicing/string choice, down/up attack order, timing, dynamics, damping, slides and bends are score/performance choices, distinct from a patch or a physical model. A genuinely guitar-like timbre may still sound artificial with implausible voicings; better timing cannot guarantee that a non-guitar timbre will become convincing. The current four-note stagger is one weak probe, not exhaustive evidence.
4. **TinySynth extension evidence is narrow.** [Upstream documentation](https://github.com/g200kg/webaudio-tinysynth) describes `setTimbre` and a timbre editor; this proves a customization interface exists, not that a guitar-specific extension is convincing. The researcher did not find a substantiated guitar-specific upstream extension in the bounded search. That is a search limit, not a claim none exists. [Keystrum](https://github.com/kimhinton/keystrum) describes a separate browser Karplus–Strong/strumming project; it is not a TinySynth extension. Its exact revision, code, license and sound must be checked before reuse. Sample-based MIDI instruments are another family, but exact sample licenses, size and redistribution would need separate clearance.

## A small, reviewable next audition proposal

Retain the current raw guitar A/B files. After root reviews any renderer/resource implications, create a short **same-note, dry comparison** in the report folder:

- **Timbre test:** one plucked note and the existing chord score rendered with the better-liked Plan 39 guitar A as control, one carefully scoped genuine-upstream `setTimbre` candidate if source evidence suggests one, and one simple procedural plucked-string candidate. The latter is a new synthesis path, not TinySynth and not a relabelled Plan 39 repair. No library or sample acquisition by implication. If there is no evidence-backed TinySynth candidate, omit it rather than inventing a claim.
- **Performance test:** within one chosen voice, contrast a plausible guitar-register voicing and controlled stroke/damping against its otherwise matching baseline. Keep the sound source fixed and document every changed onset, pitch, note-off and velocity. This is a separate question from which voice sounds better; the existing 60 ms stagger remains a control, not the purported absence of strumming.
- Keep acoustic-like clean/fingerpicked and electric-like clean or gently driven **as distinct possibilities**. Start dry; any body filter or amp-style distortion is a separately labeled condition. Use a short note/chord/phrase, known gain and listening adjustment, serial bounded rendering, honest finite tails and retained exact score/bytes. Ask the owner whether each reads as guitar and what sounds artificial. Do not claim realistic acoustic or electric guitar from metrics, a GM label or model feedback.

The exact node/buffer/event and output-retention budget, cleanup, review and safe coexistence with existing 8 retained comparison files need a compact root clearance **before** a new renderer or expanded resource path is executed. Existing per-engine baselines and owner choices remain intact; no long track or production change follows automatically.

## Whether to use external deep research

Deep research is optional and may be valuable for finding **actual, traceable guitar implementations and assets**, especially if the first bounded procedural probe fails or a sample-based option becomes attractive. It need not delay defining a small experiment. `guitar-deep-research-prompt.md` is one focused optional prompt the owner may ferry; its answers must be independently checked and do not authorize downloads or license adoption. The user's ear remains the decision gate.
