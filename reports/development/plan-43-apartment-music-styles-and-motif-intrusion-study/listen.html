Early sound comparisons

Each A/B pair plays the same notes through two different synthesizers. These are short dry tests, not finished music. The combined phrase is a sparse 14-note mapping probe, with its notes concentrated in roughly the first 12 seconds and a long tail.

The default turns down B to align each pair's measured sample peaks approximately. This is not perceptual loudness matching or LUFS, and it does not make the comparison fully level matched. Use the sliders to adjust by ear, or choose raw levels to hear the original output. The WAV files are unchanged; use a comfortable device volume.

Listen for individual notes, bass and the staggered guitar chord. Which sounds feel usable? Does one have artifacts or a distracting attack? Do either of the combined phrases suggest a direction worth developing?
