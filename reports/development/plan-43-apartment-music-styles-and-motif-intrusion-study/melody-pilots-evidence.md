# M1 and M5 30-second pilot auditions

2026-11-02 owner accepted the latest drum balance and requested longer arrangement variations compatible with the accepted M1–M6 melody expansions for use as Suno inputs. Root cleared this first M1/M5 subset and 74,000,000-byte retained cap; the other four candidates remain a later listening gate. `melody-pilots-listen.html` has downloadable 30-second WAVs and links the Plan39 baseline laboratory. The six-candidate plan and proposed resource limits are in `melody-pilots-proposal.md`, not all implemented.

Each candidate uses its exact four-bar Plan39 lead pitches, beat positions, durations and symbolic velocities at the beginning, followed by four accompaniment-led bars and a four-bar reprise. Upstream TinySynth receives velocities rounded to MIDI integers; no note transposition. The score includes the complete original melody twice, and repeats the four-bar harmony in the bridge and reprise. M1 uses Skyward harmony; M5 uses Minor-iv harmony, including its B-flat/G-minor color. Guitar voicings are three selected pitches from the corresponding chord and bass pitches derive from the corresponding Plan39 bass progression; these are authored arrangements, not copies of the baseline mix. M1 uses a brighter, slightly longer/higher-velocity guitar treatment and later offbeat hats; M5 uses a softer, shorter guitar treatment and different hats. These are testable musical intentions, not proof of perceived quality, and the middle sections are accompaniment-led rather than newly composed lead melodies.

The selected genuine upstream marimba B, Plan39 bass A, Level4 guitar (softer excitation, slower decay, pickup color 4) and original-kick/tighter-snare/short-hat selected drum patches are used. The hat operator is amplified 3× before synth compression; it is not identical in signal path to the owner's earlier 3× post-render audition. Upstream noise is randomized, so repeat renders need not be bit-identical. Plan39 source hash `f5462620a3ffe7f83eaac7405cac22fdb9f92c2cbd4bff1bf0d4c400539ae68f` is included in each manifest. Each manifest preserves exact event source, realized patches, source hashes, schedule bounds and waveform statistics.

Static tests passed 2/2 for complete melody equality, segment-relative guitar bounds and M5 B-flat chord content. Each full melody has 19/20 original lead events respectively plus one complete reprise (38/40 total); both have 36 guitar, 24 bass and 48 drum events; guitar maximum concurrency 3. Final PCM sample was zero for both; no seamless-loop or complete-tail claim.
