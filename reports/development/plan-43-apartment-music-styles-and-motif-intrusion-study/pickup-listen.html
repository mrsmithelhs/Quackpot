Pickup-color comparison

The higher-octave reference and Levels 1–4 play the exact same phrase on the combined softer-attack and slower-decay voice. The four levels apply progressively more of one pickup-inspired sound-coloring effect; their perceived differences may not increase evenly. The earlier lower phrase is included for register context.

This is a simplified experiment, not a full electric-guitar pickup or amplifier model. The last notes end around 8.2 seconds; the 11-second files include fade and quiet. Starting one sound rewinds the others.

By default louder players are reduced to roughly align measured sample peaks. This is not perceptual loudness matching or LUFS; adjust by ear. Raw restores the original playback levels. The WAV files are unchanged.
