## One-note offline feasibility witness

Corrected browser run 2026-11-01 used the Plan 35 isolated Chrome/CDP lifecycle helper via unchanged Plan 25 bounded launcher, after root's browser-slot release. Three-second, 48 kHz stereo `OfflineAudioContext`; quality 1, reverb off, max 16 voices, program 12; one note-on at 0.5 s and note-off at 1.0 s. Actual source was unmodified. The constructor's original AudioContext was closed before replacing it; offline context reported closed after completion. The table recorded a released note at 0.5 s, and PCM had peak 0 before 0.4 s, 0.1794 in 0.5–1.0 s, and 0.000109 in 2–3 s. That supports this one coarse timing observation, not accurate note duration, sound quality or general scheduling.

**Fail:** the upstream `setAudioContext` initialization sends two MIDI messages while the offline context is suspended. Both invoke `resume()` before offline rendering starts and produce unhandled `InvalidStateError` promise rejections. The rendered sample exists despite these errors; it does not make this an error-free or faithful export adapter. Stop and return the issue to root; do not suppress errors or alter upstream silently. Voice-stealing/future scheduling, arbitrary score mapping, live/offline parity and repeatability remain unresolved. A small tail remained at the three-second cutoff; no tail-completion claim.

The iframe was removed and an instrumented callback counter showed zero additional interval callbacks during the following 300 ms. This is bounded observation, not proof of browser-wide resource reclamation. The helper separately reported no residual owned browser processes, confirmed tree termination and removed its profile. Launcher: exit 1 due to the explicit failed criterion, 3.97 s, 456,859,648 sampled peak private bytes, no launcher stop reason or cleanup notes. No audio file was exported or retained. Prior sandboxed Chrome launch exited immediately without output; an escalated `--dump-dom` attempt returned `PENDING` before async completion, so it provided no feasibility result.
