Plucked-string variations

These are short sound tests with the same notes and playing. The reference is the improved plucked-string voice you heard previously.

Softer attack changes the initial pluck's brightness. Slower decay changes how the note fades while held. All three still use the same short fade after the written note-off, so the slower version cannot ring indefinitely.

The default reduces louder players to roughly align their measured sample peaks. This is not perceptual loudness matching or LUFS; adjust by ear. Raw restores original levels. The WAV files are unchanged.

Does either variation sound less tight or more like a guitar? Which attack and decay do you prefer, and what still sounds wrong?
