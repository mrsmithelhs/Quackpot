## Proposed executable lifecycle for root review

- **Eligibility:** On Day 2 or later, while the ordinary apartment is available and no session, rent/work/story47/recovery/terminal overlay takes precedence, the first introduction can be encountered in any life phase. Availability is a derived candidate; it does not consume time or create a commitment. While the player visits Door, show one durable knock indication for that day if introduction is unfinished, no earlier knock was ignored that day, and no introduction conversation is already open. A day may pass without seeing it; no penalty or offer is recorded.
- **Ignore and return:** Record Ignore only after the indication was actually presented and the player chooses it. Remove that knock for the remainder of the current game day and permit the next eligible day, with no HP, anchor, offer, unanswered, history or relationship change. Unseen candidates expire without a record. Reload after Ignore preserves the same-day suppression; a new day is an existing game-day transition, never a real-time wait.
- **Open and navigation:** Open begins the introduction exactly once. Keep a durable dialogue cursor and selected branch; the line is logged only once on actual display. Moving to another apartment location closes the visible conversation but preserves the cursor and allows resumption by returning to Door. Resuming shows the current line without a duplicate display event. Leaving and returning does not create another knock or introduction; an explicit close/back preserves the same unfinished conversation. Settings and Uninstall/Stay restore the same cursor. Uninstall/Leave proceeds immediately to Receipt and does not mark unseen lines or number as shared.
- **Contact and finish:** Persist number-shared only after the relevant dialogue line has actually been displayed, once. Persist intro-completed separately after the final player response; while the introduction remains unfinished, even with number shared, suppress all Ren offers and contact controls. On completion enable the chosen contact access once, with no automatic invitation duplication or +5 HP. The exact subsequent contact entry point awaits owner/Plan49 coordination; retain existing functional channel only after completion until a separately cleared replacement is chosen.
- **Phase exit and 0HP:** No social state globally prevents normal apartment actions. A successful phase/day transition, forced recovery or other higher-priority overlay suspends an open conversation without marking it complete, without displaying unseen lines, and without creating or expiring an offer; resume at Door after the ordinary blocking condition clears. Resolve interruptions within the existing atomic life transition and preserve the cursor. If terminal Uninstall ends the run, leave incomplete and project only actual evidence. No forced recovery exemption is needed for a first knock because it is not a commitment; existing commitment exemptions remain intact.
- **Revision strategy:** Add revision 2 for newly activated door introductions and keep revision-1 replay/validation under explicit version dispatch, including its actual display text and event rules. Preserve revision 1 rather than reinterpreting it as revision 2; no unsupported cutoff is proposed solely for convenience. Accepted non-social saves retain existing replay. Provide targeted synthetic v1, v2, no-social, mismatch and reload tests plus display/Receipt checks before asking for visual technical clearance. If version-dispatch proves infeasible, stop with evidence for a separately reviewed compatibility alternative rather than silently migrating.
- **Presentation and art:** Owner refined the layout: Ren speech bubble at the left and Decide at the right when space permits; move Decide below the scene on smaller screens. Keep Ren visible and the speech and replies legible at desktop and narrow sizes. The closed-door art must use approved apartment architecture. If a new drawing is required, a bounded Haiku authoring brief and rendered inspection precede adoption; no invented geometry in mechanism code.
