Proposed `social46` contains version, introductory/channel state, next stable serial, at most one invitation/commitment, the last six closed eligible opportunities (IDs and outcome categories), current consecutive shown-unanswered streak, internal qualitative state, last completed Ren visit day, and an optional in-flight scene with stage, stable id and completion status. ### Proposed timing and opportunities

An opportunity is one eligible offered visit evening, whether initiated by Ren or the player, identified by its target Evening and serial, and closes once by reply/expiry/commitment outcome. One invitation can be rescheduled once to the next eligible Evening; a second request is a decline. Acknowledgment alone does not occupy the anchor. Invitation target is a named Evening, never wall time; player can read/reply free before it and gets a clear target and consequences when accepting. Proposal: generate no more than one actionable opportunity at a time. After a completed visit on day D, earliest next visit is day D+2, including repair; a commitment or failed offer alone does not pretend a visit occurred. Ren's initiative is normal at eligible opportunities while connected, every second eligible opportunity while drifting or reconnecting, and none while strained; the player can initiate repair in drifting/strained even when Ren does not offer. Exact eligible-opportunity cadence and day 2 introduction placement must be confirmed in root review with implementation traces; suppressed slots are not invented counted invitations.

At the authoritative transition leaving target Evening (including late-night, advancing, or a confirmed next-phase phone action), resolve a pending invitation or commitment exactly once *before* arrival. Reading is never expiry. The owner selected that only an actually displayed invitation can expire unanswered; discard unseen offers without a relationship event. A shown unanswered expiry increments U; any explicit response resets U. Other closed opportunity outcomes break the consecutive sequence; retain the last six closed actual opportunities, dropping the oldest when the seventh closes. Only broken commitments and shown unanswered expiry affect the selected thresholds; neutral outcomes still age the six-opportunity window. Do not present an unseen offer as displayed or record a reply that did not occur.

At a close, first append the actual outcome and age the window, then calculate thresholds. Connected becomes drifting at two broken commitments in that window or three consecutive unanswered expiries within it; a further break *while already drifting before that outcome* becomes strained, so the second break cannot cause both transitions. Drifting returns to connected on completed repair or honored visit. Strained has a free player contact leading to an available repair scene, then reconnecting; one subsequent honored visit returns connected. On a return to connected, clear the counted window and unanswered streak for the new episode; on entering reconnecting clear the old episode as well. A broken commitment during reconnecting returns to strained and makes repair available again, as selected by the owner; notice and forced bedrest remain exempt. No state labels or thresholds in player-facing text or Receipt.

### Anchor, conflicts, interruptions and couch

An accepted plan reserves the *named* target Evening without consuming the anchor until the player actually begins the visit. Before any competing anchor or phase departure, disclose the specific plan and offer `Tell Ren I can't`, `Continue anyway`, and `Go back`. Telling is free, closes without a break, and the chosen competing action requires an explicit subsequent confirmation; continuing deliberately closes once as a break and performs the competing transaction atomically. Forced bedrest closes the plan without a break; the exemption applies even if recovery starts from another phase event. Dinner and reading do not consume or cancel the plan. A begun visit uses the Evening anchor, holds a durable scene/stage, resumes after reload, and completes at most once; only successful completion applies capped +5 HP and records factual completion. Uninstall remains immediately available; Leave records an interruption with no completion or HP, Stay resumes the scene. Retention/Settings overlays must not create display evidence underneath them.

The couch sale itself remains neutral. Only a previously established shared couch game-night context creates a seating disclosure. If a plan exists, disclose the seating impact before the player confirms the sale; after a completed sale mark that plan as requiring reconfirmation. Show the factual floor-seating change before the visit or any conflict resolution, with `Confirm floor`, `Tell Ren I can't`, and `Go back`. Confirm preserves the same plan; telling is exempt; leaving the prompt unresolved neither breaks nor silently cancels the plan. A later deliberate conflicting action must still show the named plan and allow notice. A sale before any shared context is ordinary; do not infer the context retroactively. A sold couch remains sold; physical companion and digital collection stay separate.

## Consolidated technical amendment for root clearance

This section supersedes any less-specific timing, activation or atomicity wording above. It is **proposed**, not source clearance.

**Activation and historical replay.** Keep the existing initializer and all pre-activation transitions unchanged. On policy-1/schema-4 runs at day 2 or later, the first eligible actual introduction surface offers a free introduction; this includes an existing save already past day 2. Eligibility requires nonterminal life, no rent notice requiring resolution, no forced recovery, no ongoing work, no `story47` scene, no active session scene or modal/retention/Settings overlay; defer until those conditions clear, without moving time or generating past offers. Merely becoming eligible doesn't mutate. On the player opening the actually mounted introduction, commit one version-1 activation intent and a separately validated shown event; if the introduction is declined or postponed, no anchor is spent. Duplicate activation, malformed version, pre-day-2 and ineligible activation are rejected. Old schema 4 histories contain no activation and continue to replay identically, including snapshots and effects; enabling an already-advanced save never creates earlier introductions, invitations, or counted events. Schemas 1–3 stay on their established legacy path and never acquire the extension. Unknown markers fail closed. Validation must cover interruption/reload at each activation/display boundary, not reconstruct display from activation.

**Slots versus actual opportunities.** Derive eligible calendar slot `k = (day - 2) / 2` for even-numbered Evenings beginning day 2; slot identity is `ren-slot-${day}`. This is derived from the existing day, not an independent ticking clock. A visit on day D prohibits a visit on D+1 even if a slot is otherwise available. A closed actual opportunity has its own monotonic ID distinct from the slot; a suppressed or unavailable slot is not an opportunity and never ages the six-record window. On a slot, after introduction and when there is no pending opportunity, connected Ren may initiate; drifting and reconnecting initiative is only on every second *calendar slot*, measured by `(slotIndex - initiativeEpoch) % 2 === 0`, with the epoch set to the first slot strictly after entering the state. Strained Ren initiates no invitation; the player can freely contact Ren to request repair, with the scene offered at the next available slot. Suppression never requires incrementing an offer counter and never creates an invisible expiry. Player-initiated contact is permitted at an eligible slot, subject to one pending opportunity and the last-visit gap. A pending invitation is generated only for a specific current or next eligible slot, has no effect until actually shown, and can be answered free. One reschedule selects the next eligible slot and keeps the same opportunity ID; a second reschedule request declines it. When an unseen invitation's target passes, it closes neutral without counted expiry or public display claim. A seen unanswered invitation closes as one U only when its target Evening is left; shown, reply and closing are each idempotent. A commitment closes on completion, notice, deliberate break, or exempt forced recovery; it is not simultaneously counted as unanswered. On each **actual closed** opportunity append exactly one outcome and retain the last six. Each explicit response and each non-expiry closure resets the consecutive U run. Check B/U only after closure; selected transitions and owner-selected reconnecting break apply. Entering connected or reconnecting clears counted history and U; initiation epoch is freshly derived as above. Example: D2/D4/D6/D8 slots; normal initiation may use each, drifting begins after a D4 close and has candidate slots D6 then D10, not D6 then D8. If an invitation was never shown at D6, no U or fabricated shown record; D8 suppression still follows calendar slot, not an offer counter. Calendar slots, offer creation and closure require explicit replayable data/intent and negative tests; skipped slots do not require recorded synthetic events.

## Final owner cadence and unseen-offer correction

This section supersedes the fixed-even-day formula and neutral-closure rule above. The owner selected **flexible eligible Evenings**, preserving the maximum frequency of one visit every other Evening: after a completed visit or repair on day D, another visit/repair may take place no earlier than D+2. Before any completed contact, the earliest is the first eligible Evening on or after the actual introduction. An unconsumed calendar candidate is identified by the actual `life.day` (`ren-evening-${day}`); it is derived directly from the existing phase and does not itself create an offer or count as an opportunity. Eligibility also requires Ren's availability, no overlapping pending plan, applicable dinner/obligation/anchor guards, and the contact gap. Normal initiative may offer at an eligible Evening, with one actionable pending opportunity at a time. Drifting/reconnecting initiative is at most every second **calendar Evening after entering that state**, using a deterministic day-based epoch, subject to the same eligibility and gap; when suppressed, a free player-initiated contact can request the next eligible slot. Strained Ren does not initiate but the player can request the next eligible repair. One reschedule targets the next eligible Evening, not a fixed odd/even calendar; one pending ID survives that change. These cadence parameters are proposed technical defaults subject to root clearance, not additional owner-selected numerical rules.

Generation is not presentation or a counted opportunity. If an invitation reaches its target without ever being shown, discard that pending candidate as internal bookkeeping only: it creates no public evidence, no closed opportunity, no last-six aging, and **does not reset or increment** the consecutive shown-unanswered streak. The six-entry window ages only for an actual shown/replied/committed opportunity that closes. A shown unanswered expiry increments the streak; an explicit reply or closure of an actual opportunity other than shown-unanswered resets it. Tests must include: shown unanswered A, unseen candidate disposed, shown unanswered B and C → U3; one prior break plus six unseen disposals retains that break; actual neutral closures age the window and an explicit decline resets U; reschedule and last-visit gap across D/D+1/D+2; late introduction and player-initiated repair without a fixed-day delay.
