## Remaining phase controls: source-checked implications

At the pinned baseline, `life-view.js` shows **Continue to evening** during `life_work`, and `main.js` warns before advancing an employed player who has not completed that day's scheduled shift. The resolver records a missed shift on this transition and can end employment after repeated misses. This is therefore a real decision with consequences, not merely a dismiss button. A future location must preserve that warning and give an unemployed player a sensible way onward. Morning's **Continue to afternoon** is a distinct conditional route for unemployed players and specified historical tutorial states; it must not disappear in a visual cleanup.

**Finish run** appears in `life_work`/`life_evening` from Day 4 at this baseline and asks for confirmation before opening the Receipt. It is an explicit metagame end control with different meaning from immediate Uninstall; don't describe it as available on every screen or hide it behind a fictional object without owner agreement. A compact operational control may be more honest than inventing a clock, lamp or calendar metaphor. Exact placement remains a proposal for discussion, not a decision or permission to change production.

The owner clarified that **Quackpot is the core competing activity that can cause a work shift to be missed; dinner is not the action intended to move afternoon into evening**. The earlier dinner-as-transition candidate and its preview are superseded. The owner further selected that **starting Day 2**, an available competing activity that advances time can cause an unresolved dinner to be missed. Use the existing skip consequence (no cash spent, up to 5 HP lost) and warn before the player commits. Day 1 retains the guided opening; compatibility for existing runs still requires a versioned proposal. This is selected experience/policy direction for later design, not implemented behavior or source-write authority.

At the pinned baseline, completing the actual work story and dismissing the payslip already invokes the existing advance to evening; no extra end-of-afternoon button is needed for that route. Current source also warns when an afternoon pull/race uses an unworked scheduled shift. Existing meal guards can prevent phone progression while dinner is unresolved; they require a separate, versioned mechanism proposal before the owner-selected competing-action direction can be implemented. Do not characterize opening Quackpot, browsing, reading, canceling, or a failed pull/race as spending time or skipping a meal.

There remains a genuine design gap: the current evidence does not identify an already-implemented action that is always available to every unemployed, low-cash, recovery/obligation and used-anchor state and naturally leads to evening. A pull/race only qualifies when actually available and time-advancing. Future design must cover those states without manufacturing filler, forcing spending, or repurposing free navigation/reading as a time cost.

The future mechanism must stage warning, optional social-conflict confirmation, actual availability, phase transition, missed dinner/HP, any missed shift, play outcome and factual event records coherently: cancel, failed destination or failed action must not consume the phase, money or HP, create fatigue or break a Ren commitment. Do not double-count a previously recorded missed shift or meal. Due rent/recovery and the selected social conflict seam remain authoritative. The exact interaction of reaching 0 HP from a missed meal with the chosen activity, and compatibility/replay for legacy or pending saves, require a separate owner/root-reviewed proposal. Receipt evidence must reflect actual outcomes without inferring intent.
