# Flash synthetic audio transport/content control

## Setup and expected sound

This is the one root-cleared local-path synthetic control, not a motif review or a finding that Flash cannot generally hear audio. The audio-only staging directory contained only `item.wav` (48,044 bytes; SHA-256 `de7314f29f9230dc3791f7bfcb1bb05e88b71d5b249158f796ddda7593b2130a`). It contained no score, generator, transcript, answer, other media or source. The prompt requested the number of tones and direction of successive pitch changes based only on sound, with abstention if unable to hear; neither count nor pattern appeared in the prompt or filename. Tool access was restricted as in the staged Flash route; no shell, web, MCP or other tool was requested. Normal provider/local session persistence and incompletely audited startup context remain limitations.

The separately held-back `audio-control-generator.py` defines three pure tones with short envelopes and silences. `held-back/audio-control-fixture.wav` is an exact copy of the staged WAV. An independent read of its PCM samples (without model inference or spectral-analysis tools available to the model) found mono, 16-bit PCM, 16,000 Hz, 24,000 frames; 0.2-second stable-window positive-crossing counts were 88, 132 and 66, corresponding to approximately 440, 660 and 330 Hz. Thus the predeclared relative pattern is **up then down**. This verifies this generated fixture's frequency order; it is not evidence of human listening quality or model access to the audio. Generator SHA-256 `f2dd50c9d4110ecbd3e22bff13c6754ad26823a697c7430612238baea4c5610e`.

## Observed result

CLI init reported `gemini-3.8-flash-medium`; medium effort requested but not independently observed. A `read_file` tool event for the WAV reached DONE and no other tool step was recorded. The model reported three tones, but said pitch rose from first to second **and** from second to third. Count was correct; contour was wrong relative to the verified PCM. The stream exposed no WAV payload, audio MIME or base64 in the recorded tool event, so it does not directly show what audio representation reached the model. An answer and a successful file step do not by themselves establish audio-bearing transport. Both the route and this content control remain unvalidated; the evidence does not prove either lack of delivery or general inability to hear audio.
