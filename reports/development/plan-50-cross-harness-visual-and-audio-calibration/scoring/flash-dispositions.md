# Flash historical visual/UI pilot — preliminary dispositions

Five independent frozen still reviews under the owner-amended staged condition. Terra inspected cases 2–5 against the actual images after the restart; root reviewed the responses, scoring and actual images on 2026-11-01; owner review remains pending. Case 1 retains the existing root-corrected assessment in `flash-case-1-retry.md`. These are evidence-linked judgments, not an independent blind ground truth or owner acceptance. Categories follow the predeclared rubric: **U** directly useful, **H** useful hint needing checking, **A** valid additional visible observation, **P** preference/context-dependent or unresolved, **W** unsupported/wrong. A factual subclaim can be W while its broader question is H/P; a single claim is not counted as both a confirmed defect and a false alarm.

5. Rent-marker proximity to the container edge: **P/H**, visible crowding may warrant a layout check; rounded-edge clipping and a positioning-code cause are not established. It does not identify the clipped Call Mom text.
