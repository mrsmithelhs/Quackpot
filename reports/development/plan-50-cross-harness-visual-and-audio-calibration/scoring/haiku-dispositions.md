## Evidence and limits

Haiku identified a supported doorway concern, did not explicitly identify the clipped label, and made a visibly unsupported arm-color claim; The model returned 24 numbered observations across five cases, many conditional or subjective; all 24 require a human disposition against the task and image, and this study did not time that effort. The five duck-view suggestions in particular do not establish useful defects. Do not pool subjective art judgments and objective clipping as one accuracy percentage. Sanitized structured responses and usage fields are in `raw/`, with hashes of full local receipts that contain image data and machine paths and are not committed. All five trace records show one Read of the intended image; a sibling-path denial is a bounded single-case confinement test, not exhaustive isolation or startup-context proof. The first synthetic media control's captured output was partial. Model identity is CLI-reported; effective effort unverified. List-basis cost fields are not proof of billing. Lead inspected five source images and classified responses, but did not time checking effort or use a second independent scorer. These stills do not establish usability, accessibility, device, runtime, or production acceptance. Flash has not been tested and audio and broader adoption remain gated.
