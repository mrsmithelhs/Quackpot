## Separate paired comparison

The pair used unmodified 390×844 frozen files: A was case 5 (historically repaired), B was case 4 (historically clipped). The reviewer was not told the mapping or chronology. Its complete question was:

> Use Read to open a.png and b.png. Compare the two supplied game-view images. Describe visible changes between A and B and any remaining or new presentation issues. For each observation, identify the visible evidence and state uncertainty. Do not infer interactions, hidden state, accessibility behavior, or facts that cannot be established from the images. Use only these two images; do not seek other files, use tools other than Read, or delegate.

It correctly compared A's full “Call Mom” label with B's visible “Mom” and identified the missing text as the clearest difference. That is stronger paired discrimination than its single-image wording, but the pair is a different task, not a controlled causal experiment about prompts. It called B a “regression” and said the label “now” reads Mom: A/B provided no historical order, and actual chronology is the reverse of that implication. Do not treat this as evidence of a real regression or mechanism. It speculated about the clipboard, sticky-note movement, lighting and causes with uncertainty; these fine differences are unverified. It also presented crowded Rent label, tiny note text, blank space and muted text as remaining concerns; whether those require change needs contextual review.
