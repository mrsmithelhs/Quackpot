## Rent escalation mapping and unresolved slot contract

The accepted Day4 policy requires an unaffordable balance to trigger escalation immediately without notice-click gating; an affordable balance permits Pay or conscious Hold, with holding also triggering landlord escalation. Existing policy1 progression surfaces an overdue shortfall and permits later payment, but does not itself implement the full selected ladder. The older standalone `GameEngine` escalation offers, in order, extra shift, couch sale, TV sale and a credit advance for rent; it uses older values ($10 shift, $10 couch, $15 TV, $15 advance) and auto-pays when funds suffice, then ends via Running on Empty if the legacy ladder exhausts. Those old figures, automatic payment and terminal behavior cannot be transplanted as the new five-slot policy. The founding ladder calls for explicit meal reduction, extra work, couch/TV and credit, with later payday/Mom/streaming deferred; its exact timing and credit terms remain unresolved.

For Plan52, the unresolved seam must define: (1) finalize the selected real-slot overtime reservation and its eligible offer/terms while preserving ordinary intervening choices and the pending obligation; (2) sale/credit costs, exact terms, eligibility and whether they consume a slot; (3) when the player explicitly pays or holds after obtaining funds, including the solvent-withholding path; (4) escalation ordering, exhaustion and any terminal Receipt route; (5) priority and persistence when recovery and rent are simultaneous. The owner's Day4 stop choice is an evidence boundary, not an adoption of an ending or an excuse to omit the escalation contract. Until those decisions are resolved and tested, Plan52 cannot be called ready for opening.
