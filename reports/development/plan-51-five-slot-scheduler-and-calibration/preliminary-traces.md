# Plan 51 preliminary executable traces

2026-11-02. These deterministic, schedule-only examples import the unmounted scheduler directly. No actual gacha purchase, draw, race, gameplay RNG, rent, fatigue application, job termination or playtime is simulated. Symbolic operation references are not receipts from the real engine. Seed list in `sim/plan-51/routes.mjs` is reserved for a future genuinely seeded study and is irrelevant to these deterministic traces; it does not constitute a cohort.

- Seven balanced days: breakfast and dinner at $6 each, $60 scheduled shift each day, ordinary leisure and normal sleep. Given $80 initial cash and work available every day, the scheduler reaches Day8 with $416 and 100 HP. Rent and leisure recovery are excluded, so this is not an economy forecast.
- Day2 Midday play with scheduled work marks one missed shift and advances to Early Evening only on session completion. Employment escalation is not modeled.
- Day2 Early Evening play displaces dinner, records one missed dinner and loses 5 HP (100→95); it does not charge the $6 meal. The play result itself is symbolic and has no modeled price.
- Evening session can record one symbolic pull and one symbolic race, then a separately confirmed further pull stages extension into Late Evening and records pending fatigue. Its actual next-morning fatigue application is not yet modeled.
- At 90 HP early-bed candidate +5 ends at 95, candidate +10 ends at 100; both skip the Evening activity and Late Evening play. Neither value is selected.
- At 3 HP skipping breakfast explicitly causes recovery; with candidate +25 recovery and no other obligations, free recovery leaves Midday shift available, while consuming the Midday slot marks that shift missed. Recovery at a meal slot is described below. The owner rejects a special Day1 exception; current reachable tutorial starts at 100 HP and loses at most 5 before the required first shift.

## Reserved overtime while rent remains due

`rentOvertimeTrace()` is a bounded candidate mechanism trace. Day4 starts Morning at $120/100 HP with $170 rent due. The player acknowledges the shortfall and reserves an offered Evening overtime shift; that reservation consumes no current slot and the rent remains unresolved. They buy breakfast (cash $114), complete the scheduled Midday shift (+$60, cash $174), then deliberately pay $170 rent (cash $4). The overtime reservation remains intact. They skip dinner (−5 HP, $0), complete the reserved Evening overtime (+$60, −15 HP), and reach Late Evening at $64/80 HP with rent paid. No payment or overtime wage occurs automatically; each requires its actual operation. This is not a claim that overtime offer eligibility or +$60/−15 terms have been finally selected.

An alternative ordinary Evening activity can compete with the reservation; the proposed mechanism requires an explicit withdrawal and records it, without inventing a penalty. Forced recovery consuming the reserved Evening records a recovery exemption and does not forgive the rent. Normal intervening eligible activities remain available; there is no special crisis action lock while the reservation is active. Rent escalation beyond this candidate, credit/sale terms and exhaustion are unresolved. Model tests cover conscious payment, reservation persistence, withdrawal, and recovery; actual UI, engine transactions and save restoration are Plan52 work.

## Illustrative pacing arithmetic — not observed playtime

The scheduler advances on decisions, never timers. To make the 30-minute design target reviewable without presenting an elapsed simulation runtime as playtime, an illustrative longer route might contain eight 60-second narrative scenes (8 min), eight days with 90 seconds of combined reading/choice per day (12 min), six races averaging 22 seconds of presentation (2.2 min), three minutes of other interaction, and five minutes of Receipt reflection: about 30.2 minutes. Every component is an unmeasured scenario assumption, including the race presentation; the routes studied here do not prove those scene counts or durations. A low-reading route will differ. No wait or delayed exit is added; natural-play timing and whether the content actually supports these assumptions remain open for later owner experience testing.
