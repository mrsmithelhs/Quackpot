The missed-work path reaches the first bill $66 short, accepts $66, explicitly pays rent, and repays $99 on Day9 Morning; its HP is 45 by the Day11 $300 stop. This is one deterministic script, not a claim that these player outcomes are likely. The sales script deliberately sells **both** items for $70 even if one sale made rent affordable; in the actual run it takes no loan. This case is not evidence about simultaneous sale-and-loan burden. The no-job-named script actually stops Day4 at an unresolved first-rent refusal with $104 before its third missed shift: it does **not** reach unemployment. No whole no-cash/no-job continuation or recovery-at-zero route was established here.

The isolated full-principal control explicitly adjusts the Day4 balance to $0 (not a natural-play trace), borrows $170 and separately pays rent. At the Day9 Morning deadline it has $246 against $255 due after five completed Midday shifts and nine meals following the loan; it stops $9 short before Day9 breakfast. This is conditional on this timing, no other income and no extra shift; it is not inevitable default. The isolated $50 principal control repays $75 on Day9 with $171 remaining, then has $267 for a $300 bill on Day11. Both controls begin with a labeled synthetic balance adjustment, and do not prove those starting balances are reachable in ordinary play. The collision probe places both obligations on Day9 and stops before either payment: no priority is inferred.

## Decision implications and open rules

The candidate amount and interval must be chosen together. A Day11 $400 bill leaves no margin in the steady script and fails it by $10; a Day14 $400 bill is affordable in the same steady script but fails the missed-work script by $13. A Day14 $300 candidate is more forgiving across these five continuing examples, but still says nothing about the earlier refusal path or real play. These are diagnostic comparisons, not a final recommendation or a reason to mandate a specific outcome.

Before production, select the one-time fee and precise repayment deadline, whether and how a player may refuse or repay early, what happens at an unaffordable loan deadline, and distinct outcomes for inability and conscious withholding. Decide the interaction and presentation when recovery, loan and rent coincide; select second-rent interval and amount from a broader review. Consider merging the housing-loss story into the existing Running on Empty terminal/Receipt plumbing, with an explicit factual reason; the old terminal trigger and old ladder are not selected automatically. The owner's selected second-rent-failure direction still needs its exact trigger, timing, outstanding debt and presentation. No branch here creates eviction or forgives debt.

## Method and limitations

The existing study scheduler reserves rent overtime only for the same day's Evening, so the selected future-Evening behavior is not shown. The model uses Day4 Morning loan acceptance; an Evening acceptance followed by a Morning deadline could allow fewer shifts and needs a separate approved probe. The scheduler's external success references are symbolic, not authentication of game results. Study-only spending, furniture and financial ledger are not production atomicity or replay verification. The rent2 sidecar has no live engine implementation. The simple three-test suite checks fee rounding, duplicate and unaffordable financial operations, the real first-rent shortfall/payment path, overall cash reconciliation and collision stop; it does not independently test the full promised set of 0-HP ordering, sale/loan combined routes or UI. No browser, student save, app outcome, accessibility or human play duration was examined.

Both commands used the unchanged Plan25 launcher after read-only process preflights. Focused tests: exit 0, 3/3, 0.527 s, sampled peak 1,884,160 bytes. Model: exit 0, 0.520 s, sampled peak 1,863,680 bytes, no stop or cleanup notes. Existing Vite server was left running. No additional suites were run. Advisor posture: orchestrator-gate-only for this report-local behavioral model; no pre-proposal advisor ran under the explicit root direction. The separately authorized Terra-medium review remains required for the eventual production implementation.
