# Early Evening lighting — initial investigation

The owner observed that the dinner-to-Evening transition could be more apparent and requested an investigation of a distinct Early Evening lighting scheme. They said the dinner decision's lack of clock-effect copy does not need a copy repair. This is an investigation, not an adopted appearance or production change.

The five-slot view's `warmLife` selector in `src/ui/life/life-view.js` maps Early Evening, Evening and Late Evening to the same `warm` mode. Morning and Midday use `window` or `neutral`, depending on the room. Therefore the current light treatment does not itself distinguish Early Evening from Evening. The shared apartment kit exposes three static modes: `neutral`, `window`, and `warm`; the warm mode includes a lamp treatment and a local orange pool with darker surroundings. A visually distinct Early Evening that still leads into the existing Evening would need a new presentation treatment or a different combination of existing modes; changing a label alone would not create one.

Plan26's report-local living-room daylight study includes a low late-afternoon sun and an owner-liked strong-sun shadow treatment. It is a useful visual reference, not a production implementation or proof for the kitchen, desk or bedroom. Its effects were not mounted in the game and its room-specific geometry must not be silently reused as a universal treatment.

Candidate for a small visual comparison: a gently warmer, lower-angle daylight impression during Early Evening, transitioning to the existing warm-lamp Evening. Compare this against the current treatment in the kitchen and at least one other room at the same viewport, including legibility of the notice, object labels and the physical duck. Also inspect a narrow viewport and avoid making meaningful actions rely on color alone. The exact color, intensity, window direction and treatment of rooms without visible windows remain open. A report-local comparison and owner selection are needed before any production lighting change; no new weather or animated effects are proposed.
