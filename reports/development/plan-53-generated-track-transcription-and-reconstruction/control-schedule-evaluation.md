The authored Plan43 M1 control has 146 scheduled events (38 lead, 36 guitar, 24 bass, 48 drums). The Medium raw control has 117 starts. Before evaluation, the comparison rule was fixed at exact MIDI pitch and drum-versus-pitched category, with raw model onset within ±0.10s of scheduled reference onset and no fitted time offset. A maximum-cardinality one-to-one pooled match found 92 pairs, leaving 54 scheduled events and 25 predictions unmatched. Layer-specific independent matches are 38/38 lead, 28/36 guitar, 22/24 bass and 4/48 drums; these independent counts are not additive and could reuse a prediction across roles in general. In this run no event had multiple candidates under the fixed criterion.

All matched pitched roles were labeled `acoustic_piano` by the model; the four matched percussion events were labeled `drums`. The pooled matching's chosen signed onset differences had median -0.01s, minimum approximately -0.10s and maximum +0.06s, with one pair within 5ms of the tolerance boundary. Different valid maximum matchings may yield different assignments or distributions; these figures are not uniquely optimized alignments. Full unmatched events, per-role counts and candidate/boundary details are retained in ignored `local/control-evaluation.json`.

These are **agreement with the authored schedule**, not validated transcription accuracy: the score is not an independent waveform annotation, and actual sounding onsets, voice retention and per-note audibility in the synthetic source were not established. They do not measure offsets, octave-near matches, articulations or subjective quality, and the synthetic control does not establish performance on the Suno source. The observed agreement and label collapse provide a useful reason to compare alternatives without claiming a real-world precision/recall figure.
