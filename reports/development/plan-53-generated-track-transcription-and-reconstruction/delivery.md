They suspect the original might use a different instrument sound for lower-octave chords than for the higher melody: the reconstructed piano's high notes sound fine to them, while the low notes do not. A possible next bounded comparison would retain the 50% timing and compare the current piano control, a low-register attenuation/dynamics treatment, and at most one alternate low-register timbre. Constant assigned velocity 85 and piano patch decay are alternative possible contributors to the low-register sound. This remains a proposal for owner choice; no further render is authorized by the feedback alone.
