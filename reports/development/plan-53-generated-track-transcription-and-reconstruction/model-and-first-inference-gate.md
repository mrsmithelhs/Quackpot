## Separate first-inference gate

Sequential Medium runs only: one confirmed 25-second Suno excerpt and the existing 30-second known-score mix. Preflight the real host, coordinate a resource turn and require >=16 GiB available RAM, >=30 GiB free disk, >=6.5 GiB free GPU memory, and no conflicting heavy jobs. Each run: 15-minute wall watchdog, 8 GiB hard owned-job commit limit, device-wide GPU-use abort at 7 GiB, 20 GiB sampled workspace ceiling and 30 GiB disk-free floor. Sample at least once per second; log maxima and gaps. GPU and disk checks are sampled and can miss spikes, device-wide memory includes other workloads, and Windows job commit is not a measured private-memory peak. Stop on failed monitor, OOM, unexpected access, limits, timeout or unclear owned cleanup; no CPU fallback, retry or automatic memory increase. These are proposed ceilings, not claims of fit or performance.

**Offline enforcement is unresolved and must be proven before inference.** Concrete candidate: a uniquely named temporary outbound Windows Firewall block for the exact verified dedicated interpreter executable, plus a job active-process limit of one to prevent unblocked child executables, with owner/admin approval and root-reviewed creation/removal.
