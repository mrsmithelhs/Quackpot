## Proposed separate supervisors and limits

These are ceilings for review, not evidence of feasibility. Bootstrap and inference are sequential, each in a new Windows Job Object with kill-on-close, owned child tracking and bounded output; prove timeout and cleanup with a harmless synthetic process before real work. If containment, accounting or cleanup cannot be established, stop. Never kill unrelated processes or restart the existing server.

| Stage | Wall ceiling | Owned-process host private memory | GPU | Disk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bootstrap, including resolution/download | 30 min | 4 GiB | no model load | 20 GiB total local footprint; stop below 30 GiB free |
| One Medium excerpt or control | 15 min each, sequential | 8 GiB hard job ceiling | stop at 7 GiB device-wide used; require at least 6.5 GiB free before launch | same 20 GiB footprint and 30 GiB free floor |

Package downloads are provisionally budgeted up to 8 GiB and model/auxiliary downloads up to 4 GiB, within the footprint cap; actual dependency sizes and auxiliary models remain unverified and must fit before acquisition. Medium weights alone are about 1.14 GiB; peak VRAM, RAM and duration are unknown. Large is not authorized and needs a separate size and feasibility proposal. These ceilings are not the existing Node/browser 2 GiB/90 s budget.

Sample owned-tree private bytes, device-wide VRAM, free disk and elapsed time at least every second; sampling can miss transient spikes, WDDM GPU accounting may be incomplete, and device-wide VRAM cannot attribute competing usage. A Windows job memory limit is proposed as an additional host commit guard; polling thresholds are not hard VRAM/disk enforcement. Abort on failed monitoring, competing heavy activity, thresholds, unexpected network, timeout, OOM or an uncontained child. Terminate only the owned job, wait for exit and verify cleanup; preserve partial diagnostics and do not retry on CPU or automatically increase limits. Offline library flags do not enforce a network boundary: an independently verified OS-enforced per-process/network isolation approach and evidence that both outbound connections and unexpected attempts are blocked/observed remain prerequisites. No global firewall changes are authorized. Separate per-run logs record sampled peaks, gaps, elapsed time, exit and cleanup, without secrets or personal paths.
