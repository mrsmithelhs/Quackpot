The 7 GiB device-wide used-VRAM stop is a sampled safety threshold, not a hard allocation cap; sample at least once per second, preserve max/gaps, acknowledge WDDM attribution/overshoot limitations, and stop if monitoring fails.

**No OS offline boundary has been demonstrated.** The virtual-environment launcher and base interpreter are different executables; child behavior has not been observed.
