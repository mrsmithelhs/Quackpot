# Concurrent Bounce and steering: owner direction and proposal

On 2026-11-04 the owner directs that Bounce may activate during ordinary weaving and during collision deflection/recovery, preserving the effects of the earlier collision. The intended experience is like a kart racer using an ability while steering: ability and movement are concurrent, subject to the ability's own conditions and actual physics. Bounce is predictive and automatic in this study; no reflex input is added. Protection never changes an earlier contact or heals an existing injury. Full protection remains an owner-favored candidate to compare with reduced protection, not a selected balance outcome.

Current study code evaluates ability state during a tick before updating movement, but explicitly makes ability eligibility depend on `!e.steeringEpisode`. That gate also currently applies to crowd acceleration; the owner direction specifically resolves Bounce. This is a rule preventing concurrent use, not evidence that the duck physically cannot respond quickly enough. Separate diagnostic assumptions also include checks every 0.25 seconds, a 0.5-second prediction horizon and a 0.1-second rising phase; their impact has not been established, so retain them for the first isolated eligibility comparison unless orchestration approves otherwise.

Proposed report-local source preparation, subject to orchestration review: remove the steering-episode restriction for Bounce only; retain the episode itself and its steering and recovery consequences, other abilities' current behavior, real contact resolution, independent injury for each participant, and existing controls. Record kind and phase of the ongoing episode at predicted threats, eligibility, decision and contact, together with a specific indication of activation concurrent with collision recovery. Verify deterministic no-ability parity, same keyed uniforms and actual contact behavior, same earlier collision effects, no retroactive protection, and race/finish/guard integrity with bounded pure and arranged correctness fixtures. Explicitly distinguish ability activation from airborne collision separation and from a real protected collision.

A natural-exposure screen and any full/partial comparison need a separately specified finite allocation, pinned sources, immutable controls, resource limits, stop criteria and explicit QP Orch clearance. Report threat/activation and true protected-contact denominators; zero exposure cannot assess balance. No production changes, default changes to crowd acceleration, tuning, races or owner acceptance follow from this proposal.
