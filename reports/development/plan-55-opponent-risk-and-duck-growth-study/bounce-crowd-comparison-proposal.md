## Contract-first candidate, not production rules

- **Bounce:** the duck's own present-state prediction triggers preparation and takeoff **before** an estimated imminent collision; actual contact is not a prerequisite for the hop. A cue begins at activation. The estimate is uncertain: if the hop occurs and no contact follows, do not invent a collision or claim a collision was certainly prevented. Any genuine residual duck-to-duck contact during takeoff or air interaction is recorded, and its injury treatment follows the pending owner choice; the other participant's eligibility remains independent. Activation can expire without any contact. No cooldown/ability value or risk-tolerance adjustment is selected yet.
- **Crowd acceleration:** independent ability activates when an objective local-neighbor condition is met; it briefly adds a bounded acceleration effect without requiring Draft or Bounce. Preserve the accepted speed cap unless the owner later selects a different explicit rule. Proximity is an opportunity, not proof of a collision or a free guarantee of passing. It can interact with defensive steering and the duck's risk preference.
- **Combination:** a duck may have both; their triggers and timers remain distinct, and each audit states whether it was available, activated, canceled, expired or actually affected motion/contact. Common/non-combination controls isolate each effect. Rarity grants, ability development ceilings and combinations beyond this candidate remain separate tests.

Tentative implementation questions for root review before any race: use current positions/velocities and a bounded prediction horizon without future RNG; pin prediction horizon, activation-to-takeoff timing, hop duration, takeoff/landing timing, cooldown, activation probabilities/decision keys, risk-tolerance shift and crowd threshold/acceleration explicitly in a source contract. Candidate numbers are not silently selected by this document. During actual hop, maintain explicit ground/air state, allow air-to-air collision with independently eligible actual airborne participants, and apply no blanket immunity to genuine residual contacts unless the owner selects that outcome; whether ground-air contact is possible depends on the final spatial/height rule. A descending duck can interact on or after modeled landing; specify same-tick ordering and no repeated roll on continuous overlap. A duck that crosses while airborne receives its true interpolated crossing, not a delayed or forced finish; finish/guard/rollback and an expired unused preparation never invent contact or injury. Define wall boundaries and emergency/non-finish explicitly. No ability's purpose is to guarantee an injury-free race.

Air-to-air interactions require dedicated synthetic correctness fixtures because the primary screen puts only P in the air.

**Proposed Bounce activation:** at the already-defined 0.25-second predictor checks, a closing candidate with estimated time of closest approach in (0, 0.50] seconds and predicted separation below the existing collision diameter plus a provisional 0.2-unit warning margin can trigger preparation using a separate participant/opportunity keyed draw. No future decisions or hidden collision certainty are available. Cue and takeoff begin at the decision tick, independent of whether contact ever happens. Two airborne ducks are collision-eligible in synthetic tests and use genuine independent participant injury decisions. No extra altitude, speed or finish credit is invented. Enforce a 2-second cooldown after landing/expiry and no re-trigger during an active phase; permit one candidate-trigger test per opportunity and audit whether an activation occurred, timed out, contacted during lift/landing, or hopped without a subsequent contact. These thresholds deliberately overlap the earlier proximity metric but are not assertions of a physical pileup.

## Opportunity and boundary refinements

For the proposed first study, each ability has a **diagnostic** 0.75 activation probability per newly declared eligible opportunity, initially independent of risk; Bounce and crowd use different keyed namespaces and counters. An opportunity is latched after its one attempt whether activated or declined. It rearms only after two consecutive 0.25-second samples without the relevant threat/crowd condition. Continuous eligibility cannot silently retry every sample; a successful phase also enforces its 2-second cooldown, and an eligible condition during cooldown consumes no draw. The proposed report-local pure `ability-opportunity-candidate.mjs` models these timing/latch rules only; it does not resolve collisions, landing, injury, audio or production behavior. Pure checks passed 4/4 (0.546 s / 4,055,040 sampled bytes; 13.42 GiB free), after an initial 3/3 pass. This is not a test of ability effectiveness.

Airborne does not disable course walls/corridor or permit teleportation: wall and corridor rules continue to constrain horizontal motion in every phase. For a proposed 0.05-second tick, takeoff and landing transitions must be represented at their actual fractional times. On landing, test the descending duck's position and ground participants using the same swept-pair ordering and genuine separation/rearm policy; order crossing, contact and phase transition by their fractions, preserve the accepted finish-before-later-contact rule, and record an unresolved/guarded tick rather than inventing a displacement or erasing an overlap. Exact tie ordering and how a collision is processed when eligibility changes within a tick need root-reviewed executable fixtures before execution. This is a model proposal, not a promise that the simplified phase system has been validated.

The base-10 P already won 14–15 of 16 prior races, leaving a substantial placement ceiling. The first P-only screen can test trigger/interaction/movement/injury costs, but a high win rate cannot prove an ability has no value. Before final claims about ability value, separately propose a matched-stat or other meaningful headroom contrast with rested/healthy starts. Do not infer that a favorite must race tired/injured; no multi-race rest/recovery conclusion follows.
