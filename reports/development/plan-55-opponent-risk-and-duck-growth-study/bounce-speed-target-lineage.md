# Speed target and `maxSpeed` lineage

2026-11-04. Read-only inspection after the arranged crowd fixture reported target 14.2319, above the named `PHYSICS.maxSpeed = 12`. The name alone does not establish an enforced global speed ceiling.

In accepted production `src/core/race-resolver.mjs`, `maxSpeed` is defined at line 21 and used as the upper bound of the binary search in `curveTargetSpeed` at lines 192–193. The entrant's sampled rating is mapped by `8.25 + 0.5 × rating` at lines 97 and 114 to `topSpeed`; it is not clamped to 12 there. At lines 870–884 the controller starts with that top speed, limits it by the computed corner target when the curve/braking condition applies, applies other existing target factors, and accelerates or brakes toward the resulting target. The `Math.min(targetSpeed, ...)` limits an accelerating step to the current target; if speed already exceeds target, the braking branch decreases it rather than instantaneously clamping it. Existing impulses and target changes are distinct. Search of this production resolver found no other `maxSpeed` use.

The pinned injury Stage 2 adapter has the same meanings at `injury-stage2-adapter.mjs` lines 21, 97/114, 208–209 and 951–969, with the separately studied 0.95 injury target factor. The report-local Bounce adapter uses the same mapping, curve search and target controller at lines 31, 117/135, 213–214 and 972–991; crowd adds to acceleration only and the existing target-bound step remains. The exact fixture had speed 10 → 10.225 with target 14.2319, so it proves this one step did not overshoot **its inherited target**, not a global cap of 12 or saturation at target.

Accordingly, describe `maxSpeed` here as the upper bound for the curve-target search, not an established global straight-line cap. Preserve the accepted inherited controller and mapping without inserting a new clamp. Whether a separate game-wide speed cap should exist is outside this investigation and would need its own decision and integration scope. This is source interpretation and a diagnostic fixture, not a balance or owner-acceptance finding.
