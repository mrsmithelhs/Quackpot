## Experimental seam and tie contract

`bounce-study-adapter.mjs` is a separate copy of `injury-stage2-adapter.mjs`; prior sources and results remain untouched. It adds P-only independent opportunity states, prediction-triggered cue/takeoff, crowd acceleration and layer-aware actual pair resolution. P remains healthy/rested with base rating 10 vs rivals 8; that placement ceiling limits any claims of ability value. It keeps the course, walls, speed target/cap, contact recovery and seeded baseline. Bounce uses two 0.05-s rising ticks, ten airborne and two landing ticks; opportunity sampling every five ticks, 0.75 chance per latched opportunity, 40-tick cooldown after phase completion, two absent samples before rearm. Crowd sampling/activation is independent; 10 active ticks add 1.5 acceleration, still bounded by the existing target/cap. Bounce-active ordinary advice-follow chance is multiplied by 0.8. These are provisional study settings. A prediction or takeoff with no contact is recorded as an activation, never a fabricated or certainly prevented contact.

At genuine swept contact fraction f in simulation tick k, layer and protection are evaluated at absolute tick coordinate k−1+f against half-open phase windows (transition boundary belongs to the new phase). Ground, rising and landing share a contact layer; air contacts air; ground and air are separated in this model. Partial uses 0.5× the medium conditional chance, full uses zero; both retain the same keyed injury uniform as the baseline and the unprotected participant keeps their own independent chance. A zero-probability decision is still logged. A duck already injured receives no further injury roll. Course/wall constraints remain active while airborne.

The existing accepted resolver interpolates a genuine crossing and terminally excludes that finished entrant from the tick-end endpoint contact resolver; this includes an earlier swept candidate for the finisher, as its existing ordering audit documents. It is not claimed to be a general physical event-time solver. For entrants not excluded by finish, phase boundaries are half-open and a boundary tie takes the new phase. If the duck transitions to landing while already horizontally overlapping a grounded entrant, this draft **stops with `landing-overlap-unresolved` guard and rolls back the tick**; it does not teleport, invent a contact, or claim successful landing. This conservative limitation may stop an otherwise interesting run; root must assess it before any screen. Actual pair rearm/separation remains unchanged, and the source keeps `layerSeparated` distinct in contact audit rather than claiming that a contact occurred.

The phase/timer state lives inside checkpointed entrants and ability audit length is restored on guarded rollback. Its full behavior with live contact, injury, finish and phase transitions has not been proven. The layer-separated log, arrival phase, rank-at-contact and actual injury rolls must be checked against trajectories in any future replay. No new owner-facing animation is implemented; generic study cues and Plan57 assets must not be conflated.
