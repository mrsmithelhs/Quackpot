## Predictor contract and limitations

Only a followed threat gets a new target choice. `clearance-target-candidate.mjs` tests offsets -0.4,+0.4,-0.8,+0.8,-1.2,+1.2 about current lateral coordinate; rejects targets beyond ±6 corridor, samples t=0,.25,.5,.75,1 s against currently active local neighbors (within six world units and eight along course), and scores each feasible choice by minimum predicted neighbor separation over all samples. Ties use smallest absolute offset then negative side. It selects only if the best score exceeds no-advice baseline by at least 0.1 units; a time-zero minimum may make all candidates a legitimate no-op. Audit stores all candidate scores/feasibility, chosen value or no-op reason, trigger and risk decision.

Approximation: set ω = existing ordinary speed-dependent turn cap in degrees/s × π/180; at time t, use lateral reach bound v(1−cos(ωt))/ω (zero for ω=0), capped to target-offset magnitude. Estimate own world position as current position + current velocity×t + current course normal×signed bounded lateral reach. Neighbors are linear current-state extrapolations. This deliberately adds a lateral term to the unchanged velocity vector and assumes an initially aligned turn for the bound: it is **not** an exact controller simulation, may overestimate actual reachable movement or total speed and ignores changing curve normal. It supplies a heuristic ranking only, not guaranteed clearance, collision avoidance, or a physically valid path. Live race motion still obeys its existing controller and caps. The implementation does not invent future RNG or force duck separation. `clearance-stage-core.mjs` records requested, approximate one-second and realized lateral change, actual contact and cancellation within the window; after-contact movement may be recovery, not advice effect.
