# First duck-growth choice and injury lifecycle - owner interview draft

Status: report-local design, not implementation authorization or numerical balance. Owner selected in this chat on 2026-11-04: the first duck-earned, bankable, permanent point may be assigned to Bounce or crowd acceleration, without consuming a life slot; an injured duck remains raceable and recovers as in-game days pass, with no required purchase. Earlier owner preferences: every completed race advances the entered duck, stronger finishes and event goals can add progress, racing while injured may extend recovery, and accessible low/medium/high risk plus optional exact conditional odds are preferred. Full Bounce injury protection during its active window is selected for now; actual collision effects remain and the other participant's injury risk is independent (DECISION-084).

## Candidate growth trace - illustrative pending owner response

Consider each entered owned duck having its own progress and point bank. A completed race gives 2 progress even in last place; top-three finish adds 1, so two losses earn the first point at 4, and a first-place then a loss gives 5. No progress for a cancelled/unresolved race; an unrelated duck does not silently gain entered-duck progress. A point is not awarded twice for one committed race. A four-point first threshold does not let a top-three bonus accelerate the first choice: both two losses and any two races reach it. An alternative five-point threshold makes three losses, or two races including a top-three finish, earn it; owner comparison pending. At five points a rotating three-duck roster after six races reaches the point for a duck only if that duck achieved at least one top-three finish. Do not claim the bonus matters to the first point at four. The player can leave it banked or assign it permanently to Bounce or crowd acceleration without spending an additional life slot. Explain the choice and permanence before confirmation. This first slice needs only this first point; later thresholds, repeat investment, event-goal bonuses, starting abilities by rarity and eventual combinations remain open. The example does not override the preference for stronger finishes/goals to add progress and does not prove an ordinary actual run has enough races. At threshold four a 3-duck player who rotates gets two races per duck after six races and could reach each first point; actual access and life costs still need a bounded real-path check.

Bounce automatically prepares when its candidate predictive conditions occur; full injury protection is active in the selected window. It can still have a real collision and can injure another duck. Crowd acceleration is a separate automatic ability whose temporary acceleration is subject to the existing target and opportunities; neither choice promises to activate, prevent a collision or win a race. Candidate exact probabilities/durations, animation and numerical effectiveness need a tested contract; do not advertise an unsupported speed or victory guarantee. For a duck that later already starts with one ability, whether a first investment improves an existing ability or unlocks the other remains a separate design question; do not silently assign a duplicate.

## Candidate injury trace - illustrative pending owner response

A new injury occurs on an actual independently evaluated contact, with participant-specific event/roll evidence and current Bounce protection. The duck remains selectable and receives an illustrative 10% rating penalty while injured, distinct from normal rest; if the player nevertheless races a duck already injured at commit, show a simple extension-risk label with accessible exact conditional odds before committing. A race by a healthy duck that becomes injured is not also treated as starting injured; actual same-race effect, whether the extension depends on finish versus commitment, and risk percentage need explicit selection. No new injury is stacked on an already injured duck unless separately designed. Explain any injury plus rest composition in the choice UI rather than presenting a falsely simple final speed.

Illustration: injury after a race on day 2 starts with two day changes remaining. On day 3 it has one remaining; if it is not raced injured, the day-4 transition clears it for free. If an eligible race while injured triggers an extra day, day 4 is not necessarily the recovery day. Precise sequencing at transition, ties between extension and recovery, and whether playing/racing during a day affects that day must be specified before production; do not silently heal via elapsed real-world time, waiting, paid item, or UI-only clock. A single-duck player can continue racing with the disclosed downside and cannot be trapped behind a purchase.
