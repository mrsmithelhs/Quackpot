# Limited-time Quackpot events - owner proposal

Status: separate report-only future-event proposal, not production implementation, approved schedule, numerical balance, or reason to delay the basic first growth and injury slice. On 2026-11-04 the owner proposes limited-time events lasting one in-game day or sometimes only a couple of five-slot time slots. Example benefits include more duck customizations, increased Down paid for duplicate pulls, and ability-point requirements lowered by one progress unit (interpreting the owner's example as a normal first point at 4 progress lowered to 3). The owner wants one or two early events during relatively quiet periods, followed later by events that overlap important social moments, work shifts or deadlines. Owner selects free access to the event itself: normal costs and availability of individual actions or customizations are not automatically waived and must be shown honestly.

This is a distinct proposal from the one-day Gem purchase in `gem-one-day-progression-proposal.md`: a free event's opportunity, a paid progression boost and a paid pull can overlap in future, but their eligibility, cost, sequencing and interaction are not yet selected. Do not conflate event entry with purchasing Gems or assume all example benefits occur in the same event. More customizations could mean new options, not necessarily free items; any fictional cost, availability and terms must be clear before the player acts. No event changes the free injury recovery or creates a purchase lockout.

## First-point illustration and finite schedule

Under the selected ordinary first-growth rule, a completed race gives an entered duck 2 progress, with 1 extra for top three; a first ability point needs 4. Under a proposed event threshold of 3, a first top-three finish from zero would reach that threshold in one race, whereas an unplaced race would still leave the duck at 2 until another qualifying race. With the normal threshold, either path takes at least two completed races. This assumes the event applies to that duck/race and does not decide treatment of pre-event progress, timing of accrual/expiration, paid-boost stacking, later point thresholds or extra points; those must be selected before a production promise.

The accepted five-slot schedule has Morning breakfast, Midday work when available, Early Evening dinner, Evening leisure/early bed and Late Evening optional active play/bedtime. An illustrative two-slot event could span Evening and Late Evening, with the end boundary declared in the fictional game; early bedtime can skip past opportunities and does not pause the event. A later event might overlap an actual available scheduled shift or a specific social commitment, with the existing slot choice and consequence warnings. Exact event timing must use live five-slot schedule data and explicit slot boundaries, not real time or assumptions that every day has an available shift. A one-day event's start/end interpretation is still to be defined and should not be conflated with the separately proposed paid boost's same-slot-next-day expiration. A gacha session currently allows one pull action and one race in its slot under the selected life contract; do not assume an unlimited number of races or pulls can be squeezed into one slot.

The early/later schedule can teach value and then create a consequential conflict, but it is a design hypothesis about pressure, not evidence that a student will respond in a particular way or choose to neglect a commitment. No invented forced wait, real-time countdown, blocked exit or mandatory spending is authorized. Event offer timing, duration, actual eligible actions, costs, rate changes and terms must be intelligible before commitment; show remaining in-game window accessibly and honor reduced motion. Keep an ordinary nonspending path and truthful immediate exit.
