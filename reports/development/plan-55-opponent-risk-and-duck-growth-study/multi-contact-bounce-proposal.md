## Defensive-driving candidate

At an actual approach opportunity, a duck could make a modest seeded steering adjustment toward a safe available path, subject to existing turn-rate and course limits and a recovery path. This may reduce sustained bunching, but cannot guarantee no contacts or create an unrecorded avoidance. Compare enabled and disabled, and record opportunities, eligible participants, attempted direction, actual path and any later contacts. Avoid claiming that a particular collision was prevented just because a cue was shown: estimate a paired aggregate difference and, if proposing an individual counterfactual, compute it explicitly with limitations.
