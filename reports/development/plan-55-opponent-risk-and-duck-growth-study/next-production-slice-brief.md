## Observable first slice

A player races an owned duck, sees an actual completed result and progress, banks its first point and permanently assigns Bounce or crowd acceleration without spending a life slot. A genuine collision can independently injure involved ducks; injured ducks remain selectable with disclosed rating effect and extension chance, recover across actual game-day changes, and follow DECISION-085. Bounce can activate automatically during steering, protects its duck from injury during its active window, and can still collide on landing. An unresolved attempt must never be presented as a completed finish or award; retained validated injuries must persist independently of playback with accessible no-result explanation. Preserve current Plan23 race behavior, rest and no-injury controls as independently testable reference paths; exact injury occurrence, trigger and motion parameters in `current-delivery.md` remain diagnostic candidates until explicitly approved for the packet.
