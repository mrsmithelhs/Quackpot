# Predictive traffic avoidance — candidate contract for root review

2026-11-03. Owner prioritizes avoiding sustained clumping and clarifies that ducks should steer away from crowds likely to lead to collisions, with risk profile and proximity-benefiting abilities affecting whether they follow that advice. This is a report-local design proposal, not code, selected numbers, production behavior or execution authorization.

## Why this candidate

The initial provisional rule looks at the number of nearby ducks to choose a side. In eight paired mixed-field seeds, it recorded 67 contacts with advice vs 59 without, and 11 vs 10 rapid cluster pairs; it did not demonstrate an anti-clumping benefit. Those records use an earlier advice implementation and remain immutable. The subsequent corrected target-cancellation/audit version has correctness checks, not an equivalent effectiveness comparison. Counting neighbors on one side does not necessarily tell a duck whether a nearby path is converging or whether moving sideways enters another duck's route. No conclusion about a particular causal mistake follows from aggregate counts.

## Candidate perception and decision

Every active duck uses the same course-local perception method, including player and rivals. From current world position, velocity/heading, course progress, participant radii and corridor boundaries, estimate projected footprint proximity over a finite horizon for nearby active entrants on the same relevant track segment. Reject physically nearby but unrelated track segments using a documented course-relative check, including lap-wrap and curve cases. Use the current snapshot and declared extrapolation; forecasts are uncertain, not future collision facts. The model sees no future random draws or private player information.

For an eligible approach, evaluate the current path and two candidate small lateral targets, using the **same existing controller, turn cap, corridor and short horizon**. Discard a side whose predicted path would leave the corridor or requires an infeasible turn. Compare a predeclared projected-risk score based on the nearest predicted footprint gap/closing encounters; choose a side only if its predicted score improves on staying by a fixed minimum. Ties use stable participant identity/order. If neither side improves the forecast, record `no-improvement`; if no path is feasible, record `no-feasible-path`. Do not teleport, override a real contact or choose by peeking at later events. Avoidance is an advice opportunity, not a promise a collision will be prevented.

For a first deterministic candidate, propose projecting 0.75 s in finite 0.05 s steps and reconsidering no more often than every 0.25 s per duck, with a 0.5 s candidate cooldown after a chosen maneuver; final exact geometry, lateral target size and minimum improvement must be root-reviewed and pinned. Reason for reconsideration: at a 4 m separation and a relative closing speed of 8 m/s, a one-second wait can span twice the approximate closing time. This is a timing illustration, not a measurement of current race velocity distributions or evidence that the selected horizon is sufficient. Report actual observed relative speed, proximity, prediction and latency; avoid silently tightening cadence or picking easy seeds after outcomes. Existing speed and turn constants remain unchanged as a control.

Once an improved feasible path exists, a separately seeded decision uses the entrant's declared risk profile: riskier ducks ignore advice more often. For initial direct comparability, the prior 0.75 steady / 0.35 variable follow probabilities are candidates, not selected player-facing odds. A proximity-benefiting ability can alter this advice decision **only when actually equipped and eligible**, with any multiplier separately specified before an ability experiment; the base traffic candidate has no ability modifier. Keep prediction, advice probability/draw, selected side, actual final steering/recovery priority and observed contacts separate. An active collision or weave recovery takes priority and cancels stale advice; no unrecorded reactivation. Record local neighbors, projected scores, side feasibility, advice followed/ignored, active target and actual consequences without revealing a fabricated real player or recording a fictional cue as displayed.

## Relationship to Bounce and injury

The same course-local perception primitives might later provide an automatic pre-contact Bounce opportunity, but **prediction and activation are distinct**: threat detection does not cause Bounce unless it is equipped, eligible and its own independently keyed trigger succeeds. A false alarm/no-actual-contact case and a changed path case are required. Bounce's preparatory animation and altitude/landing contract remain separately scoped; don't infer a prevented collision from activation. Its active risk-tolerance effect must have an explicit duration and independently reviewed rule.

The susceptibility comparison was completed using corrected prior advice as an explicitly provisional traffic baseline: eight paired seeds in injury-off, first eligible per duck/race and per-distinct-contact arms. The results are in `injury-susceptibility-results-summary.md`; 12 versus 27 of 48 entrant-races were newly injured in the two injury arms under diagnostic values, with 43 exposed in each. This small single-race study does not validate this predictive driver or final injury rates. No repeat susceptibility run is proposed by this document. A future predictive-traffic comparison must not simultaneously retune the collision model, injury p, abilities and rarity, or it cannot isolate the effect. Root should decide sequencing and exact finite scope; no additional sample is implied merely by this document.

## Suggested gates and honest evidence

First inspect the actual controller/track geometry and prepare deterministic synthetic correctness fixtures: converging versus diverging footprints, same-world-position/unrelated-track rejection, curve and lap-wrap, two unsafe sides, side choice that does not improve risk, risk-conditioned follow/ignore, episode cancellation, no injury/contact invention, individual finish and full-tick guard rollback with live contact/advice state. Include no-collision and prediction-changes cases. Synthetic inputs are correctness evidence, not balance races. Before implementation or execution QP Orch reviews the exact adapter seam, prediction approximation, participant inputs, fixed seeds/arms, finite fixture and model-call counts, source/config pins, output/memory/wall limits and shared resource allocation.

Compare original production one-contact reference, frozen multi-contact prior candidate and one predictive candidate only where meaningful; if collision policies differ, don't attribute all differences to advice. Report per-seed actual participants/contact episodes, repeated same-pair encounters, crowd/constrained duration with sampling cadence, attempted/followed/ignored/no-improvement/no-path and time-to-contact, individual crossings/guard outcomes. Crowding and collision counts do not establish that the racing looks or feels right. Preserve no-injury reference and do not tune by seed filtering, forced contact or forced finish. No production/graphics/provider action is authorized here.
