# Stage B readiness proposal — not execution clearance

Fourteen fixed shards `progression-00.json` through `progression-13.json`, exclusive-create/no overwrite. Shard k owns indices 8k..8k+7, eight runs × 12 opportunities = at most 96 calls. Index/14 gives seed `p55p-000`..`007`; index mod14 selects exactly the frozen list in `follow-on-progression.mjs`: favorite then rotate, each no ability/Burst/Guard in post-race and same-race modes plus one injury-off/no-growth control. Maximum 112 runs / 1,344 Stage B calls. An unresolved race stops its run, records its attempt and reason, and restores pre-attempt candidate state; no growth/reward/history/recovery commits. Later opportunities are missing, not replaced. Exceptions abort the shard, retain its failure output and completed records, count all attempts and stop the batch; no automatic rerun.

A six-rival stable pool rotates order by opportunity, then chooses uninjured entrants first and injured entrants if needed to fill five slots. Slot-to-stable identity, chosen stance, known injury and rating are explicit in each race; actual contact slot participants map back to stable IDs. Independently keyed injury draws include the stable participant ID and genuine contact ID; paired branches need not encounter identical contacts. Each completed race commits once in report-local state and duplicate race IDs throw. An injured absent duck/rival recovers after that completed race, before the next selection, with recovery recorded; a selected injured entrant gets 0.9 input, an extension draw and any new contact injury, never stacked. Player selection follows root-reviewed effective-rating fallback. No early-bed healing, forced contact, extra policy, production state or invented display. Player's pre/after status, personal progress/assignment, favorite use, input, placement/payout, five rivals and their known states/choices, actual contact/participant rolls and recovery updates are retained. Tentative unresolved contact is not committed.

`follow-on-progression-analysis.mjs` is a predeclared descriptive per-seed report, not bootstrap precision: per run, finished/missing/unresolved denominators; favorite and injured/tired entries; wins/losses/payout/mean placement; point earning and first-use opportunities by duck; player/rival injury exposures and events; extension and recovery events. Report actual values for all eight paired seeds per comparison, including Guard's injury/availability effects. It validates shard/index/cell/seed and refuses missing/failure/malformed data. Output `progression-analysis.json` uses exclusive-create and includes seed units. Further read-only tables must preserve individual seed and denominator, not count paired cells as independent. A finite analysis job and resource bounds require explicit clearance.
