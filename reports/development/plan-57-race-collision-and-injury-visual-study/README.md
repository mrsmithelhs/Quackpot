# Collision and injury gallery

Open the local report gallery at `/Quackpot/reports/development/plan-57-race-collision-and-injury-visual-study/gallery/` on the existing local Vite server. The course, movement and event histories are authored presentation fixtures. They do not represent a simulation result or selected injury rule.

Use the scenario selector to compare contacts with no injury, either or both injuries, already-injured contact, wall impact and controls. The defaults are the no-injury contact scenario, angular burst, whitening off, attached bandage, normal lighting and motion enabled; the initial clock is at zero. Press Play or move the time slider to about 1.2 seconds to see the contact. Pause freezes the clock; Replay returns it to zero. Collision cue, whitening duration, injury marker, static mode and lighting are independent controls. The playback controls are keyboard-operable. Each section has its own clock: one is a six-duck race-size context and one is an enlarged two-duck comparison. An injury label and narrative remain independent of the collision cue.

For an initial comparison, try angular burst with whitening off, splash with 0.5 seconds whitening, and no shape with 1 second whitening. Compare attached bandage with label-side tab; these are suggestions for review, not owner selections. Static mode and the system reduced-motion preference use a non-pulsing participant symbol and text. The gallery has no audio or game-storage dependency.

See `source-context.md` for production versus stand-in boundaries, `consultation/provenance.md` for artwork provenance, `evidence/` for bounded checks, and `integration-brief.md` for the separate future gate.
