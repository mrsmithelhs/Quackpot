# Collision and injury gallery

Open the local report gallery at `/Quackpot/reports/development/plan-57-race-collision-and-injury-visual-study/gallery/` on the existing local Vite server. The course, movement and event histories are authored presentation fixtures. They do not represent a simulation result or selected injury rule.

The continuation is ready for **owner comparison**. Its defaults are no-injury contact, irregular burst A, one-second whitening, attached bandage, normal lighting and motion enabled; A is a candidate, not the owner's selected silhouette. Choose A, B or C under Collision cue, then press Play. The onset, expansion, breakup and settle buttons pause at particular frames; Pause, Replay and the time slider allow closer inspection. Each section has its own clock: one is a six-duck race-size context and one is an enlarged two-duck comparison. Collision and injury remain separate facts; select an injury scenario to see the attached marker.

The owner selected the one-second white return and attached bandage and requested irregular angular-burst variants; do not reopen those selections without owner direction. Static mode and the system reduced-motion preference use a fixed cue, non-pulsing participant symbol and text. The gallery has no audio or game-storage dependency. The isolated GPU-permitted captures were intact on the reported AMD renderer, while earlier `--disable-gpu` sequential captures lost duck layers. This is a configuration-specific evidence limit; actual behavior in the owner's headed browser and other devices is not established. See `progress.md` and `gpu-browser-proposal.md`.
