# Sonnet final-three concept archive

These eight standalone source SVGs and their eight renderer snapshots are static concept studies from the high-effort attempt described in `../../consultation/final-three-high-attempt.md`. The helper reported failure and the model's requested sheet read failed; a delayed sheet appeared afterward and was archived as found, without rerendering. This is not a successful self-inspection or evidence of continuous motion. Eight originals and eight snapshots passed independent strict inert XML/namespace/attribute checks; contents match after line-ending normalization, with both byte versions separately pinned in `hashes.txt`. All staged synthetic inputs and helper remained unchanged.
