# Shared independent creative consultation brief — draft

Give this same brief and the same two actual staged reference images to each consultant independently. The images show a layered-paper digital duck rig in a synthetic six-duck scene and a two-duck crop, not a genuinely enlarged duck. They do not depict accepted ability art or the actual full race track. Please read the supplied images; don't infer hidden implementation or gameplay from them.

Quackpot is a fictional duck-collecting browser game. We are studying automatic seeded race-ability cues. The duck may have accessories and a variety of body colors; its label remains upright when the body turns. In a six-entrant race the paper duck is roughly `clamp(2.5rem, 9%, 5.25rem)` wide. A cue must work at that scale, on turns, against water and under normal and dim drama lighting. Enlarged voluntary demonstrations for Duck-Dex inspection and choosing a racer may explain source, target, activation, active state and end more clearly, while keeping the race cue's identity. There is no compulsory animation. Static and text alternatives must carry equivalent participant and state information; avoid color-only distinctions.

Plan 55 is exploring actual multiple duck collisions, anticipatory avoidance influenced by risk and proximity abilities, and automatic activations. Exact mechanics, probability, duration, effects, targets and catalog are unresolved. Collision and possible persistent injury are separate subjects in Plan 57, whose current artwork is experimental. A cue must not imply guaranteed triggers, an injury, protection or performance that the actual event does not establish. In particular, the collision that precedes Bounce is real; Bounce is a subsequent possible activation, not an eraser. Crowd acceleration triggers independently of Draft. No new audio, full-scene flashing, strobing, obscured labels or forced result.

Candidates: Burst is an earlier whole-race speed-rating proxy with no flight event; Guard is a proposed injury-related response after a real collision; Wide Flap may push nearby ducks without directly injuring them; Dive submerges self temporarily; Flap Ahead flies above other ducks before landing; Draft follows a faster leader; Straight Shooter preserves heading after actual contact; Bounce rises above later ground traffic after actual contact and a successful activation; Quick Recovery shortens continuous lane recovery after actual contact; Crowd Surge accelerates self in a crowd; Steady Nerves moderates a self-generated wobble; Wake Break affects a close follower's acceleration. All are review candidates, not selected final mechanics. Air-to-air contact, landing, eligibility and state endings are open. Don't invent precise odds, timing or guarantees.

Propose a coordinated visual vocabulary for the full family, including two distinguishable directions each for Bounce and Crowd Surge and a readable combination where their events overlap. Suggest how to distinguish Bounce from Flap Ahead, Draft from Crowd Surge, Quick Recovery from Straight Shooter, Guard from injury, and Wide Flap from a collision. For each direction describe shape, paper-layer motion, local water/air accent, start/active/end, participants and limitations at actual race scale. Include no-trigger, cancellation and static/text alternatives, and explain how facing, accessories, colors and dim lighting affect readability. Identify overlap, consolidation or deferral candidates and any visual that promises more than the stated event. Separate memorable creative suggestions from assumptions. Do not select mechanics or claim these fixtures demonstrate balance. Provide concise prose and rough descriptions, not code or a final owner decision.

Output compactly: begin with a small shared vocabulary, then list all twelve candidates in a concise table. Give two distinct Bounce and two Crowd Surge treatments and one overlapping combination extra detail; use brief rows for the others. Finish with a short list of ambiguous promises and suggested merges or deferrals. Limit prose for each treatment to a few sentences. This is a request for compact output, not an enforceable token or spending cap.
