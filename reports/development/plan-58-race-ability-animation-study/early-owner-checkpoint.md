# Early owner checkpoint — Bounce and crowd acceleration

Open the report-local [ability gallery](http://localhost:5173/Quackpot/reports/development/plan-58-race-ability-animation-study/gallery/) while the existing local development server is available. This is an early comparison, not the finished twelve-candidate gallery or a production game. Each of the three sections has independent controls. Select a scene, choose treatments, then press Play or scrub Time; Replay starts again. Still mode and reduced-motion preference keep the state still and allow scrubbing. No game saves are involved.

## What to compare

- **Bounce: spring arc versus rebound pad.** Choose “Predicted threat, then Bounce” and switch the Bounce selector. Does either cue communicate a duck preparing and lifting clearly? Does the pad read as an unwanted physical platform or collectible? Is the arc too arrow-like? The illustration does not guarantee a collision would have occurred or been prevented.
- **Crowd acceleration: bow wedge versus paddle folds.** Choose “Crowd acceleration” and switch the Crowd selector. Which looks like a duck accelerating in a crowd at six-duck race size? Are the marks visible without looking like an impact, Draft or a guaranteed pass?
- **Combination.** Choose “Independent overlap” and vary both selectors. Does the active Bounce remain distinguishable from the separate crowd state? During the airborne portion, the gallery deliberately suppresses a water wake and retains the crowd state in text; this does not decide how future physics works.
- **Enlarged examples.** The Duck-Dex and racer-selection sections use genuinely larger ducks and optional replay to explore the same cues. Are they useful explanations, and do they feel connected to the race-scale version? The sections are report-local mock contexts, not game features.

The six-duck scene, positions, event timing, threat marker and contact ring are synthetic presentation fixtures. The contact control shows a separately authored actual A/B meeting; it is not evidence about production collisions or injury. The four Haiku SVGs are original, unselected candidate effects. The remaining ability descriptions below the gallery are text storyboards, not finished visual treatments. The Bounce trigger may be predictive; exact threat geometry, risk tolerance, air/contact and landing rules remain open in Plan55. No ability effects, probabilities, rarity or production integration are selected here.

## Evidence and limits

Initial captures and a focused label repair were reviewed by the lead and QP Orch. At 1024px viewport the race duck measured 84px wide and each enlarged duck 190px; at 390px viewport these were 40px and about 160px. The bounded checks cover the specified synthetic event states, seek/play/pause/replay, manual still, CDP-emulated reduced motion, selected facing transform, instance counts and narrow contact label separation. Browser cleanup observations are sampled only with retained profiles and uncertainty; physical devices, actual OS settings, screen-reader speech, real race behavior and learner experience are untested. See `evidence/lifecycle-assessment.md`, `evidence/terra-review.md`, and `progress.md` for caveats and failures.

Please choose, combine, revise or reject the two treatments in each family, and comment on the combination and enlarged explanation contexts. After your feedback, the remaining candidate visuals and final selection will continue within Plan58. Production integration remains separately gated.
