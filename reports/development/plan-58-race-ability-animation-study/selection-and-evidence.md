# Plan 58 — selected visual directions and evidence

This is the owner-reviewed **report-local study selection**, not a production ability catalog, final timing, mechanical contract or acceptance of a particular implementation. Eleven distinct ability directions were reviewed; their A/B fixture durations and duck positions are illustrative. QP Orch reviewed the consolidated report as a study delivery; the owner decides investigation closure. Selections are supported by `owner-feedback-01.md`, `owner-feedback-04.md` through `owner-feedback-12.md`, and the owner conversation; the individual historical reports remain provenance, not competing current mechanics.

## What was actually reviewed and checked

- Owners reviewed evolving live examples and selected the directions above. QP Orch independently viewed source-bound stills at early Bounce/crowd checkpoints, the 390/1024 Guard/Dive and later batch checkpoints, the alternatives/refinement captures, and Steady's ring/overlap/side/end captures. The truly enlarged Duck-Dex and racer-selection examples reuse one study renderer under different headings; they do **not** establish differentiated production layouts or content.
- Synthetic fixture/unit checks and finite isolated browser jobs verified specific event/control state, named participants, sampled headings/turn, effect geometry/layering, seek/pause/replay, multiple instance/teardown counts, manual still and CDP-emulated reduced-motion behavior. Selected examples include `evidence/airflow-03/`, `evidence/refinement-03/`, `evidence/final-three-01/`, `evidence/final-three-phases-01/` and `evidence/steady-combined-01/`. Each receipt binds its own capture-time sources; do not treat an earlier screenshot as a capture of a later modified source. Inspect `evidence/*-assessment.md` and individual receipts for failed attempts, exact coverage and repairs.
- Bounce/crowd simultaneous overlap has explicit fixture and checked captures; other independently overlapping ability combinations were not comprehensively demonstrated. The selected bandage is pinned and shown only in the authored Quick Recovery injury case. Plan57's selected collision animations are not fully mounted in this study; its generic contact marker is a placeholder.
- The gallery has finite event start/end, no-activation controls and neutral reset through seeking; it has **no authored race-finish/post-finish cancellation scenario** and no general cancellation protocol. Those are real integration design/validation gaps, not passed checks. Static mode and emulated reduced-motion were exercised; OS accessibility settings, screen-reader speech, physical devices, frame pacing and all color/accessory combinations were not established. A/B snapshots and selected owner playback do not prove every possible combination is legible.
- The single authorized read-only Terra-medium review occurred at the early checkpoint (`evidence/terra-review.md`); requested `gpt-6.1-terra`/medium, reviewer could not observe actual model/effort. Its findings were disposed and repaired with targeted evidence, but it is not a final full-catalog review. No second review is claimed.
- Sonnet and Flash both contributed independent early creative input; the requested drawing studies used Haiku/Sonnet. Later Sonnet-high sessions include one truncated-output failure and two local renderer failures, one with a command differing literally from the approved command. Safe artifacts and later independently viewed delayed sheets are recorded as qualified concept references, not successful model visual reviews. The final-three high session did not inspect its delayed sheet. No silent retry or production art adoption occurred.

## Recommendation and unresolved decisions

QP Orch reviewed this consolidation and agrees the selected study directions support owner consideration of **investigation closure**, with the gaps above explicitly carried into any future integration. They do not establish a release-ready feature. Require a separate bounded integration packet and ownership/semantic event handoff from the current Plan55/Plan57 work; do not import report artifacts at runtime. Any additional gallery work should be driven by an actual unresolved owner decision or a concrete reproducible issue, not a claim that these examples prove production mechanics. See `integration-brief.md` for pinned source seams and deferred requirements.
