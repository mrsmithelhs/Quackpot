# Integrated integer race prototype — frozen experiment contract

2026-11-05. Prepared before implementation under QP Orch's amended report-local clearance. This is an experiment, not an adopted numerical policy, production implementation, save contract, or claim of v1/native parity. Existing numeric modules/table/PRNG and both race resolvers are read-only. The runtime game must not import this directory.

## Identity and numeric model

Input has an explicit schema/rules/course/precision identity, nonempty bounded UTF-8 seed and race ID, one player and exactly six unique stable IDs and unique lane/row slots. All numerical fields are canonical bounded decimal integer strings, validated before draws; exactly the documented fields are permitted. Integer units are millionths for positions/velocities/rating and microseconds for time. Angle uses unsigned 2³² turns, trig uses pinned Q62 CORDIC, and intermediates are checked at signed 2¹²⁷−1. Signed multiplication/division rounds to nearest, ties to even. Stable ID ordering is code-unit order. Keyed `sfc32` outputs are unsigned 32-bit integers; rational thresholds are compared using integer products, without decimal-floating decisions. Diagnostic no-injury identity is distinct and not production-acceptable by default.

## Bounds and sampling

One tick is 50,000 integer µs, at most 800 ticks. Finite caps: 256 substeps and 16 correction passes per tick/boundary, 512 events including reserved active-effect closeouts, 801 trajectory samples, and 2 MiB canonical envelope. No truncation into success. Initial sample and each completed tick are retained; failure returns last validated boundary and factual partial crossings with no placements. Every committed state/time is an integer. Default maximum sampled relative course-map travel is 250,000 integer distance units; half-step sensitivity is 125,000. Positive µs step is bounded using conservative doubled summed speeds, with integer ceiling where needed and no minimum-step fabrication. A submicrosecond requirement is unresolved.

Course progress crossing is motion-only: truncate the integer step at the ceiling of the next crossing time, then determine crossings from the actual credited pre/post progress. Compare crossing fractions by checked integer cross multiplication. Order a strictly earlier crossing before sampled endpoint contact within the candidate step; at an exact endpoint tie contact precedes crossing. Recompute the remaining interval after any physical impulse. Fractional interpolation is evidence within the recorded integer step, never persistent state or a claim of exact continuous curved motion. Stable ID breaks exact rational ties. Finished entrants become ineligible for later contact and abilities. Corrections change a separately recorded offset and never count as progress; unsafe finish/corridor correction or ambiguous nonconvergence stops unresolved, without fabricated crossing.

**QP Orch atomicity amendment:** The whole integer-microsecond step is one atomic transaction. An interior crossing is a pending candidate fact: order it before endpoint contact and provisionally exclude that entrant, but publish the crossing, positions, integer clock, events, keyed counters and injuries only after the complete endpoint validates. If any endpoint contact or other guard fails, discard the entire candidate step, including its pending crossings, and retain the preceding validated integer checkpoint. Guard evidence may describe the discarded pending crossing only as non-authoritative. Previously committed crossings/injuries from earlier validated steps remain. Every retained crossing is at or before the retained clock and carries its integer-step/rational evidence; fractional state, unsafe checkpoints and forced crossings are prohibited.

Sampled pair contact is based on actual integer course-frame squared distance, with unit diameter. A discrete non-overlap margin of 1,000 position units is provisional physical study input, not a numerical error bound. Correction is bounded by the preceding sampled relative travel plus that margin, with finite wall-then-pair convergence and third-party/finish checks. A new pair episode needs both observed 1,200,000-unit separation and 500,000 µs; sustained constraint creates no new contact/impulse/roll. The same-boundary protected landing remains a contact when genuinely occupied and non-closing, with zero invented closing impulse. This sampled model may miss tangencies or tunneling; it does not establish continuous collision order.

**Landing-specific study amendment:** Only a pair actually occupied at a fresh airborne-to-landing transition may use a diameter-derived correction budget of one body diameter plus the margin per participant. The nominal separation is split symmetrically; bounded wall clipping and redistribution remain subject to the per-participant cap, corridor, third-party and no-finish-credit checks. Ordinary contacts retain their sampled-travel cap. Exact coincidence without a normal remains unresolved. Correction evidence records penetration, per-participant displacement, budget and pass count; the whole microstep and any events remain atomic. This is a discrete candidate, not a continuous 3-D or production model.

## Abilities and lifecycle

Bounce/Crowd use Plan59's provisional diagnostic values: 5-tick opportunity sampling, 75% keyed activation, two absent checks to rearm, 40-tick post-effect cooldown; Bounce rise/air/landing 2/10/2 ticks and complete participant protection through landing; Crowd ten ticks at +1,500,000 units/s² toward the inherited target with two grounded unfinished neighbors within 4 spatial and 8 course units. Injury is 5% steady or 10% sprint, independent participant-keyed per eligible new real contact, no healing or stacking; 0.9 multiplies rating before the 8.25+0.5 affine conversion. Existing injury is preserved. Ability closeout is emitted exactly once at committed finish/unresolved boundary, with capacity reserved.

## Serialization and comparisons

Result uses sorted canonical JSON keys, integer fields encoded as canonical decimal strings, bounded size and a versioned integrity digest over the complete canonical payload. Restore checks exact schema/fields, integer syntax/range, sizes and digest; unknown fields and tested tampering are rejected. Digest detects modification relative to a trusted digest; it is not authentication. Outputs are detached/immutable. No browser or game save policy is implied.

The report-boundary envelope now uses the same asynchronous WebCrypto SHA-256 adapter in Node and a secure browser context; the race kernel remains synchronous and digest/version/canonical bytes are unchanged. This is a portability candidate, not browser validation. Contact-depth guard evidence records non-authoritative candidate positions and the retained checkpoint without changing correction limits or committing a failed step. Only the two previously failed mixed inputs are reproduced for diagnosis; their historical records remain intact.

## Predeclared finite runs

Two serial bounded jobs under lane B, each after fresh normal-host ≥8 GiB/process preflight and existing 90 s/1 GiB heap/2 GiB sampled-tree launcher: (1) one focused test file including direct controls, time ordering, contact, landing, rollback, caps and codec checks; (2) `compare.mjs` on exactly `pair-0`, `pair-1`, `pair-2` × control/mixed, plus half-step controls for `pair-0`/`pair-1` and the same three no-injury/no-ability diagnostic identities. Each result is repeated in the same runtime and checked for canonical repeat/restore/tamper; no seed filtering or rerun for favorable result. Full records and measured per-result bytes and elapsed time are saved exclusively. If a job hits resource or safety bounds, preserve the receipt and stop/report instead of weakening the contract. Chromium requires a separate gate.

Before execution, the second job is predeclared as three serial per-seed shards rather than one potentially long BigInt job: `pair-0` contains control, mixed, half-step and diagnostic; `pair-1` the same; `pair-2` contains control, mixed and diagnostic. Each shard has the same independent preflight and launcher envelope and retains every outcome. This changes neither inputs nor acceptance criteria.

The discrete integrator and integer controller are experimental candidates. They may differ from native through quantization, integer steering/geometry, left-wall reflection, explicit ties, multi-contact and independent random streams. Fixed controls can expose differences; they cannot prove portability, balance or historical compatibility.
