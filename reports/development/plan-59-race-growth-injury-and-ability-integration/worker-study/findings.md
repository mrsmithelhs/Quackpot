## Representations and measurements

Compact `race-v2-compact/v1` records the full validated race input, selected committed facts (status, crossing facts, injury state, contact/injury/ability/crossing events), and the full canonical envelope SHA-256 digest. Reconstruction reruns the pinned kernel from the recorded input and compares the complete canonical result digest and selected facts. The digest is **not authentication**: a self-consistent replacement input, facts and digest cannot be distinguished here from authentic history. The future engine/replay must independently reconstruct the authoritative input and validate committed state/log effects and identities. Compact JSON is capped at 128 KiB per record.

The alternative `race-v2-gzip/v1` records the complete canonical envelope compressed with browser-native gzip and base64. It caps compressed bytes at 512 KiB, bounds declared and streamed decompressed output to 2 MiB, requires exact decoded length and UTF-8, structurally decodes, then reruns the kernel and compares the full envelope. Truncated/invalid gzip and base64, length lies, excessive compressed input and a decompression expansion fixture were rejected. These are per-record study bounds, not an adopted global quota. Neither representation silently drops evidence.

For the one fixed pair-0 mixed result, Chromium measured 286,790 canonical bytes, 9,327 compact-record JSON bytes, and 74,663 gzip-record JSON bytes, including 74,596 base64 characters. On one desktop and one narrow viewport, real module-worker preparation was 1.35–1.37 s; compact semantic reconstruction 1.37–1.45 s; compressed semantic reconstruction 1.33–1.36 s. Times are individual observations on this host, not multi-device or long-history benchmarks. Prefer compact for further evaluation: it retains authoritative input and committed facts with much smaller storage, but the measured ~1.4 s per historical race makes asynchronous staged replay and progress/cancellation necessary. Extrapolating that single-run time serially to 60 races suggests roughly 80–87 s, not a measured 60-race workload or acceptable boot guarantee.

The existing synthetic historical **v1/schema-5** Plan52 `session-panel-witness-attempt3` has 21,519 bytes before-return (61 log events) and 183,273 bytes at race-committed (87 events). UTF-8 JSON reserialization of the event arrays measured 14,932 and 22,151 bytes respectively. The 161,754-byte whole-save delta and 7,219-byte log delta include intervening v1 activity; they are not a measured v2 per-race log increase. ## Worker and lifecycle evidence

The new `race-worker.mjs` is a static local module worker; it prepares a candidate and never writes state, rewards, RNG or storage. `worker-client.mjs` creates an owned worker per operation, checks run/operation/revision/log/state identity on response and again at one-time claim, terminates on success/cancel/error, rejects diagnostic rules and ignores late/duplicate replies. The future engine must supply a complete epoch and revalidate actual state and resources synchronously at publication; client checks alone do not implement that transaction. The result decoder is structural, and the semantic reconstruction is a comparison with stored input, not authentication or full game replay.

Focused bounded run `../persistence-worker-focused-02.txt`: four tests passed, including exact compact/gzip re-resolution, altered input/facts/digest, malformed/truncated/oversized data and expansion guard, plus simulated worker lifecycle cases and a report-only day-order witness. The day witness persists a study-only `day-order-study/v1` object containing before/entry day and before/entry recovery counters, and demonstrates elapsed-day recovery before entry, a new two-day injury after entry without immediate decrement, entry-injury-only extension eligibility, and cancellation without mutation. It is not an actual scheduler/save integration, random extension test, or a selected production event schema.

The one isolated browser batch used Chrome 157.0.8103.38 and Node 26.7.0. On 1024×768 and 390×844 fixture viewports it verified exact frozen bytes, real worker resolve/restore/termination and cancel, no horizontal overflow, three programmatic Settings/exit-equivalent clicks during resolve and both restores, and timer activity (maximum observed 28.8/32.2 ms). Stale replies, duplicate completions, error and startup failure used harness fakes, not actual engine/Settings/Uninstall; no live save/profile was written. `browser-attempt-01/browser-summary.json` and per-viewport JSON preserve outcomes and pins.

Artifact checks passed, but strict cleanup **did not**: sampled-only process coverage left `uncertainty=true`, `treeTerminationConfirmed=false`, and a retained isolated profile. The observed browser root/descendants and loopback server exited with no identified residual; that is not proof of total cleanup. The launcher therefore exited 1 as required. No browser rerun was attempted.

## Proposed later shared-file ownership and mounting sequence

These are next-turn tasks, not writes authorized here. Root must refresh all shared pins and serialize Plan52/63 ownership first.

1. **Engine/progression owner:** `game-engine.mjs` and a narrowly owned race growth/injury module. On a draft, admit the schedule and apply elapsed-day recovery first, freeze exact entry facts, launch the worker, and retain run/operation/revision/log/state/resource identities. Before publishing, synchronously revalidate the whole epoch, selected duck, resources, schedule, trial and immutable input. Publish one draft containing schedule, validated result, retained injuries, completed-only payout/trial/growth/extension and log; cancellation or stale completion publishes nothing. Explicitly reject duplicate completion and diagnostic rules.
2. **Recovery inventory:** at the current read pin, `#publishTransaction` call sites include asset sale (line 225), ordinary schedule (439), social (460), work story (480), activity story (521), staged-next-play (622), pull (776), race (816) and life bundle (855). Repin and audit any direct mutation or new call site before implementation. A shared draft hook compares actual old/new schedule day, applies recovery exactly once per elapsed day, and records replayable evidence; race admission must stage that hook before entry snapshot, never decrement a newly incurred post-entry injury for an earlier day.
3. **Storage/replay owner:** `storage.mjs` currently synchronously validates in `validateSave`, `saveGame`, `loadGame` and unsupported-save replacement, with `restoreRaceResolution` at lines 247/507 and a v1 combined-resolution cap at 552. `life-replay.mjs` has `validateFiveSlotTransactionHistory` and synchronous `validateSeededRaceHistory`; it enforces race-choice/outcome adjacency. Design a schema-6 version dispatch and bounded asynchronous reconstruction queue, independently compare each result and committed effects to authoritative history, preserve adjacency or review an explicit replacement, and distinguish corrupt from unsupported data. Preserve existing schema-5 review saves; do not rewrite, erase or migrate them silently.
4. **Boot/client owner:** `main.js` calls synchronous `loadGame` around line 140, `GameEngine.fromEnvelope` around 199 and `saveGame` in autosave and later paths. A staged async boot must retain Settings/exit access, progress/cancel behavior and a non-destructive failure path while waiting for semantic validation; no worker message independently commits or saves. Recheck cancellation at navigation, new run, reload, retention and uninstall boundaries. Fresh owner-profile behavior, multi-race reconstruction throughput, quota, unsupported environments, replay mismatches and duplicate callbacks need dedicated evidence.
5. **UI/checkpoint owner, later turn:** race presentation and authored effects stay outside this study. Assign UI and checkpoint files only after engine/storage ownership and pins are set; source-bound real Settings/exit, lifecycle, replay, recovery and Receipt evidence remains required.

No packet completion, deployment, push, or shared source change follows from this study. Review is orchestrator-gate-only; no additional provider consultation was run.
