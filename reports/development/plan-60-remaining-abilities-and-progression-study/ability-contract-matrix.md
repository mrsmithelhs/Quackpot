# Nine abilities: proposed contract matrix

These are investigation contracts, not approved numerical rules or production-ready interfaces. The owner chose to explore all nine separately and clarified that **every activation is temporary within a race**, possibly with different durations/cooldowns and later duration upgrades. A learned ability may persist between races; an active effect does not. A candidate uses a named source duck, race/entrant identity, ability version, eligibility facts, independently seeded opportunity/activation and traceable start/end/cancel; participant facts come from the mechanical resolver, not animation. No column assumes that existing production exposes these inputs. Exact duration, cooldown, rearm, activation chance, probability and magnitude remain unresolved for every row. No animation independently causes or proves an effect.

| Candidate / selected Plan58 visual | Candidate input and source → target | Active effect and termination / cancellation | Key unresolved contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guard / B** | Source predicts likely contact and braces; target is itself; actual contact independently identifies all participants. | While braced, reduce the source's injury chance on actual contact; window ends, cooldown follows; cancel on its own finish. A false prediction can waste the window. | Prediction geometry/cadence, activation and boundary order, partial reduction, active rearm; protect neither an earlier injury nor the other participant. |
| **Quick Recovery / A** | Actual contact/deflection and source's recovery episode → itself. | Temporarily assist return toward course; retain actual impulse and injury; stop on duration, recovery end or own finish, then cooldown. | Controller seam, gain/cap, overlapping contacts and steering priorities; no heal or teleport. |
| **Dive / revised A with locator** | Traffic-risk opportunity, eligible source → itself; actual underwater/ground/air participants remain named. | Temporary slower underwater interval with candidate grounded-contact immunity; return at real position, then cooldown; own finish ends ability under a future explicit crossing rule. | Activation and layer boundaries, occupied resurfacing, underwater crossing eligibility, air interactions and readable location. |
| **Wide Flap / A wing sweep** | Nearby named entrant and eligible source → one target as a simple rival; multiple-target policy open. | Temporary lateral influence; end at window/target loss/finish and cooldown. The flap itself is not injury or contact; later real contact is separate. | Target selection/count, impulse versus steering, wall/corridor handling, effect attribution, simultaneous targets. |
| **Flap Ahead / revised A with arcs and shadow** | Eligible passing opportunity, source → itself; relevant airborne contacts and landing participants named separately. | Temporary airborne interval, candidate ground separation and landing slowdown; end on actual landing or own finish, then cooldown. | Air collision, occupied landing and injury independence, airborne crossing, acceleration/cap and landing placement. |
| **Draft / A layered wake** | Eligible nearby faster leader B and follower source A → A, with B named. | Temporary follower assistance while relationship exists; end on window, lost target or either finish, then cooldown. | Geometry, speed/cap exception, following behavior, target-switch/rearm and interaction with crowd acceleration. |
| **Straight Shooter / A heading rails** | Actual contact and source's heading response → itself. | Temporarily resist heading deflection without removing impulse or injury; end on window, controller completion or own finish, then cooldown. | Actual controller priority, trade-off with obstacle avoidance, measurable distinction from Quick Recovery; possible merger is for owner review. |
| **Steady Nerves / revised A ring and accents** | Actual self-generated weave opportunity → source. | Temporarily reduce eligible self-wobble; end on window, event completion or own finish, then cooldown; does not suppress collision/injury. | Which self-weaves qualify, magnitude, risk/stance relationship and visibility of a real effect. |
| **Wake Break / B counter-eddies** | Eligible source and actual close follower B → B. | Temporarily reduce B's acceleration without direct push/injury; end on window, relationship loss or either finish, then cooldown. | Geometry, target selection/switch, acceleration floor, interaction with Draft and truthful attribution. |

In all rows, finish means a genuinely resolved individual crossing in a future actual resolver, not a gallery timer or the synthetic study's supplied witness. The exact policy for pending effects, airborne/submerged crossing and simultaneous finish/contact remains for a technical decision. Cooldown persistence and pause/replay reconstruction require a future versioned lifecycle contract; no cross-race active protection is implied. Existing owner-selected Bounce protects fully through its active window including landing; an occupied landing remains real contact and the other entrant retains independent injury risk. Independent crowd acceleration is not Draft-dependent. Current injury is raceable, applies its rating penalty and recovers over actual day changes; it is not a short-lived contact animation.
