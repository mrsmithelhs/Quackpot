# Baseline exposure protocol — proposed, before execution

This protocol is a proposed next batch, awaiting QP Orch's checkpoint and renewed compute-lane assignment. No measurements are recorded here. It is not a model of any new ability or injury. A baseline trajectory cannot show the result of an intervention, altered stance caused by an ability, a safe flight/dive route or an injury prevented.

## Opportunity definitions and observations

For P only, count actual `duck-contact` events whose `participants` include P, by unique event ID, and actual `weave-turn` event IDs for P. These are existing resolver events, not injury or ability activations. Remove duplicate ticks if any (keep last frame). At each left endpoint compare P with rivals that have not yet individually crossed. A candidate leader is a rival whose progress is strictly ahead by <=8 course units, Euclidean center distance <=4 units and speed strictly greater than P; a candidate follower reverses ahead/behind and uses no speed criterion. A broad episode starts on false-to-true for any candidate; choose the nearest qualifying participant by Euclidean distance then lexicographic ID. A named-target episode also starts when that ID changes, and a switch while continuously eligible is counted separately. The left-endpoint state represents the interval to the next frame, clipped to P's and that selected target's actual fractional crossing times; no interval follows the final frame. Use the result's observed `dt` to convert ticks. This sampled estimate can miss mid-interval entry/exit or a different eligible target for a fractional remainder; do not imply exact physics. If P lacks a finish, retain exposure up to the committed last frame as censored and report its status. No crossed/coasting entrant is eligible. These coarse geometric proxies are not selected ability triggers and cannot prove wake physics, ability activation or collision avoidance.

For each seed/cell retain run status/reason, horizon, P finish time and place when present, per-P contact and self-weave counts, eligible leader/follower intervals, broad episodes, named-target episodes and switches, sampled seconds, and witness identifiers/times. Aggregate distributions including observed zeros, median, range and per-cell fraction with at least one observed opportunity out of all 32. Unfinished observations are right-censored: a zero in the observed portion is not evidence of no opportunity in its unobserved tail. Report paired per-seed cell differences, direction counts and quantiles; 32 paired seeds are exploratory and do not establish general balance. Missing, incomplete or guard-stopped races remain visible and are not treated as successful finishes or missing-at-random.

## Rival questions and limits

1. Healthy P Guard has meaningful exposure versus almost none: use actual P contacts. Predeclared descriptive flag: fewer than 4 of 32 observed cases with a P contact in a particular cell means this sample cannot support a broad typical-use claim; 4 or more is evidence of occurrence only, not value. Compare steady/sprint counts separately without assuming a direction. These cells are equal P or weaker P, not a stronger favorite; they do not characterize every healthy favorite.
2. Steady Nerves has eligible events versus none: use actual P weave-turn count. Fewer than 4 of 32 cases with a weave in a cell flags insufficient observed exposure for broad typical-use claims. This is not an automatic design rejection.
3. Draft and Wake Break are situationally distinct versus similarly exposed: compare paired leader/follower episodes and seconds. A median paired difference within ±0.25 seconds and at least 24 of 32 seeds with absolute difference <=0.5 seconds is a descriptive near-equivalence flag for this proxy only, not proof of equal ability effects. Conversely larger differences do not prove player value.

Injury is absent from the baseline; do not infer Guard prevented injuries. An independent separately preregistered synthetic shared-roll demonstration may use 5% steady and 10% sprint candidate per-contact injury probabilities and a Guard multiplier of 0.5 as **illustrative diagnostic assumptions**, with explicitly independent keyed rolls, healthy/injured entry distinctions, and no claim that these are chosen odds or a measured effect on placement. Its full case table and exact seeded draw method require a separate written protocol before executing it. Authored semantic cases and numerical tolerances also require their exact preregistration before execution; the <=60-case maximum is a ceiling, not permission to improvise outcomes.
