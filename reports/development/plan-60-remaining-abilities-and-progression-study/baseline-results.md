# Existing-race exposure screen

This is a preregistered, no-ability/no-injury **baseline**. It does not measure Guard, Draft, Wake Break or Steady Nerves effects, future multiple-contact races, injury prevention, a fair balance, or actual player choices. The owner has selected no numerical ability settings from it. All 128 cases finished: 32 seeds in each cell, with P rating 10 and five opponents 10 or 14; only P's stance changes. Cell order below is equal/steady, equal/sprint, stronger-field/steady, stronger-field/sprint. Fixed seed000 witnesses and all per-case outputs are in the five JSONL receipts.

**Major structural limitation:** current production `resolveOneDuckContact` returns once *any* prior duck-contact event exists. Thus this baseline can record at most one duck-to-duck contact in the entire race. In these inputs P was a participant for the same 14 seeds in all four cells. This is not evidence about Guard's future exposure in Plan59's selected multi-contact direction, nor that risk or injury rates are equal across stances. Contact was absent in the other 18 observed runs in each cell; all results finished, so no unfinished tails were censored in this particular sample.

Weave opportunities occurred in all cases, meeting the preregistered exposure-only screen; that does not prove an effect would be useful or visible. The leader/follower proximity definitions are crude geometry, not selected abilities. The leader-minus-follower per-seed median was -15.37, -15.23, -7.46 and -6.59 seconds respectively; 1, 0, 1 and 0 of 32 paired differences fell within ±0.5 seconds, so the preregistered near-equivalence flag did not hold for this proxy. In 32/32, 32/32, 31/32 and 31/32 seeds follower-proxy time was larger. This describes opportunity under these cells, not effectiveness; starting positions and the baseline race model may contribute. Cumulative proximity seconds do not establish one persistent, activatable relationship. Continuous broad proximity is not necessarily a stable target: mean nearest-follower switches per race were 6.16, 5.75, 6.59 and 6.81, respectively.

For matched seed sprint-minus-steady, recorded P-contact count was unchanged in all 32 pairs in either field; self-weave count changed in 5/32 equal and 4/32 stronger-field pairs (range -1 to +1). Leader-proxy duration differences had medians 0.00 and -0.17 seconds (ranges -6.35 to +11.16 and -4.92 to +3.70), and follower-proxy differences medians -0.18 and -0.14 seconds (ranges -6.10 to +2.76 and -6.70 to +3.80). The fixed raceId aligns keyed identities where opportunities match, but changes in timing and opportunities make these paired cases neither proof of matching all random draws nor a causal estimate of an ability. Neither cell represents a stronger favorite against its field.

The included four-case pilot took 1.07 s, followed by 28/32/32/32-case jobs taking 3.71/4.26/4.24/4.21 s; all exited 0, with no reported stop or cleanup note. Peak sampled owned-tree private memory was at most 116,285,440 bytes, and fresh preflights reported at least 14,649,688 KiB available. Three targeted harness tests passed, including actual-event counting, individual crossing clipping and target switches. Sampling can miss mid-interval changes, and clipping to the selected target's fractional finish can omit another eligible target during that fractional remainder. The initial PowerShell aggregation had a parser error before producing a result; its corrected read-only command yielded the statistics above. No race was repeated for this repair.
