# Candidate purposes and working contracts — owner checkpoint

Owner in this chat selected separate purposes for Guard and Quick Recovery, separate exploration of Dive and Flap Ahead, Draft and Wake Break, Straight Shooter, Wide Flap and Steady Nerves; later points should offer both improving an ability and learning another; rarity study should favor greater eventual potential. These selections are directions for investigation, not approval of exact triggers, numbers, slots, rarity mapping, finish policy, costs or production implementation. They do not require all nine to survive final recommendation.

Owner clarification: **every ability is a temporary, limited-time capability within a race**. Different abilities may have different active durations and cooldowns, and duck levels might increase durations. Learning or assigning an ability persists, but an activation and its protective effect do not carry across races. A resulting injury may persist across actual game-day changes independently. This clarification selects no exact cooldown, duration, level curve or between-race state.

Each row is a **candidate**, not an adopted mechanic. Automatically seeded activation is conditional on an actual eligible opportunity. For all: named participants, finite active window, one latched opportunity and rearm after it ends, explicit cancellation at the duck's genuine finish; no activation fabricates contact, injury, finish, reward or an avoided collision. Times, activation probabilities, cooldown and collision priorities remain illustrative and must be versioned before any future integration. Interactions are proposed rather than established.

| Candidate | Player purpose and opportunity | Candidate effect, limit and failure |
|---|---|---|
| Guard | Temporarily reduce a favorite's injury risk during a race when real contact occurs. | Owner selects visibly bracing **before likely contact**. A successful predictive automatic activation opens a finite protection window then a cooldown; eligible real contact inside the window receives a reduced independent injury probability, without erasing impulse or the other's risk. A prediction may be wrong; the brace neither proves nor creates a contact. A contact before activation or outside the window uses ordinary injury eligibility, and no completed injury is retroactively erased. Prediction geometry, same-tick ordering, window, cooldown and reduction remain open. Unlike full active-window Bounce protection it is partial/probabilistic, does not heal, and ends within the race. |
| Quick Recovery | Regain course after a bump. | Following real deflection, shorten controller return to its assigned lane without teleporting or changing injury eligibility or its rating penalty. The path may still encounter traffic; unfinished recovery ends at actual finish. |
| Straight Shooter | Hold heading through a bump. | After real contact, temporarily damp heading deviation while retaining the impulse and injury eligibility; holding the line can delay avoidance or recentering. Compare actual path and recovery with Quick Recovery; merge/defer if the distinction fails in meaningful cases. |
| Dive | Trade speed for a protected underwater interval. | In eligible traffic submerge, move more slowly, and ignore grounded contact during the underwater window; surface at the actual position. Define real occupied-surface contact and independent risk rather than teleport. Finish is a real line crossing if eligible under a future explicit underwater crossing contract; do not force surfacing merely to manufacture a finish. |
| Wide Flap | Make room nearby. | A named nearby grounded duck receives bounded lateral steering displacement; flap is not itself contact or injury. Subsequent actual collision can injure either duck. No target means no effect; caps, wall behavior, multiple targets and source attribution need design. |
| Flap Ahead | Pass grounded traffic by a brief flight, paying a landing cost. | Eligible traffic and activation produce an airborne interval; ground ducks are not airborne contacts, air-to-air contacts remain possible. Landing slowdown and occupied landing are real; protection is not inherited from Bounce. Genuine crossing may occur in air under an explicitly defined future crossing contract. |
| Draft | Gain a positioning benefit from a specific leader. | Eligible nearby faster leader ahead yields bounded acceleration/target-speed assistance; terminate when relationship fails or either duck finishes. Exposure is not guaranteed; whether any cap exception or reduced contact risk is warranted is unresolved. |
| Steady Nerves | Moderate self-generated wobble. | On an actual eligible self-weave, reduce its angular magnitude, not contact response or injury. No weave means no effect; earlier arrival is not guaranteed. |
| Wake Break | Defend position from a specific nearby follower. | During an eligible close-follow relationship, impose bounded reduction to the follower's acceleration, not a push, contact or injury. Stop when relationship fails or either finishes; follower identity and truthful cue are essential. |

Owner-selected Bounce has full protection during its active window including landing, but remains able to contact and expose another participant to independent injury; crowd acceleration is independently proximity-triggered, not necessarily a rear-rank effect. The nine candidates must coexist with these constraints and their exact combinations remain open. Selected Plan58 animation directions are visual references only; event admission, static equivalents and lifecycle are future work.

## Progression alternatives to compare

Preserve first point at four, +2 per completed entered-duck race and +1 top three, bank/overflow, no life slot and no repeated cost assumption. Later choices are not yet specified. Compare an illustrative equal-cost point buying either (A) another separate ability or (B) a bounded enhancement of an already owned ability; separately compare a small active loadout with all learned abilities active. A high-rarity ceiling could mean one more eventual active slot, one extra upgrade tier, or an exclusive breadth ceiling; these are distinct alternatives, not cumulative grants. Include equal-ceiling/head-start as a rival. All numbers used for traces are explicit examples, not selections; test whether a common favorite has meaningful choices even if its ceiling is lower, while a rare advantage is tangible. Show one-duck loss, nonspending, repeated favorite and rotating-roster paths; no shared progress, healing, Gem accelerator or event is assumed.
