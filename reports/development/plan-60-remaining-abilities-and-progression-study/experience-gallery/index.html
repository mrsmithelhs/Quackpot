The comparison

Contact can send the duck off its line; its ordinary steering brings it back.

Guard

Brace before contact

During the brace, a real collision has a lower injury chance—not guaranteed protection. It does not heal an injury.

Quick Recovery

Return after contact

After a bump, the duck returns toward its course sooner. The collision and injury chance still matter.

Straight Shooter

Hold heading through contact

The duck holds its heading more firmly. Reduced ability to steer around traffic is a hypothetical trade-off to investigate.

What to look for

Guard changes injury risk, Quick Recovery changes the return after a bump, and Straight Shooter changes the response through it. In open water, none has a bump to answer. A brace may expire unused.
