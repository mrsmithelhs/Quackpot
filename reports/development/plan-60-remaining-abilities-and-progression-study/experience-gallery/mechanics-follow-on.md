# Proposed bounded mechanics follow-on

The owner considers Guard, Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter reasonable enough to proceed as distinct candidates. Guard and Quick Recovery remain the prioritized group; retain Straight Shooter as a comparator. The gallery is qualitative, not a choice of numerical rules. This is a proposal for a separately scoped study, not authority to run it or change production.

## Candidate alternatives and falsifiers

| Candidate | Diagnostic alternatives to compare | Evidence that would challenge the concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guard | Predict likely contact using a short forward traffic envelope; compare conservative and broader envelopes with fixed finite brace windows, partial injury-risk multipliers and cooldowns. Do not activate retroactively on a collision. | Braces mostly expire without an eligible real contact, or making opportunities common requires prediction so broad that it is almost always active. Too little or too much conditional protection also challenges a particular parameter, not the purpose itself. |
| Quick Recovery | Trigger after eligible real contact; compare two finite strengths or durations of assistance back toward the pre-contact course while retaining the real impulse and injury roll. | The duck does not return detectably sooner in matched episodes, recovery is unstable or overshoots, or the benefit is only visible after unrealistic disruption. It never heals. |
| Straight Shooter | Activate around an eligible contact and compare bounded heading resistance with a shorter or longer window; investigate reduced steering authority as a hypothetical trade-off, not a settled rule. | It is indistinguishable from Quick Recovery in held-heading and return timing, negates the real collision, or its traffic trade-off makes it consistently unhelpful. Do not infer player preference from a numeric difference alone. |

All variants need finite race-only windows and cooldowns. Diagnostic values should be fixed in advance and clearly labeled as temporary; compare modest alternatives rather than search for a favorable result. Owner choices needed before production include triggers and opportunity rules, active duration and cooldown, effect strength and caps, interruption/stacking and the Straight Shooter trade-off. Later level effects and rarity/capacity remain separate progression decisions. These experiments do not select any of them.

## Minimum informative investigation

1. Pin a stable race kernel or report-local adapter and its source closure. Predeclare a small set of controlled contact geometries plus fixed natural seeds and a small healthy favorite field. Run a no-ability control and each candidate with matched starting states and random draws. Record actual swept contacts, participant, timing, heading, course deviation, recovery completion/censoring, activations, cooldown, eligibility and injury draws. A shared seed does not guarantee matched later contacts; restrict paired injury claims to the same actual contact and draw.
2. For Guard, report predicted windows, expired unused braces, actual eligible contacts during a window and resulting conditional injury-roll changes; separately report no-contact cases. Existing healthy screening supplied only 9/4/1/1 protected rolls across its four cells, so three cells were sparse. First verify real controller-reachable contact and event order, rather than assuming the supplied-fraction tests demonstrate natural opportunity. Preserve genuine injury and entry-injury rules, including no healing or new roll where prohibited.
3. For Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter, use the same predeclared reachable contact cases to measure heading immediately through the bump, lateral displacement and return over time, including unfinished recoveries. Include a traffic case to investigate the hypothesized steering cost and a no-contact case. Compare like episodes before interpreting any pooled median. Recorded numerical path differences and the gallery establish possibilities, not measured player legibility or power.
4. Stop for a separate owner-facing presentation decision if mechanically distinct results warrant one. The inherited adapter's finish-first handling remains an approximation; the geometry diagnostic did not prove a naturally reachable missed pre-finish contact. Do not use this study to certify contact/finish ordering, repeated contacts, R2 assistance interaction, or full production lifecycle without explicitly covering them.

An initial proposed allocation is **one source-inspection and protocol review, up to four short serial diagnostic jobs, and one predeclared small paired screen** under the project’s current bounded launcher and fresh host-memory preflight. Fix exact case count, time/memory ceilings, stopping rules and browser needs with QP Orch before execution. No provider or browser run is assumed. If the stable seam lacks required event detail or the natural cases cannot expose the question, stop and report the gap rather than enlarge the screen or tune toward a result.
