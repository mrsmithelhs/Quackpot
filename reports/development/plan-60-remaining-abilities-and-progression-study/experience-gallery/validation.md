# Owner comparison gallery: scope and validation

## Selected, context and placeholders

The owner's selected purposes are pre-contact temporary Guard bracing with partial injury-risk protection, post-contact Quick Recovery without healing, and Straight Shooter as a separate heading-retention comparison. All abilities have finite race-only windows and cooldowns. The priority of Guard and Quick Recovery is selected; exact triggers, timing, magnitude and Straight Shooter's traffic trade-off remain open. The gallery is a qualitative authored schematic, not a resolver, a measured replay, a physics model, production art or a balance proposal. Positions, motion and window/cooldown timing are illustrative placeholders. Open water is a no-bump example in which Guard's illustrative brace expires unused; this does not claim the eventual trigger will fire there. No first-point, Bounce, crowd acceleration, injury, progression or Plan59 rule has been changed. The existing recorded-path preview remains separately linked.

The page is local HTML, CSS and modules with inline schematic SVG. It has no storage, production boot, third-party assets or runtime network calls. It starts paused, uses one shared clock, provides play/pause, seek, reset, a situation selector, textual ability descriptions and state, and responsive panels. These are study controls, not accessibility certification. The source manifest `source-pins.txt` pins the final files; browser receipt additionally pins the capture dependencies. No provider calls or new race simulations were made.
