# Guard, Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter: follow-up recommendation

The study provides a stronger basis to **continue developing the three concepts**, but not to select production constants or rank their power. Keep the owner's priority of Guard and Quick Recovery, and retain Straight Shooter as a comparator until an actual race presentation or a more discriminating controller comparison shows whether its different line is meaningful to a player. No merge, deferral or production integration is selected by this report.

The report-local adapter can produce a real numerical difference between Quick Recovery's and Straight Shooter's paths after an authored swept contact while retaining that contact's recorded impulse and injury rolls. In the small natural screen, completed-only recovery medians often favored the Quick Recovery candidate, but the sets of episodes and their completion rates differed across arms. Those medians are **not** matched time-saved estimates. A fixed full-race seed also had differing paths; neither result proves a player can see or understand the distinction.

Guard's purpose is reducing the risk from a real injury, not necessarily improving same-race placement. In the healthy screen the candidate had only one eligible protected injury roll in each favorite-duck cell; three of the four cells had fewer than eight protected rolls. Two matched contact/draw comparisons across the two sprint cells had an injury outcome in the no-ability control but not the Guard arm, with no matched opposite outcome. Those are sparse conditional observations, not a population rate, causal estimate for unmatched contacts, or evidence to dismiss Guard. Favorite placements were the same as control in this sample, which can reflect field and placement ceilings as well as genuinely small effects. In the injured-entry screen Guard did not heal or generate a new P injury roll, and matched recorded outcomes were unchanged; that is a no-healing control, not a fair test of Guard's protective value.

The study compared 16 fixed healthy seeds per configuration and eight injured-entry seeds in an equal field, with the owner's existing injury and progression boundaries held conceptually separate. Cases all finished. A seed match does not make later contacts identical after a controller change; injury outcome comparisons are restricted to matching actual contact ID, entrant key and draw. The data do not measure recovery-day extensions, long-run roster viability, actual audiovisual presentation, accessibility, real-device behavior or whole-game balance. The candidate rates, .5-second window, 2-second cooldown and effect magnitudes are diagnostics only.

## Recommended next decision and evidence

1. **Before choosing a merger or final ability numbers**, consider a small owner-visible, report-local comparison of one or two **predeclared existing recorded contact witnesses** for Quick Recovery versus Straight Shooter, showing the actual recorded paths, contact, activation, sampled timing and a no-ability control. The page should make clear when later contacts cease to match, provide a static/text equivalent and never imply the simplified replay is the production race. It needs a separate scoped preview/capture proposal and root resource clearance; no browser work is authorized by this recommendation. If the retained seed000 data lack enough event detail to explain an honest comparison, say so and do not fabricate frames.
2. **Before claiming Guard is balanced or ineffective**, collect independently planned evidence with a healthy favorite and actual protected contact exposure, or let a future stable race kernel expose that evidence. Do not filter for favorable seeds or tune the predictor until it appears to work. Wider activation, injury and cooldown parameters are owner/design choices, not facts discovered by this sample.
3. **Before production adoption**, resolve the stable Plan59 race seam, event/versioning and save/injury transaction ownership; validate actual contact/finish ordering, injury caps/slowdown, controller/assistance overlap, natural repeated-contact interruption, and real production presentation. Plan59 and Plan52 do not block a separately approved report-local investigation, but no source mutation follows from these findings.

The inherited adapter resolves the terminal endpoint with crossed entrants excluded; the fixed geometry diagnostic did not establish a controller-reachable strict-closing pre-finish contact, so this is an acknowledged approximation and open coverage limit—not a demonstrated natural-race defect or a general safety guarantee. The bounded source and event-protocol implications for a future kernel are in `remaining-gaps.md`.
