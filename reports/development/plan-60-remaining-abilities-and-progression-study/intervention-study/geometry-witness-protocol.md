Use `L=COURSE_LENGTH`, `dt=PHYSICS.dt` (expected 0.05), and the table's progress offsets from L. Each pair lists P then B as `(start progress offset, start lateral) → (end progress offset, end lateral)`. These are kinematic checks, not evidence that the actual steering/acceleration controller generates that trajectory. The independent source `frameAt`, `pos`, `velocity`, `sweptPairCandidate` and crossing formula provide the observations. Report actual raw `<0` closing and a conservative `<−1e-9` distinction to reveal floating-point edge cases. No injury, ability, RNG or actual race is simulated.
