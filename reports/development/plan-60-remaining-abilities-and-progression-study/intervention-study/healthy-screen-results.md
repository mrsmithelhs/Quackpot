# Healthy-entry exploratory screen

**Status:** all 256 preregistered healthy-entry cases completed, with four arms across 16 fixed seeds, two field configurations and two P stances. The corrected PowerShell wrapper resumed at index16 without replaying the first16 cases; `screen-wrapper-failure-01.md` and `screen-wrapper-repair-compatibility.md` preserve the failure and original bindings. The separately authorized injured-entry continuation is reported in `injured-screen-results.md`. These results are exploratory on the pinned Plan55-derived model; they do not establish final balance, actual student experience or production behavior.

Each table cell is 16 races. Guard uses a diagnostic predicted-threat trigger, .75 activation chance, .5-second window, 2-second cooldown and half of the diagnostic injury probability; these are **not selected production values**. Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter use their own predeclared experimental controller modifications, not final mechanics. No ability is equipped in the `none` arm.

Three cells have fewer than eight Guard-protected P rolls; the favorite results are particularly sparse. In equal-sprint and favorite-sprint, one same-contact, same-entrant-key, same-draw comparison in each cell had injury in the control and no injury with Guard. The other two cells had no such discordance. This is a description of matched susceptible rolls, not a population injury-prevention rate; later paths or contacts can diverge, and a duck already injured no longer draws a new injury. No exact false-prediction or contact-within-window fraction rate is available from the retained natural-run audit.

These medians describe **completed** recorded recovery episodes only; episodes still active at finish or otherwise not recentered are not in the median, and denominators differ. The table includes P recovery episodes whether or not an ability activated. Therefore it is not an unbiased estimate of how much an activation shortens recovery. Same-seed trajectories may differ and later contacts need not match.

For the fixed seed `p60i-000`, equal/steady, both Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter activated once and each had one recorded P contact; their retained P trajectory witnesses have maximum sampled lateral separation 0.2 units at tick268. This is one predeclared witness, not evidence of perceptible animation, general effectiveness or a typical effect size. The earlier isolated controlled fixture also showed numeric path divergence but did not include continuous traffic or full-race presentation.

Same-seed P-place changes versus none were modest in these inputs. In equal/steady, Quick Recovery had 2 better, 13 same and 1 worse; Straight Shooter 3 better, 11 same and 2 worse. In equal/sprint, Quick Recovery and Straight Shooter each had 2 better and 14 same. All 16 favorite cases per stance had the same P place as control in these arms. Guard place was unchanged except one better equal/sprint case. These are observed outcomes, not proof that a particular activation caused each result, nor that the abilities have equal power. The full analysis includes distributions and matched-roll counts.

Known evidence limits: the copied adapter inherits a finish-first terminal interval; the geometry diagnostic did not establish a physically consistent strict-closing pre-finish contact and did not prove general safety. Real within-window contact fraction, full R2/advice overlap, observed injury at a specific capped-corner/slowdown branch, continuous repeated-contact interruption, actual presentation/accessibility and long-run balance remain unverified. The fixed potential seeds/configurations are too few for a reliable player-population estimate. Wilson intervals in the analysis have independent-seed assumptions and do not account for the shared paired design across arms; they are descriptive, not a determination of balance.
