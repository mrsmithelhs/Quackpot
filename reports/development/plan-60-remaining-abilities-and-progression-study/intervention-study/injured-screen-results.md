P started injured and had **no new P injury roll in 8/8 cases in every cell**. New-injury probability is inapplicable here, not an observed zero-risk rate or a measure of Guard's benefit. Guard cannot heal. A read-only comparison found the finish, contact and injury-audit objects identical for all 16 seed/stance none-versus-Guard pairs; the two retained seed000 P-trajectory witness pairs were also identical. This is consistent with the study's no-heal behavior for these cases, not a broader effectiveness claim about Guard on healthy favorites.

The median recovery comparisons include only completed episodes, with different and partially unfinished observation sets, including episodes where the ability did not activate. They cannot be interpreted as matched causal time saved. In the eight steady seed pairs Quick Recovery's P place was better/same/worse in 6/1/1 and Straight Shooter's in 5/3/0; in sprint the respective counts were 2/5/1 and 1/6/1. Candidate movement can change later contacts, and eight seeds leave substantial uncertainty; these are not player-population win rates or evidence of balance.
