## Four arms and temporary semantics

One ability belongs to P only; rivals remain unchanged. Retain actual contact impulse, other participants' independent injury and genuine crossing. Each ability uses a study-specific independent deterministic activation key `p60-intervention/v1/{raceId}/{entrant}/{ability}/{opportunityOrdinal}`; sample the same opportunity identity only where its eligible episode is actually shared. Fixed sample constants below are deliberately diagnostic: activation probability .75, active 10 ticks = .5 s, cooldown 40 ticks = 2 s after end, and one opportunity per latched episode; rearm after two consecutive absent 5-tick checks for Guard, or a new eligible contact after cooldown for the two contact abilities. At own finish end/cancel state; preserve rollback checkpoints for state and audit. Identical activation probability does not imply identical exposure or strength.

1. **None:** no new intervention; selected injury correction above still applies.
2. **Guard:** sample every 5 ticks using the pinned `findBounceThreat` geometrical predicate: active grounded rival, course distance <=8, current separation >1 and <=6, closing, closest approach in (0,.5] s and predicted separation <1.2. This is a diagnostic trigger, not an avoided-contact claim. A successful earlier predictive opportunity opens the 10-tick brace window; the duck stays grounded, no trajectory or collision geometry changes. P's independent actual-contact injury threshold is multiplied by .5 only while active. Other participants' risks are unchanged. Contact at exact start is unprotected unless activation was committed at an earlier tick; contact at end is unprotected. No retroactive injury erasure. Unlike Bounce, Guard grants partial injury-risk reduction without airborne behavior or full protection. Preserve candidate no-activation/false-prediction and cooldown cases.
3. **Quick Recovery:** on an actual P contact, first retain resolved impulse and injury draw; at the next controller step, a successful activation temporarily sets the contact steering episode to its existing `recover` phase, rather than continuing its acquire/hold deflection. While active, use the normal recenter controller and inherited R2 assistance/caps; never teleport, remove the impulse or undo injury. Effect ends at recovery completion, 10 ticks, interruption by a new contact, or finish; then cooldown. If time expires first, retain the current physical heading and proceed with the normal recovery controller, never restore an artificial past heading. This candidate changes the post-contact controller path and may change subsequent real contacts.
4. **Straight Shooter:** on an actual P contact retain impulse and injury first; successful activation makes that contact episode's *subsequent* target offset half of the sampled deflection magnitude for at most 10 ticks, without altering restitution velocity, injury, wall/corridor limits, or other entrant. Adapt only the episode's acquisition/hold minimum to `min(10°, target offset − 1°)` so a 7.5–10° diagnostic target is reachable; use inherited controller/caps and handle no attainment/timeout honestly. At expiry/interruption/finish remove the modifier and let current actual steering/recovery continue without snapping. This is a candidate distinction, not a promise of an advantage or a specification of the final game.

For contacts, reuse Plan55 `plan55-susceptibility/v1/{raceId}/{contactId}/{entrantId}/injury` keyed independent draw in **every** arm with diagnostic medium band .05 steady/.10 sprint, changing only Guard's eligible threshold. Same seed/raceId/entrant plus the same actual contact ID yields the same draw; different contact IDs, times or participant paths are *not paired*. Preserve each raw draw and eligibility. A controlled harness supplies an identical actual contact identity and draw across arms; a natural run reports contact/participant matches separately from unmatched outcomes. Opportunity ordinal keys may match only if the same actual opportunity occurs; report opportunity identity and matching rather than assuming it from seed.
