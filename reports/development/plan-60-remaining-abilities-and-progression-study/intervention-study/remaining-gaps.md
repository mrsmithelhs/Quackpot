# Remaining gaps after limited Stage 0

The eight recorded diagnostic jobs passed, but they do not prove full Stage 0 correctness or authorize the natural screen. This is a source-only disposition and proposal; no additional fixture or race has been executed.

## A. Witnesses potentially reachable without changing the terminal policy

- **Real swept timing and Guard expiry:** the passing boundary tests supply fractions independently of the common geometry. Construct a fixed pair of actual before/after world positions whose swept calculation yields a known contact candidate just before Guard ends, and a second independently authored geometry yielding one at/after end. Exercise the actual multi-contact resolver path, activation/cooldown state and independent draws; verify the calculated fractions rather than injecting them. Also test an actual prediction that starts the window, not only a manually initialized state.
- **Injury, caps and assistance:** the entry-injury checks prove selected rating-to-speed arithmetic in exercised full runs and the historical onset check retained a contact impulse; they do not guarantee a particular observed corner cap or leader-slowdown reference. Construct named fixed controller states that reach those branches and inspect before/after target and velocity, including a real same-race onset, separately from the synthetic arithmetic. Validate fixed R2 assistance and predictive advice interaction in actual resolver steps.
- **Continuous interruption and rollback:** the passing two-contact case resets positions, so it does not show a naturally continuous multi-contact trajectory. The fixed injected-guard test achieved at least one rollback per Q/S arm but did not print exact covered seed counts. Existing receipts cannot reconstruct those counts; do not rerun solely to count them. A fixed continuous trajectory or controlled sequence should exercise interruption, cooldown and rollback together, with all state/audit/key restoration and retained versus discarded injuries.

## B. Inherited same-interval terminal policy — root decision needed

Source inspection: `simulate` first advances all active entrants, computes their crossing fractions, records every entrant that crossed in that tick, and invokes `runTransactionalEndpointResolution` with all crossed IDs excluded. Thus a crossed duck is not offered to the tick-end contact resolver even if a swept pair candidate lies earlier within that interval. The audit `auditTerminalTickOrdering` records candidate timing at the first crossing, but it is diagnostic and does not resolve that contact. The current injected-fraction helper is not a test of this ordering.

**Proposed deterministic geometry witness and subsequent result:** in a locally straight finish frame, let `L` be course length. At interval start, P has `(progress=L−.45, lateral=0)` and B `(progress=L−.55, lateral=1.0)`; at interval end, P is `(L+.05,.6)` and B `(L+.05,.4)`. Under linear interpolation P crosses at fraction .9 and B at 11/12. Relative B−P starts `(-.10,1.0)` and changes by `(.10,-1.20)`, so the existing closest-approach calculation gives fraction `1.21/1.45 ≈ .83448`, before either crossing, and distance below the two-duck diameter. **The subsequent seven-case diagnostic used velocities consistent with those straight-line endpoints and found closing +1.15×10⁻¹³, not negative: this is not an accepted pre-finish contact witness.** Controller reachability remains unestablished. The originally proposed idea of independently supplying closing velocities would not establish that those velocities actually produced the endpoints. See `geometry-witness-protocol.md` and the retained `geometry-witness.json`; no positive natural-race defect has been demonstrated.

Two possible future corrections, neither authorized or selected:

1. **Fraction-ordered event stepping:** find and resolve eligible contact/finish events in time order within the interval, advance to each boundary, re-evaluate geometry/velocity and crossings after an impulse, with an explicit tie rule, rollback and iteration/guard limits. This best represents the causal sequence but is a substantial physics-contract change, and an impulse can change whether or when a duck crosses.
2. **Narrow terminal-interval guard:** if a pre-crossing contact candidate involving a crossed duck exists, preserve a bounded unresolved/guard result instead of recording an unsupported crossing. This avoids silently asserting a finish but does not supply a complete physical resolution or finish for that case. Its impact on frequency and user-facing nonfinish behavior must be measured and separately reviewed.

Root should decide whether selected continuous-time/real-contact semantics require a correction before natural comparisons, or whether a clearly labeled exploratory screen may proceed with this inherited limitation and appropriate stop/censor policy. A merely post-hoc injury or contact inserted after a recorded crossing is not a valid repair. Do not change production, Plan59's integration contract, or the copied solver on the strength of this note.
