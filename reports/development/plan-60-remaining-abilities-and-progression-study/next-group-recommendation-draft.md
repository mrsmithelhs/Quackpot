# Next group recommendation — working draft for review

The owner selected **Guard and Quick Recovery** as the next investigation candidates, with **Straight Shooter** as a focused comparator before deciding whether to include it. Report-local research need not wait for the entire Plan52 transition or Plan59 port: a separately reviewed, bounded experiment could use a pinned suitable multi-contact research base, or a stable reusable race kernel could become available before the rest of the port. Plan55's adapter is a possible research base, not an automatically authorized or validated choice. Production integration still requires the appropriate stable source ownership, save/day-boundary and versioned handoffs. This is a prioritization by distinct owner-valued purposes and manageable event relationships, not a finding that they are balanced, effective, or ready for production. A healthy favorite's Guard risk and stance exposure still need evidence from a multi-contact race model. The legacy baseline capped all racers at one duck contact; it is not an adequate effectiveness test.

Guard has a clear player purpose—temporary bracing before likely traffic to reduce the injury risk from actual contact while active—and a cost: the window can miss, fail to activate or cool down during a later collision. It differs from Bounce's full active-window injury protection, air/landing behavior and selected visual. Quick Recovery addresses the current race rather than multi-day injury: after real contact it attempts a faster return toward course without undoing injury, contact impulse or injury rating. Straight Shooter could preserve heading immediately but delay useful avoidance; the authored labels did not establish that its actual trajectory differs usefully from Quick Recovery. The owner chose to explore it separately; merger or deferral would need owner review.

**Next after that:** Draft and Wake Break are attractive separate follower/leader purposes with strong named-participant identity, but need a real definition of target persistence, interruption, speed/acceleration limits and individual-finish termination. Existing proxy time is not a continuous relationship or proof of impact. Dive and Flap Ahead are distinct owner-valued trade-offs, but both need collision-layer, occupied-return, crossing and air/underwater interaction design before a safe integration proposal; neither is rejected. Wide Flap needs lateral steering, wall and downstream-contact attribution; one named target is a valid simpler rival to multiple targets, whose policy remains open. Steady Nerves has observed baseline self-weave opportunities, but whether reducing real wobble matters or is legible remains unknown. Burst stays deferred.

## Candidate versionable handoff, not an implementation grant

For a later packet, Guard's candidate input would identify entrant, learned ability/version, selected stance, current eligibility and seeded opportunity identity. Distinct event facts would be prediction/activation, active start/end/cancel, actual contact identity/participants, each independent injury draw/outcome, cooldown and finish. State would track only the temporary window/cooldown and persisted earned ability—not multi-race protection. Do not consume presentation randomness for mechanics. Decide predictor geometry, sampling, probability, same-tick order and partial-risk calculation explicitly; a failed prediction is not an avoided collision.

Quick Recovery would consume an actual contact/participant and valid recovery episode, produce a separately identified seeded activation and finite effect/cooldown, and modify a specified recovery controller without erasing impulse, contact, injury or rating effect. Its exact duration, magnitude, rearm and competing steering priorities remain open. A meaningful acceptance example compares the same controlled contact with and without its effect while retaining the same injury facts, then verifies termination at that entrant's actual finish. Authored .7-second recovery milestones are assumptions, not such evidence.

The selected Plan58 references are Guard B and Quick Recovery A; they convey study direction only. Factual named events and static/reduced-motion equivalents need separate implementation and review. Draft Plan59 scopes binding only Bounce and independent crowd and extracting other visuals; its production work depends on its gates. This study does not change its contract. Whether later mechanics fit Plan59's eventual event/state interface is **not yet knowable**: review its stable handoff and actual versioned seam, then request a separate amendment or packet if needed. Plan52 owns shared scheduler/save work. Plan59 compatibility, event/replay versioning, partial-result injuries, full finite activation lifecycle, actual crossing/landing and visual cancellation remain gates.

## Progression and decisions left open

The owner wants an earned later point to offer improving a learned ability or acquiring another and wants both higher-rarity potential axes compared. More simultaneous abilities and a higher upgrade ceiling are alternatives, not automatically combined. A common duck must retain a meaningful free path; the examples in `progression-traces-draft.md` show earned overflow, banking and one-duck losses but do not validate actual session pacing or prove equal potential. Numeric later thresholds, exact per-level improvements, rarity grants/ceilings, ability capacity, loadout and permanent-choice presentation remain owner decisions. Gem acceleration/events are separate future work.

Before claiming an integration-ready group: conduct a separately cleared intervention comparison that actually changes recovery/heading and multi-contact risk with matched controls, measure exposure and distributions without filtering, and review actual race presentation. Research can proceed on a suitable pinned report-local base before a complete Plan59 port; the production proposal must separately inspect the stable relevant race seam and shared-state handoffs. This future evidence is a gate for effectiveness and production readiness, not a prerequisite automatically imposed on closure of this report-only study. If comparisons do not show a distinct, legible purpose for one candidate, bring a merge/defer recommendation to the owner; do not tune numbers until all candidates appear successful.
