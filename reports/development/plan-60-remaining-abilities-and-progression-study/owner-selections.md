# Owner context and study choices

In this implementer chat on 2026-11-04, the owner selected these directions for exploration:

- Guard and Quick Recovery should have distinct purposes: reducing the risk of injury from a real contact versus recovering course sooner without healing.
- Dive and Flap Ahead should both be explored with distinct trade-offs; Draft and Wake Break should also be explored separately.
- Straight Shooter should be explored separately from Quick Recovery, and both Wide Flap and Steady Nerves should be explored separately.
- A later point should offer improving an existing ability or learning another. Rarity investigation should favor higher eventual potential; compare extra simultaneous abilities and a higher upgrade ceiling as separate alternatives.
- Every ability activation is temporary within a race, possibly with different durations and cooldowns; levels might lengthen durations. Learned ownership may persist, while Guard's active protection does not carry into future races. The selected injury lifecycle remains separate.
- Guard should visibly brace before likely contact, not wait for a collision to occur. Its temporary window can expire unused; prediction is not proof of collision. Exact trigger, same-time ordering, odds, duration, cooldown and reduction were not selected.

These are purposes and study directions, not final mechanical or numerical approvals. Separate investigation does not compel all concepts to survive, and the owner's earlier selections of Bounce, crowd acceleration, first point, injury and schedule remain in force. The owner accepted prioritizing **Guard plus Quick Recovery** as the next investigation group, keeping Straight Shooter as a comparator. This is a study priority, not approval of production, final balance or completion.
