## Tiny curiosity examples

1. **Print shop:** “We found a poster that says GRAND OPE ING.” Optional follow-up: “The missing N was stuck to the cutter.” The first line works alone; follow-up is a small reveal, never a task.
2. **Laundry:** “The lonely glove has company.” Optional follow-up: “Its owner came back for both.” A single line still suggests a larger life.
3. **Building notice:** “The lobby plant has acquired a paper crown.” Optional follow-up: “The crown is back with its owner. The plant looks less official.” No person or relationship is established as the player's.
4. **Work:** Marisol: “The label maker printed eleven identical labels.” Optional follow-up: “Nobody knows why the twelfth says BREAD.” The message has no shift offer and does not change gameplay.
5. **Family, identity unselected:** “Your aunt's joke goes here” is not suitable player-facing copy: first choose who this person is. A possible tone, without assigning identity: “The kitchen timer has been promoted to family referee.” A later exchange could reveal it times board-game turns, if the family relationship is explicitly established.
6. **Quackpot:** “The pond is dressing up for the next event.” Alternate return framing: “Your ducks are waiting for their next race.” These are sender claims/promotions, not evidence ducks have feelings or that an event/reward exists. Retain only if a real implemented event or return condition supports the display.

The best single message creates a small question without demanding a response. Some lead to a later companion message; others remain a pleasant loose end. A follow-up must never appear before its first message, promise an unseen decision or reveal an unearned event. Avoid a parade of puzzles, constant joke cadence or a sender who exists solely to notify.

## Future mechanism questions — proposals only

- Separate eligibility (established relationships, already delivered first message, game state, no future spoilers) from a seeded selection among eligible authored messages. Avoid equating random choice with independent spam: compare controls such as a quiet run, limited eligible pool and no-messages stretch. No numerical cadence or probability selected.
- Consider a limited per-run selection or per-contact quiet space and a non-guaranteed follow-up chosen from preauthored pairs. Exact draw timing, probability, maximum volume, failure/expiry and late-run behavior are open. A later production packet must examine optional follow-ups that never fire, current-run time, deterministic replay, cancellation and duplicate delivery. A narration sequence is not a selected reward.
- Distinguish an unread thread, a factual obligation and promotional pressure. A routine social message should not look like a payment deadline. An ignored informational message has no invented relationship penalty. Existing real Ren/social commitments keep their own selected rules.
- Quackpot can be notably more persistent than human contacts, as a fictional persuasion channel. The owner requests launch, event and unopened-today return concepts. Proposed trigger and volume are not yet selected; only message about an actual available/known event and accurately reflect whatever run state a later system defines. Avoid pretending an advertising sender knows off-screen feelings, falsely labeling an offer an objective deadline, making a notification an actual operating-system push, or promising a reward not implemented.
- Future integration must explicitly decide whether and how actual delivery, cue display, message display, decisions and promotional claims are recorded in the append-only local evidence/Receipt. No receipt should infer a tactic was displayed from a message merely eligible to appear. Do not alter seeded game outcomes or consume life slots merely to read. Uninstall remains reachable.
