# Welcome panel text options

The owner likes the M5 music and is considering text that sits more comfortably with its playful ragtime. These are copy suggestions, not selected replacements; the current line remains in the prototype.

- **Current:** “A little room, a quiet moment, and a new day.”
- **Warm and lightly playful:** “A little room, a rubber duck, and a day full of possibilities.”
- **Cheerful:** “A fresh day, a familiar duck, and a little luck.”
- **Simple and personal:** “Your duck is already here. The day is yours.”
- **A touch more buoyant:** “A new day is waiting, and your duck has a front-row seat.”

The first alternative is the closest to the existing tone while leaving room for the music's energy. No change to the music is proposed.
