## Visible comparison and source seams

- **Phone form:** Gallery is a bounded tall, full-height phone with substantial dark rim, continuous light interior, flush header and scrollable content. Current `.messages-panel` has padded floating cards, narrower variable-height content and lacks enough surrounding phone detail. Restore a recognizably physical phone with careful viewport-safe dimensions, internal scrolling and a separate collapsed Decide sibling; inspect actual wide, narrow and enlarged-text neighbors and ensure global Uninstall remains reachable.
- **Inbox:** Gallery rows are compact, visually connected, with sender and right-aligned relative timestamp, one truncated preview line, and separate emphasis for unread or true due information. Current spaced rounded cards have two-line previews and mix timestamp, unread and status on one line. Owner confirmed short list preview and full contents only inside a conversation. Do not let a preview expose source text before the existing display evidence contract allows it.
- **Conversation:** Gallery has arrow Back, title, X Close, full-height light background and distinct sender bubbles with breathing room. Current literal Back/Close buttons, different box and colored bubbles diverge. Restore recognizable arrow/X with accessible names, keyboard operation, focus and Escape/backdrop behavior; preserve separate Decide and evidence timing. Ren header currently adds a portrait absent from screenshot; compare holistically rather than treating this isolated difference as mandatory removal.
- **Header:** Owner selected overlapping-bubble icon with compact separate unread badge, no visible 'Messages' word. Production `src/ui/hud.js` adds visible text and parenthesized count. Preserve meaningful accessible label and disabled/unavailable feedback, delivery-only bounded cue and reduced-motion equivalence; verify real neighbors and global exit.
- **Avatar:** Current `messages-view.mjs` renders accepted `renderRen` for Ren but initials for every other sender. Ren can be below the initially visible viewport in the provided start. Gallery used Ren artwork and initial placeholders for others; the owner now requests actual avatar images, which is a new explicit direction, not evidence the gallery already included such artwork. Coordinate exact sender art approach and owned paths. Runtime remains local and existing accepted Ren art must not be redrawn.
- **Relative dates:** Runtime produces `Day N` from event clocks and `Contact`; the gallery depicts Today, Yesterday and Earlier. Owner requests Today/Yesterday/N days ago. Derive labels from the simulated current day and the delivery's recorded simulated day, with no fabricated real-world date or clock time and an explicit safe treatment for fixed history/contact and future/unknown values. Update displayed relative labels as simulated day changes without manufacturing deliveries, unread status, RNG or event records.
- **Expired:** Current `messages-runtime.mjs` emits `Expired` for the historical launch invitation, which is game-state language a phone sender would not attach. Omit or replace the visible label with a natural representation while preserving its real expiration, non-actionability, evidence/read eligibility and replay/Receipt behavior. Review `Answered`, `Resolved`, `No longer active` and generic `Decision available` for the same sender/interface boundary; distinguish real payment due from unread.
- **End run:** Global `End run & view Receipt` was introduced by commit `60f27f5e` (2026-11-04), not by Plan 63. `src/main.js` `showEndRun` exposes it from day 4 in qualifying life contexts, and `src/ui/hud.js` renders it globally, so it remains visible over Messages. Its placement and ending semantics belong to the coordinated life/Plan52 boundary. QP Orch must disposition; suppressing or relocating it cannot silently remove a required exit or change terminal behavior.
