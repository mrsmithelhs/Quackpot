## Mismatches and limits

* Engine `leaveUninstall` and `stayUninstall` do not themselves check that the retention modal is active; browser exposes these callbacks only inside that modal (`game-engine.mjs:1876,1892`; `main.js:1291`). Likewise `finishRunComplete` has no schedule eligibility guard at its entry. A pilot dispatcher must enforce browser availability and never advertise these merely because the method is callable. This is a contract gap, not a demonstrated player-facing defect.
* Generic five-slot `play` is explicitly rejected (`gameplay_operation_required`); using scheduler transactions to fake a draw or race bypasses outcomes. `recordFiveSlotDueOptionsShown` / story and warning records are intended for real display, while their presence may gate transitions. Headless simulation must label those assumptions and cannot assert real observation. A checkpoint containing such prehistory may fail the Plan 16 truth standard; choose a valid boundary or report blocked, not fabricate.
* Browser finance default requires a specific blocked intent, displayed actionable options, a current snapshot and warning/confirmation; engine's lower-level finance interface alone has a broader callable surface. Cancellation changes no outcome, though actual warning evidence may remain.
* `finishRunComplete` describes a Day 3 normal end in a comment; five-slot supported calendar extends beyond that. No natural solvent terminal is asserted here. The pilot must report still playable at its horizon if no browser-accessible normal end is found, and escalate any semantic disagreement.
* Default schema 5/v1 and five-slot policy are the target. `?race-v2=1` schema 6, worker and `messages-v2` are separate experimental paths (`main.js:83,531-548`), excluded from this pilot. Earlier non-five-slot `applyLifeIntent`, escalation and Plan 16/35 fixtures are historical; they do not prove today's route. Ren, settings, Dex, social, trial, concurrent dues and interruptions are inventoried at feature level but not exhaustively enumerated or validated in a browser. No reachability or accessibility result is claimed.
