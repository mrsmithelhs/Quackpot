## Technical corrections for the experiment brief

- Resolve requested/effective settings and precedence. Overcast suppressing sunlight conflicts with the claim of wholly independent settings. Start with a small named comparison set, not the full daylight/weather/lamp cross product. Define sun versus rain/storm and lamp treatment before adding combinations.
- Window rectangles and projection helpers are useful inputs, not proof of physically correct floor patches or object-height shadows. Use a consistent sun direction/elevation, ground contact and paint order; inspect on rug/floor and under cropped windows. Label approximations honestly.
- Experiment string insertion is acceptable only in report tooling with exact verified anchors, rejection of missing/ambiguous matches, valid nested SVG and scoped ID references. It is not the final effects API. No H1–H5 mutation in the current kit without a later gate.
- Keep separate tests for unchanged defaults and intentional changed variants. Numeric pixel difference does not by itself establish a convincing visual result. A global tint can change lamp pixels while lamp markup remains unchanged; verify structural preservation and final compositing separately.
- Idle plus host-triggered flashes can defeat a claimed aggregate interval; class toggling alone does not establish restart or cancellation. Do not certify a flash effect from a two-second cap or CSS-only implementation. Host embedding/lifecycle is a later integration question; do not make unsupported universal claims about SVG animation in image contexts.
