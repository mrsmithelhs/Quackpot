# In-game check-in rewards and life-choice tension

Date: 2026-10-27. Owner follow-up; investigation direction, not an adopted reward schedule or production work order.

## Existing contract and implementation

Canonical design's manipulation catalog already permits a streak reward on **in-game days**, with Receipt wording explicitly distinguishing those days from real visits. Opening includes the free ten-pull. Current production engine has daily wage processing but no check-in calendar/escalating check-in rewards. Existing founding values do not validate a new free-currency/pull schedule.

The owner proposes counting the starting free ten-pull as day one, showing later single-pull/Down/Gem rewards and an attractive later five- or ten-pull reward. Time that larger reward near difficult life decisions and physical-companion conversations. Outdoor time, friends and family are suggested alternatives to spending more time in the fictional gacha. This is a proposed connection between the economy and story, not permission to implement deferred Mom voicemail/loan/streamer branches.

## Recommended bounded investigation

Use an explicit in-game-day calendar. No real-date checks, actual daily return requirement, background time accrual or re-engagement after Receipt. Closing/reloading the browser must neither earn a reward nor count as missing a day. Day-one invitation and calendar refer to the **same** ten-pull grant, never a second award.

Start with a finite welcome track: day-one ten-pull, small varied rewards, and one clearly visible later milestone. Compare five versus ten milestone pulls against the established economy before choosing quantities. Test roster completion, pity reachability, starvation, Down exchange and run pacing; a large free milestone may remove purchase pressure or encourage waiting instead of interacting. Do not silently adopt a repeating calendar, streak reset, escalating penalty, lost collected inventory, guaranteed rare drop or selected milestone day.

Give the life choice an intelligible opportunity cost. Merely switching screens to collect a free gift while also taking the outdoor/family option creates no difficult choice. Propose how a finite in-game time/attention opportunity is consumed and how the player can decline it. Do not require check-in to pay essential bills, reach an ending or Uninstall. Prefer a small event-driven set of conversations near the approaching milestone rather than mandatory daily dialogue. Calendar expiry/claim/streak-reset semantics, story triggers and economy amounts return to owner as a concrete proposal before source mutation.

## Honest evidence

Append-only evidence should distinguish calendar/next-reward cue actually displayed, fictional day and eligibility, player claim/decline/alternative choice, actual granted reward and one-time grant ID. Scheduled but unseen milestone advertising is not a displayed cue; a closed browser is not an observed rejection. Persist claim state and RNG continuation so replay/reload cannot duplicate gifts or silently reroll rewarded pulls. Preserve normal odds/pity and record the applicable pre-draw odds for any actual rewarded pull.

Receipt example, using only witnessed facts:

- We displayed: “The calendar showed a five-pull reward two in-game days away.”
- You did: “You chose to open Quackpot instead of the offered afternoon outside.”
- Did this affect you?: “Did seeing the upcoming reward influence that choice?”

The game cannot assert that the reward caused a choice, a real-world login streak, addiction or a measured emotional effect. Free accessible/static paths and immediate exit remain available. This work belongs to a separate calendar/economy/story investigation, not Plan23's race presentation repair. No packet status or hard constraint changes are made by this note.
