## Concrete economic consequences checked

Root verified current constants (base 80/18/2; duplicate awards 1/5/20) and independently calculated the following after a bounded read-only Luna-high audit:

- From an empty six-identity roster, ten base-rate pulls guarantee at least four duplicate **cards**, not six Down. Expected duplicate-card count is about **5.731** and expected Down about **8.371**, assuming independent draws and uniform identity choice within each tier. These are expectations, not a guaranteed player experience. The existing fixture of four Common duplicates yields four Down: enough for the 3-Down item but not the 6-Down ticket.
- With a fully owned roster at base odds, expected Down per draw is `0.80×1 + 0.18×5 + 0.02×20 = 2.1`. At six Down per ticket, that baseline alone is lossy; it is not proof for pity-adjusted draws or the whole loop. A Legendary duplicate yields **20 Down**, immediately enough for three tickets plus two Down. Redrawn duplicates, pity and banked currency can extend a run even when the base-rate mean is below the price. The old simulation's 0.02 gem-equivalent per Down is not an adopted exchange rate for this new shop.
- The 1750 bundle alone leaves 150 after one 1600 ten-pull. With 50 prior race gems, the same purchase leaves **200**, enough for another single pull and then 40. The starter example remains 50+240−160=130, thirty short of another pull. Validate intended purchase points using whole wallets.

These findings support testing the owner's more usable shop, not rejecting or silently changing the selected prices. No new simulation sweep or production outcome was executed this turn. Plan 17 specifies the bounded next evidence.
