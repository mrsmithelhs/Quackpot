## Verification and synthesis standards

Open the supporting source before adopting a claim. Prefer empirical originals, author manuscripts/repositories, official composer/developer accounts and official technical documentation. A search snippet or secondary citation is not full-text evidence. For each load-bearing claim record the report that supplied it, primary URL/DOI, exact reading depth, manipulation, N/population where applicable, outcome measured, relevant result and limitations. Distinguish verified by Argon from worker-reported reading. Do not imply the integrator personally read every worker source.

Retain E-full / E-abs / P / T / H labels, with clear definitions. Full text is a reading-depth label, not an automatic quality ranking. Do not generalize unfamiliar-melody memory, cover recognition or scary-rating experiments to repeated classroom game hooks without explaining the inference. Do not treat stronger loudness as proof of stronger compositional tension. Match loudness/tempo where a proposed comparison needs to isolate arrangement.

The prior headline recommendation to freeze pitch, rhythm, key, tempo, lead timbre, length and loudness is a conservative proposed first experiment, not a settled owner rule. You may recommend relaxing an anchor if the evidence and musical rationale support it. Likewise, 'timbre costs identity and buys little tension' may overgeneralize separate tasks; examine it critically. The goal is recognizable music with expressive range, not maximizing invariance at any cost.

No causal claim about actual players' addiction, dopamine, motivation or physiological effects follows from this research. Music can express the fictional character's relationship to the game. Receipt honesty remains binding.
