## Contracts and productive challenges

- Uninstall remains visible and actionable, with immediate Leave to the Receipt.
  No obstruction, punishment, required waiting or deceptive real controls.
- All money is fictional. No real payments, runtime network, accounts, telemetry,
  remote assets or automatic student-data transmission. Static/local delivery.
- Prices, balances, relevant odds, terms, operational warnings and accessible
  equivalents remain available. Propose alternatives to control sabotage.
- Keep the physical rubber-duck companion inanimate. Gacha ducks may animate;
  camera, external lighting and composition may give the prop character.
- Receipt honesty: **we displayed / you did / did this affect you?** Depicted
  character activity, narration, persuasion and actual human-player choices are
  separate. Do not rewrite evidence or invent motives/psychological effects.
- You may propose new mechanics and even argue for reopening a settled design
  choice, but label that clearly with consequences and an owner gate. Never
  silently treat it as selected. This review does not amend existing mechanics.
- Avoid equating engagement with spending, disorientation with psychiatric illness,
  or all enjoyment with harm. Allow delight, resistance, recovery, outside
  relationships and mixed outcomes. Do not make the companion an infallible lecturer.
- Propose reduced-motion/static and sensory-controls alternatives. Music-bleed
  proposals must honor mute/volume controls and distinguish soundtrack perception
  from actual TV playback, especially after a TV sale.
- Later beats should arise from declared fictional story state, not a diagnosis of
  the student inferred from clicks. Seeded cosmetic variation must not perturb
  race/draw/economy randomness. Saving/reloading cannot silently reroll outcomes.
