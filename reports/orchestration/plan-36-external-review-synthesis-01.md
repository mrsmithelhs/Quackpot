## Pushback on the reviewers

- All strongly oppose failed/erratic return controls. Their trust, accessibility and 'looks like a bug' concerns deserve a comparison, but their categorical rejection is an artistic recommendation, not a new hard constraint. The owner explicitly requested these candidates. Keep actual **Uninstall/Leave** immediate and reliable; distinguish it from a depicted attempt to put the phone away. Any selected return disturbance needs an interruptible cap, static equivalent, clear escape and lifecycle evidence. Do not silently replace the owner's seed with a safe-looking choice menu.
- An unfamiliar hallway/another apartment does not automatically establish a psychiatric interpretation. Narrative cause, tone and audience reading matter. A grounded room shift is cheaper to try first; unfamiliar spaces can remain a higher-cost candidate rather than being declared prohibited.
- Flash's broadly moralized 'transparent systems, only framing deceptive' rule overstates the charter. We can depict uncertainty and rationalization; factual payment terms, controls/exit and Receipt evidence still have to be honest.
- Several proposals restyle bills with rarity/urgency. Prefer receipts, shopping lists and authored narration first. Financial terms, due status and commitment controls must remain legible and unambiguous; a creative framing proposal does not authorize obscured obligations.
