# Welcome music provenance

`welcome-m5.wav` is a 30.000-second, 48 kHz stereo PCM16 excerpt of the Plan 53 M5 reconstruction. It is used locally in Quackpot's noncommercial classroom game; the game makes no runtime audio request to an external service. The player enables sound by gesture. The playback cycle fades in for three seconds, holds for 24 and fades out for three; its peak envelope is 0.55. The naming scene uses a separate 0.25 multiplier and one-second transitions.

The owner's source-use attestation, relayed by QP Orch on 2026-11-05 following the owner's explicit clearance of M5, is that they are a paid Suno subscriber and have rights to use their generated works for this project. This record does not assert a subscription date, download date or specific third-party-input facts.
